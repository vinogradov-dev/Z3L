# U117 — the contour panel wears a wavy board, the niche-fit build wears a cut one

## What was measured

- `crates/ui/src/toolbars/spec.rs` had three Shape methods, not two: `FILL_POST_SHAPE`,
  `FILL_SHELF_SHAPE` and `FILL_WALL_SHAPE` (the wall pair landed with N28, `e1e281c`, after this
  entry was filed). All three drew `icons::contour::POLYGON`, the sketch tool's hexagon, so the
  glyph said neither "fit" nor "shape".
- `assets/icons/scene/panel.svg` was the same plate as the fill cells' board: an isometric slab on a
  6.5/5.5 pitch (40°, not the canonical 30° of `STYLE_GUIDE.md` §9.1), so `furniture.panels.contour`
  drew the fill cells' own artwork.
- Rendered at 16, 22, 26 and 64 px with `rsvg-convert` on the dark master ink, the whole scene theme
  reads as one family: `post` and `wall` are upright boards, `shelf` and `panel` lying plates,
  `product` an outlined cube.

## Decisions

- **`panel.svg` is a lying plate whose far long edge is a three-lobe scallop** (plan 12 × 7.5, plate
  2.2, lobes 1.6 deep on the canonical 30° basis). The wave sits on the far edge because the near
  edge carries the thickness band: a wave there produces a wavy ribbon and a second wavy line 2.2
  units under it, which at 26 px closes into a "boat", and the band under an inward-flank lobe is a
  face turned away from the viewer. On the far edge the wave is a silhouette, the near bands stay
  clean, and the plate still reads as a plate. Measured 2.62..21.38 × 4.88..19.12, 30.6 % coverage.
- **`panel-shape.svg` is an upright board on `post`'s basis** (9.5 × 2.6 × 11) **with the top-left
  corner chamfered and the bottom-right rounded** (chamfer 3.2, radius 2.8). The chamfer and the
  round are diagonally opposite, as the entry asks, and the glyph is upright because the row it
  appears in sits under the upright-post and upright-wall cells. Measured 6.00..18.38 ×
  4.50..20.62, 28.3 % coverage.
- The round corner is a swept crescent: the arc on the front face, the same arc shifted by the
  thickness, and the two tangent edges that join it to the top and side bands. That is the same
  construction the top and side bands already use (the edge extruded by −v), so the corner needs no
  new rule.
- All three Shape rows point at the one glyph. The entry names `FILL_POST_SHAPE` and
  `FILL_SHELF_SHAPE` because the wall pair did not exist when it was written; "`contour::POLYGON`
  no longer names a furniture build" can only hold if the wall row moves too, and the build it runs
  is the same `FillMode::Shape`.
- `contour::POLYGON` stays where it belongs: the sketch tab's regular-polygon tool rows.
- `scene::PANEL_SHAPE` is a new constant, so `crates/ui/src/icons.rs` gains it and the uri count in
  `every_icon_uri_names_its_theme_and_file` moves 133 → 134; `assets/README.md` gains the name in
  the `scene` row, which the theme-table test compares against `icons::scene::ALL`.

## Ruled out

- **A lying plate for `panel-shape`** (a plan with a chamfered near corner and a rounded far one).
  Every variant was rendered: in isometric a horizontal-plane round is foreshortened to
  `0.293 · r` of visible depth at the near or far corner and `0.5 · r` at the left or right extreme,
  so at a radius the board could carry the rounded corner either vanished at 26 px or ate the plate
  (the render read as a boat or a pot). A corner rounding only reads on a face seen squarely, which
  is why the shape board is upright.
- **An upright board with the chamfer at the top-right and the round at the bottom-left** (the other
  diagonal): the rounded foot removes the corner that anchors the slab, and at 16 and 26 px the
  board reads as leaning.
- **Rounding a corner of the lying plate's near edge** with the band wrapped around it: the band and
  the top face then overlap at the lobe flanks and the glyph turns into a blob.
- **A wavy near edge with a bigger amplitude or a thicker plate** (1.8–2.2 units, plate 3–4): the
  band becomes the loudest shape and the glyph reads as a boat. The far-edge scallop is the only
  arrangement that kept both the plate and the wave.
- **Reusing `scene::PANEL` for the shape rows with a different tone**: the task is the artwork, and
  two cells that differ only in alpha read as one tool.
