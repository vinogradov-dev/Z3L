# Y15 — a node's own decor mapping, and the question the entry holds before the field

a node's own decor mapping, and the question the entry holds before the field

## What was measured

- `scene::Decor::uv` (`crates/scene/src/decor.rs:255`) turns a point of the drawable's own frame in
  millimetres into a texture coordinate: `covers` spans the image's width, the height follows the
  image's aspect, and `turns` rotates the four axes. The board's `covers` and `turns` are the
  record's, so every board of a cabinet is faced at one scale and one turn.
- Exactly two consumers call it, and both pass the drawable's local point: the viewport through
  `decor_uv` (`crates/render/src/geometry.rs:503`, used by the shaded and the flat path) and the
  tracer through `soup` (`crates/final/src/soup.rs:132`, which writes the three corner uvs of every
  face). One function, two callers: a mapping has to enter that function or the two frames disagree.
- One `Decor` per library record is shared, not copied: `Decors::decor`
  (`crates/scene/src/decor.rs:340`) is keyed by the material's number and answers one `Arc<Decor>`
  to every drawable of that material, and `DecorId` is the renderer's texture key
  (`crates/render/src/gpu.rs:75`). A per-node `Decor` clone would therefore upload the same picture
  once per board — 40 boards, 40 textures.
- The document has no mapping: `Panel.render_material` (`crates/model/src/panel/mod.rs:129`) and
  `Object.render_material` (`crates/model/src/object.rs:770`) are both `Option<MaterialRef>`, both
  written by `Project::set_node_material` (`crates/model/src/project/node_cmds.rs:88`), each bumping
  the revision once. There is no engine command for either slot yet: `node.material.set` is U129's.
- `materials::Turns` (`crates/materials/src/material.rs:122`) and `scene::Turns`
  (`crates/scene/src/decor.rs:118`) are both a `u8` counted modulo four; `model` is L1 and may
  depend on neither `materials` (L2) nor `scene` (L4), so the document needs its own turn value.
- `units::Length` is serde as integer micrometres, so a scale and an offset are document numbers and
  no float reaches the model.
- The card Y15's Shape places beside the render material slot is U129's, and U129 has not landed
  (`[ ]` in `## Next`, app stream): the panel and object cards carry no material row today.

## Decisions

- **The mapping is composed at uv time and the picture stays one.** `Drawable` already carries
  `decor: Option<Decor>` (`crates/scene/src/lib.rs:571`); the node's mapping joins the drawable beside
  it, and one function answers both frames' coordinate, which is what keeps a cabinet's forty boards
  on one texture and one `DecorId`.
- **The document value is the node's, not the record's** (ADR 0251, "What we do not decide here",
  names it Y15's): a scale, an offset in two axes and a quarter turn.

## Ruled out

- **A `Decor` per node**, cloned with the node's own `covers` and `turns`: it re-uploads the picture
  once per drawable, and it makes `DecorId` a mapping's name instead of a picture's.
- **A real unwrap, or a box projection with an axis per face.** The entry closes it in its own words:
  a contour part is a prism and a shop aligns a grain rather than unwrapping a body. A per-face axis
  picker is a partial unwrap and costs a fourth field, a viewport convention nobody else draws and a
  card row that the plate cannot preview.
- **A continuous turn in degrees.** The record's turn is a quarter count
  (`materials::Turns`, `scene::Turns`), so a node's continuous angle would be a second kind of turn in
  one pipeline and would not survive the trip through `scene::Turns`.
