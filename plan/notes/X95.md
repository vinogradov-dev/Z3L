# X95 — the post is the panel's board stood up

## What was measured

- `assets/icons/scene/post.svg` (old) drew an object 5.2 units across: the `u × v` top face was the
  dominant shape at 0.45 and the two 13-unit side faces at 0.12, so the glyph was a hexagonal prism.
  Rendered at 26 px beside `panel`, `shelf`, `void` it read as a leg, exactly the report.
- `crates/ui/src/toolbars/spec.rs:926` — the cell runs `ClassCode::VERTICAL_PANEL` with
  `FillMode::Fit`, so the object the icon must show is a board standing in the niche, not a word.
- The class' drawing rule existed but only in words: `STYLE_GUIDE.md` §9.2 said "upright: the same
  plate standing on `w`, its thickness in `v`" with no sentence on which face carries the tone.
- The published basis is `x = 0.866 (u − v)`, `y = 0.5 (u + v) − w`; `product`, `cuboid` and
  `assembly` are drawn on it, so the upright is too. `panel` and `shelf` keep their own shallower
  pitch (6.5/5.5 and 7/3) and are not the reference for an upright's angle.
- The redraw measures `6.00..18.00 × 2.62..21.38` and 30.5 % coverage at the 64 px audit raster,
  against 15.6..20.6 % for the prism it replaces; `product` is 30.8 % and `assembly` 29.7 %.

## Decisions

- The object is `u = 8.5`, `v = 3`, `w = 11` grid units: the front face is the result tone (0.45),
  the top band (`0.5 · v` = 1.5 units) and the side strip (`0.866 · v` = 2.6 units) are passive
  (0.12), and the three faces meet at the near vertical edge at `x = 14.38`, drawn 1.5 as an inner
  edge. The thickness of 3 matches the plate in `panel.svg`, so the two glyphs are one board in two
  orientations.
- `STYLE_GUIDE.md` §9.2 and §14 now carry the rule the old drawing missed: the vertical face is the
  largest and loudest shape, the thickness is one band on top and one down the side, and a slab whose
  top or side is as loud as its face reads as a column. §9.2 also says `post` is `panel` stood up.
- `.pi/skills/draw-icon/SKILL.md` §6 gains the check that would have caught it: the largest face
  carries the result tone, and a passive face that is the biggest shape means the wrong form.

## Ruled out

- Keeping the 45°-ish pitch of `panel`/`shelf` (6.5/5.5): at that pitch a standing board's top band
  is 2.5 times taller and the face shrinks, which is the column reading again.
- An `u = 9.5`, `v = 2.6`, `w = 10` board and an `u = 9`, `v = 3`, `w = 10.5` one: both were
  rendered at 26 px beside the real cells; the first is squat, the second gives no more mass than the
  chosen one while pulling the bottom edge to `21.0`.
- No code change: the file stem, `icons::scene::POST`, the README theme row and `Theme::Scene` stay,
  so `crates/ui/src/icons.rs` is untouched (`assets/README.md` needs no edit either — no rule in it
  was wrong, only a rule missing from the guide).
