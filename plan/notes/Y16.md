# Y16 — the picture's own tiling over a decorated board, drawn where the coordinate wrap

the picture's own tiling over a decorated board, drawn where the coordinate wraps

## What was measured

- `scene::Decor::uv` (`crates/scene/src/decor.rs`) divides by the record's `covers`, adds the
  record's turns and the node's mapping, so **one cell of its coordinate is one picture** and a
  whole number is exactly where the sampler repeats. The vertex carries that coordinate —
  `FaceVertex::uv`, baked by `face_uv` (`crates/render/src/geometry.rs`) — so an overlay can read
  the vertex's own value and the coordinate function keeps the two callers it had (the vertex
  builder and `crates/final/src/soup.rs`).
- The face passes bind **one `DecorGroup` per picture** (`crates/render/src/gpu.rs`), and the white
  stand-in is one shared group for every undecorated drawable. A per-group flag therefore says "this
  is a picture" without a per-vertex attribute, which would have changed the face vertex layout, its
  stride in `pipelines.rs` and every `FaceKey` in `cache.rs`.
- `CameraUniform` is already written **per view**, and `ViewSpec` already carries a per-view style and
  shadow switch, so a per-view grid costs two uniform slots and no upload.
- The View tab's Display group held one live cell (`view.display.shadows`). The map's own rule
  (`every_id_is_unique_and_namespaced_by_its_parent`) spells a cell of `view.display` as
  `view.display.decor_grid`: the entry's `view.decor_grid` would break that test, so the id follows
  the group and not the hint.
- The theme's guide ink is `SceneColors::helper`, pinned to `ui::theme::helper` by a test, so the grid
  needed no second ink role; `ui::display` is where a workspace number becomes a frame value.
- `cargo xtask file` already takes `--stdin` (the usage line in `state-contract/src/cli/mod.rs`
  advertises it), so the `[plan]` debt entry that says it cannot carry `plan/map.md` is stale: the
  map's 158 kB row went through the tool.
- The report/tracer never see this: `render_with_lighting` builds a `ViewSpec` of its own and carries
  `decor_grid: None`, because a guide needs the screen's scale in points.

## Decisions

- **The shader draws the grid from the vertex's own coordinate** (ADR 0253): the step is the file's,
  the line falls on every whole coordinate, and the overlay cannot disagree with the print. A line
  pass would re-derive the wrap in world space for every board, turned or mapped.
- **The weight is the theme's, in points** (`ui::theme::DECOR_GRID_STROKE` = 1.5, the contour weight)
  and the shell turns it into physical pixels (`ui.ctx().pixels_per_point()`); the ink is the frame's
  helper at `DECOR_GRID_INK_ALPHA` = 0.65, so the picture under it still reads. The shader keeps no
  size and no colour of its own.
- **One line per axis, faded where a picture repeats faster than it can be read**: a line whose cell
  falls under two line-widths is gone, and only that axis steps back — a board at a grazing angle
  keeps its vertical seams.
- **The switch is the view's** (`Leaf::decor_grid`, off by default, the tab keeps it, a fresh document
  starts off), like the ground shadow's (U107): `ViewSpec::decor_grid: Option<DecorGrid>`.
- **`DecorGroup` carries a 16-byte uniform** saying whether the bound texels are a picture;
  `DecorGroup::new` takes its texels as one `Texels` value so adding that answer did not push the
  constructor past `clippy::too_many_arguments`.

## Ruled out

- **A checker**: it tints the picture the guide exists to help read, and the seam becomes a tone
  change instead of a line.
- **A per-vertex flag** (`uv.z` or a second attribute): a stride change for one overlay, and every
  face buffer rebuilt for a switch that only touches a uniform.
- **A frame-level `SceneFrame::decor_grid`**: a point is not a pixel, so the frame cannot carry a
  weight; the per-view spec is the only place the screen's scale is known.
- **A second colour role for the grid**: the guide ink is measured against both variants and every
  other role; a new ink would have to be measured too.

## What proves it

- `crates/render/src/lib.rs`: the uniform test (off draws nothing, the half-width is the stroke's,
  a hostile stroke falls back to a pixel, a hostile ink is repaired) and the ignored GPU test — four
  pictures across one board are five lines, evenly spaced, and a mid-cell column carries only the
  other axis's crossings.
- `app/src/document.rs`: the switch is off on a fresh view, one leaf's alone, moves no revision, the
  per-view spec carries the theme's weight at two pixels a point, and a second tab starts off while
  the first keeps its answer.
- `crates/ui/src/editor/tests.rs`: the ribbon asks the shell for the switch and writes no document.
- `cargo xtask ci` green in `stream/render`, `ui-rules` and `docs` included.
