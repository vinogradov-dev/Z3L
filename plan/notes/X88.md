# X88 — the object turn stops where the bodies stop

## What was measured

- The owner's scene (`/home/alx/scene.z3l`, not committed — a scene is the maker's data) holds a cone
  turned into a 190 x 330 x 100 board: the cone's own surface is **1.2 mm inside** the board at its
  corner, and 15 mm clear of the tall panel beside it. **The object move is not the gap**: carried from
  a free start the rule cuts the pull at the board's own face, exactly. `commit_rotation` answered
  `measured: false`, so the turn was the one gesture that could write a body inside another (ADR 0180
  deferred exactly this), and that is what X88 closes.
- The world box is unusable as a turn's reading. A door's world box at 90° is a metre-wide square, so a
  furniture mover measured by `overlaps_box` could not be opened at all. The turn therefore measures
  **every** mover by `Measured::meets`/`sunk` — its own box, or its own body — which is the predicate
  `resting_conflict` already reads, so the drag, the release and the refusal are one answer.
- The band: five cells **do not fit** the narrowest window (640 pt) in Russian with their keys. Measured
  with the dim `Gizmo` caption still in place the fifth cell ended at 497.7 pt against 448 available
  (the right zone's 30 %). Removing the caption and tightening the cells' padding to one point lands
  the band at **431.4 pt** in Russian and 325.9 pt in English: every word and every key kept, and no
  word shortened. The `Gizmo` caption is gone from the status row (the Settings page keeps its own).

## What landed

1. `geom::contact::Solid::turned(turn, pivot)` — the same body re-posed rigidly about a world pivot,
   the mirror of `shifted`; implemented by `contact-parry`'s `Body` from the shape it already shares
   (a ring drag re-poses once a frame and never rebuilds a shape).
2. `SurfaceBox::turned(turn, pivot)` — the box at the turned pose, its world box rebuilt from the turned
   corners, so the broad phase and the report are the same reading a carry gets.
3. `Project::rotate_rule(&[Id], Option<&dyn Contact>) -> Option<RotateRule>` and
   `RotateRule::{pivot, limit, blocked_at}`, `RotateLimit { angle, contact }`: the whole turn is asked
   as one placement first, a refused one is bisected (32 asks at most, `TURN_RESOLUTION` = 1e-6 rad —
   0.005 mm at a five-metre reach) and the **granted** end is the answer, so the release writes a
   placement the guard accepts. The rule reuses `Mover`, `Obstacle`, `Measured`, `nested` and
   `CONTACT_MM`, and the obstacles stay lazy (X90).
4. `TransformMode::ObjectRotate` (`view.transform.object-rotate`, `Shift+E`), `RotateKind { Free,
   Object }` beside `MoveKind`, `Target::rotate_rule`, `commit_rotation(..., measured)`, the gizmo's
   `Drag::{measure, rule, granted, stop}` with a `z3l::rotate` log line that names the body, and
   `stop_text` moved to `gizmo_math` so both gizmos print a stop the same way.
5. Tests: a door hinged at its corner stops on the wall's face (the far corner lands exactly on it,
   `Stop` names the wall, the granted placement passes the guard and one degree further does not); a
   cone tips over its own surface eight degrees past its box; a free turn passes through; the frame
   test drives one ring drag twice — 60° through the wall in the free turn, cut short in the object one
   — and the band/key/menu tests are five-wide.

## Ruled out

- **A new `Solid::travel_turn` or a swept-angle query in the port.** Bisection over the same predicate
  is twenty-odd cheap asks and needs no new geometry kernel work.
- **Measuring a furniture mover by its world box, as a carry does.** It refuses the door's first degree.
- **Naming the fifth mode "Object rotate" / «Вращение объекта».** Two words cost more than the band has;
  the pair reads `Rotate`/`Turn` and «Вращение»/«Поворот», with the hint beside the band saying which
  is which. A shorter key spelling (`⇧E`) was refused: it is a second spelling of one binding.

## Left as debt

A turn is not bounded by the workspace floor, it has no typed angle, and a hand that drags more than
half a revolution may be granted an angle that swung the body through a neighbour: the granted end is
always a *free* placement (the bisection verifies it), but the rule answers the destination and not the
path (`plan/debt.md`, `[gizmo]`).
