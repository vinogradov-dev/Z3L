# C4 — a circle by diameter and an arc from its start, end and radius

a circle by diameter and an arc from its start, end and radius

## What was measured

- The circle cell declared `sketch.create.circle.two-points` with `run: None`, and the arc cell
  declared `sketch.create.arc.three-points` with `run: None` while the cell's own method *is* the
  three-point arc (`DrawTool::Arc`) — so the arc cell promised a method it already ran, and printed
  the wrong words for its main face (`ToolArcTwoPoints`, «Through 2 points»).
- Typed numbers reach a draft only as the point they place: `PointState::placed` → `apply_click` →
  `DrawDraft::push`, and `path_with` builds the figure from the stored points alone. A new method
  therefore cannot carry a radius in a parameter; the number has to be encoded in the point, or the
  draft grows a second channel that no other tool uses.
- `FieldParam::Diameter` already existed in the atom (`crates/ui/src/point_fields.rs`, "Ø") and no
  tool read it: the atom was built for exactly these two cells and only the radius half was wired.
- With one plate on screen, Tab moves the keyboard to the *same* row (`focus_next` wraps), so nothing
  blurs and a typed number is never committed by Tab. Enter commits the focused plate's text and
  places the point in one frame. The frame test has to spell that order out: Tab on a two-plate stage,
  Enter alone on a one-plate stage.
- A typed `0` releases a lock instead of locking the number («zero is the way out of a lock»), so a
  test that wants the +X direction types 90 and rotates the expectation, not 0.
- `CONTOUR_TOOLS` is the shell's key path and the Settings page's shortcut list, and
  `every_drawing_tool_names_the_method_the_map_gives_it` demands a key for every tool in it. The
  plain and shifted chords of both new methods are already taken by the methods of the same cell
  (`C`/`Shift+C`, `A`/`Shift+A`), and `Chord` offers no third form the ribbon hint can print.
- The stored path is *local* to the object: the commit re-anchors it at its creation origin, so a
  frame test asserts the world placement or the bounds, not the coordinates of the first arc.
- `Path::arc_from_center` computes its centre, radius and angles from points and rounds all three, so
  a stored arc end can miss its click by a micrometre; the new constructor keeps that behaviour
  instead of inventing an exactness the path does not have elsewhere.

## Decisions

- `DrawTool::CircleDiameter` is a tool of its own, not a flag on `Circle`: the two clicks are the
  same, the stored ring is the same (`Path::circle_through`), and only the plate differs — a radius
  in one cell, a diameter in the other. A flag would have made `points()`, `label()` and the field
  stage read a field that exists only to choose a number.
- `DrawTool::ArcByRadius` takes three clicks: start, end, and a third that stands one radius from the
  start. Its stage reuses `Shape::Radius` with the anchor at the arc's start, so the number is the
  pointer's distance from the start — which is the radius itself when the pointer is the centre the
  person is picturing — and the placed point encodes the radius exactly, a typed one included. That
  is what lets a radius under half the chord reach the model instead of being clamped in the widget.
- `Path::arc_from_chord(start, end, radius, side)` is the one constructor; it puts the centre on
  `side`'s side of the chord and stores the minor arc, so the figure bows away from the pointer, and
  it refuses a radius under half the chord with its own error naming both lengths (ADR 0130).
- `DrawDraft::push` now validates a click that *completes* a fixed figure before storing it, exactly
  as `close` does; the arc's short radius and the circle's three collinear points come back as the
  model's sentence and the draft keeps the points that can still be corrected, rather than holding a
  point no commit can use.
- The third stage draws the chord as its construction line even though it is fixed: the radius is
  measured against a chord the person can see, and both ends are already placed.
- Neither method gets a key: the cell's key runs the workspace's pinned method, so the list is the
  route and `CONTOUR_TOOLS` keeps only the tools a key can start (documented there).
- The arc cell's main method now says `ToolArcThreePoints` («Through 3 points»); the wrong
  `ToolArcTwoPoints` and the pointless `ToolCircleTwoPoints` rows left the message table, and
  `ToolCircleCentreDiameter` / `ToolArcStartEndRadius` took their place.
- Two icons, `contour/circle-diameter` and `contour/arc-radius`, both `draw` ink with `view` control
  points and reference lines: a circle broken at both ends of a diameter, and an arc between two end
  dots with the radius standing on its centre dot. The chord was drawn first and dropped: at a 24
  grid it crossed the radius stub and read as a plus, and the two end dots already say start and end
  (ADR 0055, 0124).

## Ruled out

- A second channel from the field atom into the draft (a `radius` slot beside `sides`): a typed
  number is a promise for one point, and every other tool encodes it in the point it places; a
  second path would make the draft remember a stage the atom already owns.
- Making the third click the *centre* itself (radius = its distance from the start, thrown away when
  the pointer is not on the chord's perpendicular): it reads the wrong number for every pointer that
  is not exactly on the perpendicular, and it would place the marker away from the hand.
- Clamping the radius to half the chord inside the widget: the model's refusal is the readable
  sentence the draft has to keep, and a clamp would silently draw a semicircle instead.
- Storing a completing click and letting the commit refuse it: measured as a dead end, because a
  complete fixed tool ignores every further click while the plates still show its numbers.
- A major arc, or the arc through the pointer the three-point tool already draws: `start, end and
  radius` is one figure under this record, and a second reading needs its own ADR.
- Binding the two methods to a chord of their own cell (`Ctrl+C` for copy is taken; `Alt` prints as
  part of no shortcut label): the list and the pin already reach them, and a key that shadows an
  existing gesture is a finding.

## Measured after the plan was filed

- No live look was taken: the plates, the chord guide and the preview are asserted through the
  draw-field tests and three frame tests (a typed diameter, a typed arc radius whose centre lands on
  the pointer's side, and a radius under half the chord that the draft keeps), and the two icons are
  measured by the footprint, coverage and README-table tests in `crates/ui/src/icons.rs`.
