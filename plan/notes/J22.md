# J22 — What was measured

## What was measured

- Settings held `https://opencode.ai/inference/openaihttps://opencode.ai/inference/openai/v1/v1`;
  the panel posted to it and the endpoint answered 404. The same path with one address answers 401,
  so the wire and TLS were already right and the data was wrong.
- No code concatenates the field: `assistant_ui` binds `settings.assistant.endpoint` to one
  `TextEdit`, and `Endpoint::chat_url` only trims a trailing slash before adding
  `/chat/completions`.
- The page drew the agent process before the address, the address field was narrower than the
  column, and the modal was 640 × 320 with a 430 pt content column.

## What was decided

- `AssistantSettings::endpoint_problem` is the one rule. It answers one of five clauses: no scheme,
  a second scheme, no host, a space, or a completion path the client would add again. Empty is not a
  problem; it means "ask nothing".
- The settings page shows the clause under the field and the panel repeats it when a message is
  sent, so the person is not left with "no model is connected" or a 404.
- The Assistant page now leads with the address and puts the process under an *advanced* heading,
  with one sentence for each of the two ways and one for what happens next. The modal is 760 pt wide
  with a 540 pt column and a 420 pt minimum height; the three fields fill the column.
- Provider presets are not implemented. ADR 0189 says no provider name appears in a type, a match
  arm or a default, and a preset table is exactly that. The question and three shapes are in
  `plan/questions.md`.

## What was ruled out

- Repairing a doubled URL in code: the second half may be a path the person meant, and a silent
  rewrite would hide the paste that caused it. The page names the clause instead.
- A network probe on every key stroke: the rule is local, and a probe would spend the person's
  key and quota.
- Making the endpoint field multiline: a URL carries no line break and a multiline field invites
  one. The field is wider instead, and the validation rejects a newline.
