# U122 — four masters for the creation menu, drawn on the canonical basis

## What was measured

- `crates/ui/src/icons.rs` carried ten scene icons; `panel` is the wavy contour plate and
  `panel-shape` the cut board (U117), so the plain plate `panel.svg` held before U117 existed
  nowhere: the plain Panel cell U123 adds has no artwork.
- The creation menu U123/U124/U125 build has six keys — Post, Shelf, Wall, Overlay wall, Evenly,
  By the area shape — and four of them had no glyph: the plain board, the overlay board, the row
  of boards and the largest rectangle in a section.
- `post` is drawn on the canonical isometry of `STYLE_GUIDE.md` §9.1 (30°, `x = 0.866 (u − v)`,
  `y = 0.5 (u + v) − w`); `shelf` and `panel-shape` sit on a 6.5/5.5 pitch (40°) and were left as
  they are, because the theme is not re-cut by an artwork task.
- Measured at 64 px with `rsvg-convert` and alpha-weighted coverage (the test's own formula):
  `post` 30.5 %, `panel` 30.6 %, `void` 18.6 %, `shelf` 20.1 %. The new masters land at 24.9 %
  (board), 31.7 % (overlay), 32.4 % (row), 32.3 % (fit); every footprint sits inside
  `2.25..21.75` and every glyph reads beside `post`, `shelf`, `wall` and `void` at 16, 22 and
  26 px on the dark and the light variant.

## Decisions

- **`board` is a lying plate, 11 × 6.5 × 2 on the canonical basis**, face in the result tone and
  two passive thickness bands: the shelf's own construction, not a row of the old `panel.svg`
  numbers. It is the plate `post` (standing) and `shelf` (lying, off-basis) already are, so the
  plain Panel cell belongs to the family; it is deliberately close to `shelf`, because a panel
  and a shelf are one object with a class, and the two never share a menu.
- **`row` is three upright boards on `post`'s construction**, 3.5 × 6 × 1.7 at a 5.7 pitch (a
  2.2 gap), stepped along `u`. Three plates in a line say "evenly" with the gaps as paper, and
  reusing the post formula is what makes "a row of posts" read as the same object as `post`.
- **`fit` is one flat area**: a right trapezoid (the section) with a sloped top edge, its corner
  region passive and the largest rectangle under the slope in the result tone, drawn with its own
  result outline. Flat is the right register — the build is about the area and what fits in it,
  and the section is 2D (`STYLE_GUIDE.md` §8.1); the tonal split is drawn as two disjoint fills so
  no `fill-opacity` is composited onto a second one.
- **`overlay` is the board (result, upright, 12.1 × 6.4 × 2) standing proud of the frame's face,
  with the face's visible lip left passive above and right of it.** The lip is drawn as one
  disjoint polygon, its visible part, so no translucent fill ever lands on another and no hidden
  edge of a back plate shows through the board (which is what a two-plate draft did at 64 px).
- The constants are `icons::scene::{BOARD, OVERLAY, ROW, FIT}` and the uri count moves 134 → 138;
  `assets/README.md` lists the four names in the `scene` row, which the theme-table test compares
  against `icons::scene::ALL`.

## Ruled out

- **A second plate behind the overlay board** (a full passive parallelogram with its own
  silhouette, or a wall-style plate at another depth): every variant read as two boards, which is
  the reading the entry says a lap must not produce, and the back plate's inner edges showed
  through the front board's 45 % fill. Two plates drawn with a lip — a small board on a big face
  (a "picture frame"), a board smaller than the face on all sides, and the concentric variant —
  were rendered and all read as a frame or a moulding.
- **A lap band inside the board's face** (a passive strip along one edge of the board): it says
  "this edge is banded", which is `edge-band`'s meaning, not the mounting.
- **Glueing the new glyphs to cells now**: the Panels group becomes three cells in U123 and the
  menu arrives with U123/U124/U125, so a `spec.rs` wiring in this task would be undone by the next
  one; the constants wait for their buttons, like `board` waited for `panel.svg` to become the
  contour plate.
- **A flat frontal overlay** (a result rectangle over a passive one, offset): frontal plates are
  not a register the scene theme has — every panel there is isometric — and two offset rectangles
  read as a shadow.
