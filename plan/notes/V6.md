# V6 — a cone is a primitive drawn by its base circle and finished by the pull

## What was measured

- `PrimitiveKind` had one member, `Sphere` (`crates/model/src/primitive.rs`), and `Primitive` one
  arm, so the *Model* tab could offer a cone only as `planned(...)` — `crates/ui/src/toolbars/spec.rs`,
  `planned("model.primitives.cone", Message::ToolCone)` — and `assets/icons/primitives/cone.svg` was
  a zero-byte placeholder.
- The entry's `files:` hint named `crates/param/src/lib.rs`, written when a cone was expected to be a
  kernel solid. It is not there: `crates/param` turns a panel or a constructive body into a `SolidPlan`
  (`build_panel`, `validate_extrusion_contour`) and never sees a `Primitive`; a primitive's mesh comes
  from `crates/mesh/src/primitive.rs` through `evaluate_primitive`. The hint is corrected on the entry.
- The sphere's gesture is the precedent: `DrawTool::Sphere | SphereDiameter | SphereThreePoints` share
  the sketch's circle grammar, `DrawIntent::Primitive(kind)` says what the commit becomes, and
  `Editor::commit_primitive` reads the drawn circle back as a centre and a radius
  (`Path::circle_center_radius`). Its commit is one `object.primitive.create` and no scope.
- The height pull (`app/src/extrude_gizmo.rs`) already served two shapes: a body with a stored height
  and a profile a body cell drew, the second inside an `EditScope` so the create and the sweep are one
  undo entry. `Body::read` reads the contour of a `Constructive` or a `Path` and refused both other
  payloads; `Document::preview_extrude` rebuilt the body with one number changed.
- The pull's card is `ToolView` (`crates/ui/src/tool_card.rs`); a row is a `ToolRow` with a `ToolParam`.
  A lettered plate (`lettered_height`) is one row by construction, and `ToolParam` had three members.
- `object.primitive.replace` already existed and was reached only from the object card through
  `ObjectPrimitiveReplace`; no gesture wrote it inside an edit scope.

## Decisions

- The cone is a primitive and not a profile swept on Z: a frustum narrows as it rises, so it is not an
  extrusion, and its mesh derives from four numbers in `crates/mesh` beside the sphere's. ADR 0141.
- The base circle is drawn on the work plane and becomes the node's origin and its local `z = 0`; the
  body rises on local `+Z`, as a drawn profile's does, so the pull's arrow can stand on its top circle
  centre without a second frame.
- A zero top radius is one value, not a missing number: the mesh ends in an apex vertex and has no top
  face, and the model refuses only the gap between zero and `MIN_PRIMITIVE_SIZE`, so an almost-apex
  ring the mesh cannot tell from the apex is a readable error. The card's top field starts at zero
  (`length_row_from`), where every other length field starts at the smallest positive dimension.
- The drawn base commits the node at the ribbon's extrusion height inside an `EditScope`, and the pull
  writes `object.primitive.replace` in that scope: base, taper and height are one undo entry, the ✕
  rolls the scope back, and a cancelled create leaves neither a node nor an entry.
- The pull gained one optional number (`Pull::top`, `ExtrudeEvent::{Preview,Finish}.top`) rather than a
  second gizmo or a second card: the card is `ToolView::pull(height, top, ..)`, which draws one row
  when `top` is `None` and two when it is not. A body's pull reads exactly as before.
- `Preview` and `Finish` carry the pair, not the gizmo's state, because the shell is the one caller and
  the values are the preview's identity: a drag that returns to the same pair writes nothing.
- `crates/ui/src/command.rs` gained `dispatch_in(scope, ..)`, replacing the private
  `object_card::apply_object_edit` and serving the new `Editor::replace_primitive_in`: one scope rule
  for the card's fields and the pull.
- A primitive cell's method hint now names the shape — «Cone: Centre and radius» — because a sphere and
  a cone share the sketch's circle methods and the method alone would read twice in one ribbon row.
  `Message::FillMethod` became `Message::ToolMethodName`, which is the same text used by both cells.

## Ruled out

- A cone as a drawn profile plus a revolve: there is no revolve feature, and adding one is a different
  task (the ribbon's *Revolve* cell is still planned).
- A separate `ConeGizmo`: the pull's state machine, its preview, its focus rule and its card are the
  same, and a copy would be the second mechanism ADR 0118 forbids.
- The cone's height as a stage of the draw draft (a phantom dragged before the commit): the pull already
  exists, already writes one command, and already owns the typed `H` at the hand.
- Making the top radius a card-only number after the commit: it would cost a second undo entry for one
  shape, and the pull's card is where the two numbers belong together.
- Constraining the top to be no wider than the base: a splayed blank is a real shape, and the mesh,
  the bounds and the winding already accept it (asserted in the tests).
