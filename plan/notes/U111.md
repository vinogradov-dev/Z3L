# U111 — a side is named once, and the drawing's three conventions with it

a side is named once, and the drawing's three conventions with it

## What was measured

- `app/examples/scene_wardrobe_800.rs` (an untracked generator) carries the numbers the
  task came from: the carcass is 542 mm deep and the overlay doors make 560; the left
  compartment is 1806 mm wide, and 4 x 320 + 4 x 18 + 454 = 1806, so four clear openings
  of 320 leave an ordinary 454 cell at the bottom. Read as spacing along the axis instead,
  5 x 320 + 5 x 18 = 1690 and the leftover is pushed to the wrong end, which is how the
  shelves landed on the wrong side.
- `crates/ui/src/display.rs` already stated the eye convention: `OpeningView::Left.direction()`
  is `[1, 0, 0]` and `OpeningView::Front.direction()` is `[0, 1, 0]`, with `Front` looking
  from `+Y` towards `-Y`. The comment on the enum said it in words; no code said it.
- The arithmetic behind "left is +X seen from the front": a viewer at `+Y` facing `-Y` with
  `+Z` up has `right = forward x up = -X`, so the viewer's left is `+X` — the same
  direction `OpeningView::Left` looks from.
- `opposite`'s pairs are the drawing's: a left door and a right door, a front and a back.

## Decisions

- **`Side` lives in the model, not in `ui`.** A door, a shelf row and a drawing all name a
  side, and a view preset is only one reader of it. `model` cannot depend on `ui`, so the
  comparison test that ties the two lives in `crates/ui/src/display.rs`, where both are in
  scope.
- **The axis is a `geom::DVec3`.** That is the model's convention for a direction (`frame.rs`
  takes one). The ui test converts it with `to_array()` rather than the model handing out the
  preset's plain triple.
- **The glossary sits in `docs/domain/products.md` §2.1, under the envelope.** The depth is
  the envelope's `y` and left is its `x`, so the words and the box belong on one page; §2.1
  is a sub-section because renumbering §3-§13 for this prose would break every reference.
- **The generator's comment points at `model::Side::Left`.** The example stays a one-off and
  uncommitted, but its comment no longer sends a reader to a view preset to learn a domain
  rule.

## Ruled out

- **A `Side` that also formats its own label.** The words are the message table's (`text`);
  a second label on the model would be a second name to keep in step.
- **`[f64; 3]` for the axis, to match the preset's type.** It would carry a direction in
  screen-shaped numbers and forget the piece's frame; one `to_array()` in the test is the
  cheaper side of the seam.
- **A separate "dimension conventions" document.** `AGENTS.md` §9 sends domain vocabulary to
  `docs/domain/`, and products.md already owns the envelope these three readings describe.
