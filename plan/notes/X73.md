# X73 — a hidden part is sight, not material

The entry's two readings had to be separated before a line was written: "allows building through
itself" and "not being intersected" answer two different questions, and only one of them is the
ray's.

## What was measured

- `crates/model/src/project/niche.rs:518` — `surroundings_around` walked the whole tree, so a
  switched-off panel was a surface of the void exactly like a drawn one; `holds_body` then refused a
  cell that held one, which is the owner's "ghost stuck in the phantom facades".
- `crates/ui/src/view_flags.rs:61` — `ViewFlags::hides` was the only parent walk, so every measuring
  query had no way to ask the session's visibility without `ui` depending on `model` and no more.
- `crates/model/src/project/move_query.rs:442` — the drag's obstacles are complete by design: ADR
  0174's rule answers the **place the piece lands**, so a destination inside a hidden part is
  already cut at its face and a destination past it already passes. The move rule needed nothing.
- `crates/model/src/project/fill.rs:342` — `board` cut one footprint from the cell and nothing else;
  the cell was the only place a hidden body could bound a part.

## Decisions

- `model::Sight` is the hidden set plus the parent walk, handed in the way `SnapSettings` is: the
  document holds no view state (ADR 0065) and a query cannot read a field it does not have.
- The **sight** queries take it: `probe`, `niche`, `fill_plan(_in)`, the void atom, the cursor rule,
  the ruler. The **material** ones do not: `resting_conflict` (no sight at all) and the drag's own
  distance fields, which ask `probe` with `Sight::none` because they measure how far the piece may
  travel, not what the eye can see.
- The fill trims the footprint at the hidden body's face the measured point stands outside of, on
  the two axes the board takes, keeping the larger side when two faces would do. The cell itself
  stays the eye's void, so a body inside it no longer refuses the niche.
- The editor drops the held cell when the visibility revision moves, because hiding a part changes
  the void without moving the pointer.

## Ruled out

- **Cutting the niche cell with the hidden bodies' planes.** A body standing wholly inside the cell
  cannot be removed by more planes (that would need a hole in a convex cell), and the entry's own
  awkward case asks for the rectangle to go around it. The trim belongs on the footprint, which is
  already a convex polygon, so a half-plane cut keeps every downstream `Fit`/`Shape` reader intact.
- **Excluding hidden parts from the drag's obstacles.** It reads as the entry's "the move rule's
  obstacles" but contradicts the owner's "nothing may pass through the part itself", and ADR 0174
  makes the drag answer the destination, so a piece carried past a hidden part already passes it.
- **Trimming with a general 2D boolean (a notch).** `Fit` takes the largest rectangle and `Shape` the
  ring; neither can hold a notch, and a notch is what "never a slice of it" forbids anyway.
- **Re-plumbing `ViewFlags::hides` into `model`.** The walk is one call and the flag is `ui`'s; only
  the query value moved.
