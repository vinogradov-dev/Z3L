# U105 — the wordmark in the header

## What was measured

- The header drew the name as a text label (`theme::PRODUCT_NAME`, 13.5 pt, strong) beside an 18 pt
  placeholder mark from `assets/logo.svg` — a board with two shelves — tinted with `theme::accent`.
- `icons::mark` allocated a **square** `Vec2::splat(size)` and rasterised with
  `maintain_aspect_ratio: false`, so a wide drawing in that slot is stretched or letterboxed.
- The first header drawn with the wordmark showed the paragraph: the mark is a wide drawing, and the
  row's rhythm needs its height, not its side.
- Offscreen shots at 200 % (`target/ui-shots/*-scale200.png`) show the mark clean in the light and
  the dark theme, and the copper accent still on the active tab only.

## Decisions

- One asset, one white ink, `viewBox="2.2 2.2 36 12"`, aspect 3. The canvas is trimmed to the ink plus
  a 0.8-unit margin: the drawing is the owner's, the margin is the header's.
- `LOGO_ASPECT` is the only place the ratio lives, and `wordmark(ui, height, ink)` allocates by height.
  `icons::mark` is deleted rather than kept: a square slot for a wide mark has no consumer left.
- The mark is drawn in the **text** ink, never the accent. The accent is the user's (P1 copper, P2
  magenta, P4 violet), and a brand hue in the chrome would collide with two of the three presets;
  ADR 0136 records it.
- The name stays reachable without a label: the mark carries `PRODUCT_NAME` as hover text and as its
  accessibility label, and the window title still reads the constant.

## Ruled out

- Keeping the text label beside the mark: the two say the same thing twice, and the second time in
  the platform's font.
- A per-theme copy of the mark: the loader tints one master, and `the_logo_asset_is_one_white_ink`
  fails a file that carries a second colour.
- `egui::WidgetType` for the accessibility role: deprecated in this `egui`; `egui::Role::Image` is the
  name, and clippy refuses the alias.
