# U98 — the ruler is a transient atom, and the one probe resolves its point

the ruler is a transient atom, and the one probe resolves its point

## What was measured

- `crates/ui/src/inventory.rs` held 57 command rows and an empty `ATOMS`. The inventory test `every_registered_command_has_one_row` asserted `ROWS.len() == registry.specs().len()`, so the first atom row needed that rule widened to `+ ATOMS.len()` and to refuse an id declared both a command and an atom. `cargo xtask ui-rules` already accepted an atom id from `ATOMS`.
- `app/src/gizmo_math.rs` snaps in work-plane millimetres and answered four targets (`End`, `Grid`, `Axis`, `Free`). The ruler needed a fifth, `Surface`, not a second resolver: 0051 predicted that a snap to panel vertices or edges extends the vocabulary.
- `Project::probe` (`crates/model/src/project/niche.rs:376`) works from any point, a camera outside every product included: `surroundings_around` collects every product envelope, panel and body before the first hit, so a view ray is a legal probe.
- A probe face is a real 3D point; only its distance is quantised to a micrometre, so the ruler rounds the three coordinates once, at `length_from_millimetres`.
- `ToolKind` is exhaustive and gated by `every_tool_kind_appears_exactly_once`: one new tool touched `ToolKind`, `ToolAction`, `ToolActivity`, `tool_activity`, the `Ready` match, the tab's column count and two tests that list the live sketch cells.
- The status bar is drawn before the viewport, so its live value is the hover the previous frame resolved. The label at the pointer is current.

## Decisions

- The atom stores `[Length; 3]` points and computes the distance in whole micrometres; `first`, `current`, `complete` are the state, `MeasureStage` is the reading. A click in the measured stage starts a new measurement at that point; a hover never changes a fixed value.
- The input rule: the probe resolves the surface under the pointer (an exact face point); a ray that meets nothing falls back to the work plane through the snap resolver, with the 10 mm grid, the first point as the axis anchor and Shift for the lock. Alt takes the work plane as it is, without the surface and without the grid. A surface point is not grid-snapped: rounding it would slide it along the view ray, off the face, and the length would be false.
- The ruler is one ribbon cell in a new `sketch.measure` group (`sketch.measure.length`), `ToolKind::Measure`, pressed while armed; the inventory row is keyed by the same id with `Place::Ribbon` and `Applies::Always`, and `M` is filed under it. The `scene` role ink matches the dimension icons; the icon creates nothing, so `draw` would be wrong.
- The shell owns the pointer: `MeasureGizmo::update` resolves and feeds the atom, a primary click measures, and a click on chrome, a widget or another tool's press never measures. Escape leaves the tool in `handle_keys` before the container level, writes nothing and leaves undo empty.
- The ribbon and the key both toggle: the ribbon action arms or drops, the key is consumed by `take_shortcuts` as one more shell action, and both call `Document::toggle_measure`. The shell compares `editor.measure()` across the ribbon draw to reset the gizmos of the tool it replaced.
- The status bar keeps the split ADR 0118 asks for: the left zone names the step (first point, second point, start over), the centre zone shows `Length 500 mm`. The selection summary yields to it while the ruler runs.

## Ruled out

- A local `MeasureTarget` enum or a second snap path: `SnapTarget::Surface` extends the one table, and the drawing tool keeps its own marker colours in its own module.
- A dimension command or a document field: a drawing dimension is a document object with undo; the ruler is information (ADR 0118, 0029).
- A tool card: the ruler has no parameter to type, so ADR 0119's card would be an empty floating box for the sake of consistency.
- Reordering the status bar after the viewport to remove the one-frame lag: the bar is chrome above the scene in the window's order, and a readout that lags a frame is not a control that misbehaves.
- A grid snap on a probed surface point: see above; it would move the point off the surface it reports.
- The near face of a product's reserved box is stepped past: the probe reports a product's shell as `SurfaceKind::Shell` when the ray starts outside it, so `surface_under` restarts the ray one micrometre inside and looks again, at most four products deep. Without that loop the ruler would report the front of the reserved box while the person points at a board behind it; two frame tests name the difference (a direct panel and a product's side panel).
