# X97 — the contour overlay's two inks, and what made them one colour

the contour overlay's two inks, and what made them one colour


## What was measured

- `app/src/contour_gizmo.rs` before the change: `BASE_COLOR = from_rgba_premultiplied(150, 150, 150,
  160)` — 239 on 255 once composited on the dark workspace — and `DRAFT_COLOR = from_rgb(120, 200,
  255)`. Contrast between them 1.3:1, and both over 0.4 in relative luminance: two high values a hue
  apart, which is the report in one number. The grip ink was `(240, 240, 240)`, 1.02:1 against the
  light workspace.
- The selection ink is `scene::SELECTION_LINE_COLOR` = `(0.15, 0.85, 1.0)`, so the old draft sat in
  the same family (CIE76 34 apart) as the ink the renderer already draws a selected chain in.
- The materials the overlay lies on are `scene_build::PANEL_COLORS[0]` (linear `0.62 0.42 0.22`, the
  beech face, sRGB `(206, 173, 130)`) and `EDGE_BAND_COLOR` (sRGB `(118, 124, 134)`). The default
  preset's accent `(224, 162, 74)` is **1.05:1** against the beech face: no warm ink separates from a
  warm board by value, in any preset (P2's magenta 1.6:1, P4's violet 1.7:1).
- `cargo xtask ui-rules` read `crates/ui` only — one `shown.starts_with("crates/ui/")` — so the whole
  shell was exempt from ADR 0063, while `AGENTS.md` §6 already said the rule reads both trees.

## Decisions

- **Two roles, two rules.** `theme::original` is `text_dim` folded 35 % into the workspace of the
  variant: a neutral, quieter than any text, ≥ 2.35:1 on the light workspace and ≥ 3.07:1 on slate.
  `theme::working_copy` is the accent lifted 25 % off the workspace, the same lift `theme::helper`
  takes, so it is ≥ 5.6:1 on the workspace and 0.19–0.39 away from the original in relative
  luminance and 57–102 apart in CIE76, on all three presets in both variants.
- **The pair is cased.** `theme::casing` is the workspace's own ink, drawn 3.5 pt under the 2 pt
  working copy: over the beech face the copper core is nearly the board's colour and the dark rim is
  what reads; over the dark band the bright core reads and the rim is invisible. That is ADR 0121's
  rule — one of the two always reads — applied to a stroke instead of the pointer. 3.0 pt was tried
  and dropped: a half-point rim is sub-pixel at 100 % and reads as a halved line.
- **Weight and dash are the second signal.** The original is 1 pt and dashed (4/3), the working copy
  is 2 pt and solid, so the pair reads where a material flattens the value difference.
- **The rule reads the shell, with the debt named.** `SHELL_INK_DEBT` in `xtask/src/ui_rules.rs`
  lists the eight modules that still carry hand-picked inks; the contour overlay is not among them,
  and the gate now fails on a colour literal in any other shell module. `check_with(root, debt)` lets
  the gate's own test drive both branches.
- A grip takes `theme::text`, so the handle and the words around it are one weight of ink, and its
  ring is the casing instead of the fixed `from_black_alpha(160)`.
- The shot is written at 2 px per point: at 1:1 a 1.5 pt casing is under a pixel and cannot be
  counted. It is the same drawing at double size, so it shows what a person sees, magnified.

## Ruled out

- **A dimmed accent for the original.** Mixing the accent towards the board instead of the dim text
  ink gives a pair that differs only in strength: it separates from the material worse (a warm ink on
  a warm board) and reads as a dimmer copy of the working copy rather than as a different role.
- **Leaving the working copy at the accent's own value.** `(224, 162, 74)` on the beech face is
  1.05:1 and delta_e 12: the line disappears into the panel it is editing, which is the defect this
  task is about, moved one step.
- **A cool ink for the working copy**, the road the old pale blue took. It reads over beech, but no
  palette role is cool: the ink would be invented in the gizmo, which is what the task removes.
- **Keeping the shell exempt and only moving the two constants.** The gate would still pass a new
  literal in `contour_gizmo`, so the next session would re-open the same entry.
