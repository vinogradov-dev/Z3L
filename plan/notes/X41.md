## What went wrong upstream

- `U88` — left the bottom cluster and the product card out — `done`: its Done asserted the square's geometry and the icon over the scene and a panel, but no test moved the pointer over the floating cluster or the creation card, which are the two places the owner saw the pointer vanish. Observation: a live pointer over either surface; the headless frame never named them.
- `U88` — left the tool-owned cursor in — `shape`: the entry said "a gesture that has a cursor of its own keeps the system icon", so a contour showed the arrow and an armed primitive the crosshair, and the square was a fallback rather than the work area's pointer. Observation: arm a contour and move over the scene; the frame test asserted `Crosshair` instead of the square.
- `X40` — left the system-element category out — `shape`: it turned the owner's one phrase ("everything but menus") into "every top layer that is not Foreground" and did not ask whether panels, text fields, floating toolbars and resizing borders are system elements whose icon is the affordance. Observation: a pointer over the tree, the inspector, the cluster and a text field.
- `X40` — left the gate proving the wrong rule — `done`: its Done asserted the square over a panel and over the floating cluster, exactly the surfaces the owner now exempts. A test asserting `Default` there would have failed; the test held the implementation instead of the rule.
- `X40` — left the eye check on the wrong surface — `verify`: the offscreen shot moved the pointer onto the bottom cluster to celebrate the square there, so the only visual check was aimed at the surface that should keep the system cursor.
- `X40` — left the routing unused — `contract`: `AGENTS.md` §1.3 routes every UI and app-chrome change through `.pi/skills/ui-design/SKILL.md`, and its floating-surface bullet already says a floating surface is `Order::Middle` chrome over the scene; the session read the entry and the code, not that bullet, and never asked where the custom pointer stops.

## The sentence that would have caught it

- `.pi/skills/ui-design/SKILL.md` — "a custom pointer stays in the scene rectangle, and every panel, widget, field and border keeps the system icon (ADR 0122)." It replaces the old "The scene rectangle does not shrink when a panel folds" sentence in the floating-surface bullet, so the instruction grows by no line: a session that reads the surface rule before touching the cursor must decide the scene boundary and every system surface, and the owner's "everything but menus" cannot be narrowed silently.
