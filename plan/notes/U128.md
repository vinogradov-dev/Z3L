# U128 — the fill's choices are its menu, and a gap's field stands on its arrow

## What was measured before the first edit

- `app/src/document.rs::gap_fields` drew the fill's two gaps as `Stroke::new(1.0,
  theme::text_dim)`: 1 pt, the dim grey text ink, no heads. That is why the owner could not see
  them. The carry drew `paint_gap_arrow`: amber (`theme::helper`), one hairline and two 8 pt heads.
  Two painters for one measurement.
- `card_anchor` froze the tool card on the gesture's first frame and cleared the anchor whenever the
  card vanished, so the plate reappeared at the next pointer position: the reported "panel runs away
  from the cursor".
- The consumed click was computed as `filling || …`, and the right-click branch required
  `!consumed_click`, so while the fill was armed **no** right press could open a menu at all. This
  was the reason the owner's right click did nothing, and it was a defect, not a design.
- `crates/ui/src/tool_card.rs` had `ToolChoice`/`ToolPick` used by the fill **and** by the
  panel-by-points card (`Document::points_class_row`). The mechanism therefore stayed; only the
  fill's use of it went.

## What was decided

- One painter, `Document::paint_dimension`: 3.5 pt amber, a tick on the part's own plane, one solid
  head (12.5 pt long, 5.5 half-width) on the thing that stops the part. One head, not two: the run
  says which way it measures by pointing at the far end. `GAP_DIM` raised from 0.55 to 0.8 because
  the owner found the dim tone invisible.
- `DistanceFields::show_in_scene` is now the only layout of a measured gap: the fill, a row's outer
  gaps and both layouts of the move. Blanking `row.unit` there means a dimension in the scene is a
  number alone; the inspector keeps "mm".
- `crates/ui/src/scrub.rs` gained `width` and `PLATE_FIELD_WIDTH = 40`, so a plate hugs its number
  instead of drawing an inspector column's 76 pt box.
- The row's count is a plate at `pointer + (22, 26)` with a `×` symbol label, drawn by
  `tool_card::plate` with `takes_the_pointer = true` (the only plate that senses the hand). The pitch
  between boards is a coaxial thin run in the dim ink with no field.
- `node_actions`: `Mark { None, Chosen(bool), Switch(bool) }`, `Entry.mark`, and a drawn row (24 pt
  tall, 178 pt wide: 10 inset + 18 tick column + label + 14 gutter) instead of a `Button`, so the
  tick stands left of the label on one edge. `MenuSource.fill: Option<PanelBuild>` makes the popup
  the operation's menu while the fill is armed; `NodeAction::Fill(FillPick)` carries a class, a
  build, a mounting or a spread, and `Editor::apply_fill_pick` writes the build and re-arms.
- Class rows print the tool's words (`ToolPost/ToolShelf/ToolWall`), not the class registry's
  ("Upright"): the menu names the next click, the tree names the part.
- `Editor::arm_fill` now writes the build in the same step, so a class armed by a cell, a menu row or
  a test keeps the menu's ticks and the click's part in agreement (found through the shot: the ticks
  stood on "Post/Rectangle" while the arm was a shelf and a niche shape).

## What was ruled out

- Keeping `ToolChoice` out of `tool_card` entirely: the by-points card needs a class row, and
  `is_empty`/`with_choices` had to come back with it. `DistanceFields::show` lost its `choices`
  argument only — a distance card has no business asking what the tool makes.
- Making the gap plates take the pointer: they stand in the middle of the void the click works in, so
  a press on a number would become the press that places the part. A frame test proved it (the
  evenly-row's anchor click landed inside the low plate). The count plate is the exception because it
  stands beside the hand and on nothing.
- Keeping the held drag's card: the owner asked for the same grammar on the move, and both layouts
  are one call again.
