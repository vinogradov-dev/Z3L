# U86 — the transform gizmos are smaller and their size is a setting

## What was measured

- `app/src/gizmo_math.rs` drew every translate handle at a fixed `HANDLE_LENGTH` of
  84 pt and hit it with an 8 pt `HIT_RADIUS` plus a 10 pt dead zone; `axis_handles` built the tip
  from the constant, so the drawing and the hit test could not disagree but also could not change.
- `app/src/rotate_gizmo.rs` scaled the rings by `RING_RADIUS_FACTOR = 0.09` of the
  camera distance in both `paint` and `hit_ring`.
- `crates/ui/src/settings.rs` had no gizmo section; `Settings` carried appearance, viewport, recent,
  ribbon and panels. `roll_step_degrees` showed the established shape: a ranged `u32`, a `Scrub`
  field with the unit outside the box, a clamp in `sanitize`, and a JSON round-trip test.
- `Document::viewport` already received `&ViewportSettings` from `main.rs`; the two gizmos were the
  only transform surfaces whose metric was still a constant.
- The vertex gizmo shares `paint_axis_handles`/`hit_axis`, so the handle length has to reach it too
  or a mesh edit would draw one size and grab another.

## Decisions

- `GizmoSettings { handle_points, ring_percent }` lives in `Settings`, clamped 8–400 pt and 1–30 %
  in `sanitize` and again by `handle_length()` / `ring_factor()`, which the shell calls once per
  frame. The default is 56 pt and 6 %: below the old 84 pt and 9 %, but 84 pt stays reachable at the
  top of the range.
- `HandleSpec { axes, length }` groups the class gate and the screen length for `axis_handles`,
  `hit_axis` and `paint_axis_handles`. It keeps the three functions at a sane arity and makes "one
  value serves drawing and picking" the type, not a comment.
- The hit radius and the origin dead zone scale with the handle (`10 %` and `15 %`) with a floor and
  a ceiling: at 8 pt a fixed 8 pt radius would cover the whole handle, and at 400 pt a 60 pt dead
  zone would refuse a pointer near the pivot.
- The arrowhead and its label scale with the handle too (3–9 pt head, label 3 pt beyond it). At the
  old 84 pt this reproduces the previous 9 pt head and 12 pt label exactly.
- `Document::viewport` takes `&GizmoSettings` beside `&ViewportSettings`; the resolved floats enter
  `TranslateGizmo`/`VertexGizmo`/`RotateGizmo` `update` and `paint`, and nothing enters the session
  or the document.

## Ruled out

- **Keeping `HANDLE_LENGTH` as a fallback.** Two sources for the metric is exactly the drift this
  task removes; tests name their own `TEST_HANDLE_LENGTH` instead.
- **Resolving the settings inside the gizmo structs.** A stored value set by the caller makes
  `update` and `paint` depend on hidden state; the explicit parameter keeps the frame's value one
  argument.
- **Scaling the hit radius linearly without bounds.** At the minimum the radius would be 0.8 pt and
  the dead zone 1.2 pt on a handle whose head is 3 pt wide — ungrabbable; the clamp is what makes the
  small end usable.
- **A per-tab gizmo size.** The task is about the hand and the display, not the scene; the workspace
  file is where `ViewportSettings` already lives, so a per-tab copy would be a second setting with
  the same name.
- **Reusing the orientation widget's `RING_RADIUS`.** That constant is the corner cube's own fixed
  geometry, not the transform ring, and tying them would make one control resize a different one.
