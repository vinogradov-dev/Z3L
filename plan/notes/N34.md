# N34 — a frame drawn as a ring builds the part the pointer names

what a session measured about a contour frame, and why the cell is now read at the pointer

## What was measured

- The owner's scene `/home/alx/skaf.z3l`, whose product reserves a **ring** footprint: 2244 x 4422 x
  3885 mm, six segments, an L with one step at `(1684.69, 3703.89)` µm and a 1746.5 mm arc for its
  bottom. Every point inside it answered `NicheRefusal::Footprint`, so no fill built in any mode or
  class: 0201's rule was that a ring which is not one convex ring reserves no one cell.
- The ring's joints **do not meet**: the document keeps an arc by centre, radius and angles in
  millidegrees (0072), so at a 1.75 m radius one millidegree is 30 µm and the arc's endpoint missed the
  next line's stored point by 4 and 15 µm. `Footprint::outline` accepted that (it uses
  `joint_tolerance_mm`), but `Region`/the boolean assume one boundary: `intersect_regions` answered
  nothing, which first surfaced as `Outside` (`Envelope::holds` could not build a region) and then as
  `Unrepresentable`.
- The cell's planes are the ring's chords, and a chord lies **inside** its arc. For the owner's frame
  the bulge is up to 274 mm deep, so a large part of the frame is outside every plane and inside the
  ring. Tested on the file after the first fix: `cell.contains` said no at four of the sampled points.
- `Contour::placed` rebuilt its ring through `Contour::new`, whose `JOIN_TOLERANCE` is 1 µm: a rigid
  move refused a ring that came from the document, so a shaped fill could not even be moved into the
  panel's frame.
- Cost after the change, on the owner's own file (dev profile, one measurement each): 0.1–0.4 ms per
  niche at 2.2 x 4.4 m with the arc; the ring work is a handful of booleans over six segments.

## Decisions

- **Weld where the ring leaves the frame** (`Contour::welded`, called by `Footprint::outline` with
  `joint_tolerance_mm`): each joint is closed on the segment that can move without moving a curve — a
  line's endpoint takes the arc's, two arcs close by shortening the later one's start, whose end stays
  put. A joint the document's tolerance does not cover is left as it came, so nothing that loaded
  before stops loading and the exact readers keep their own refusals (ADR 0203).
- **`Contour::placed` no longer revalidates.** A rigid map keeps every joint gap exactly; refusing it at
  `JOIN_TOLERANCE` only refused rings that came from the document.
- **The ring is read at the pointer** (`frame_ring`): a convex ring answers itself; otherwise every edge
  whose free side holds the point clips it, through `Region::clip_half_plane_around`, which is
  `clip_half_plane` read for a region that need not be convex — the piece the point names, never the
  largest other one. What is left is the arm of the L, and the corner of an L answers the square both
  arms share.
- **The cell asks its ring.** `Cell` keeps the ring beside the planes and `contains` requires both; a
  plane over an **arc** is flagged `arc_chords` and leaves that test, because a chord is a safe wall for
  a probe and the cut but not the boundary of the space the frame reserves.
- **A cell that keeps a ring is never a `Box`.** `is_box` compares corners with their box, and a chord
  polygon can span the very box the ring bulges past; a shaped frame answers `Section`.
- `document_ring` drops a segment the document's step rounds away instead of refusing the ring: a
  micrometre is inside the joint tolerance, and the owner's welded ring left such a sliver against the
  envelope's own side.

## Ruled out

- Answering several cells: the niche is one convex cell by 0106, and the owner asked that a panel builds
  — not that the void be several.
- A cell that holds arcs everywhere (plane triples become ring/plane intersections, `cut` returns rings):
  it is the honest end state for the *box* problem the debt entry now records, but it rewrites the corner
  enumeration, the cut and the projection for posts, and nothing about building a part needs it.
- Refusing a ring that cannot be welded exactly: the envelope's sides and its ring disagree by the
  document's own rounding, and `Envelope::with_footprint` must keep accepting what it wrote.
- Keeping the "largest piece" in `clip_half_plane_around`: a line through a notched ring can cut off a
  bigger piece than the one holding the pointer, and the cell would then not contain the point.

## Verification

- Headless, geometry: `Region::clip_half_plane_around` reads the piece the point names (both arms of an
  L, and `None` for a point the cut leaves out); `Contour::welded` closes a joint the document rounded
  apart, keeps the arc's own curve, and declines a joint past its bound.
- Headless, model: `a_notched_frame_builds_the_arm_the_pointer_names` (both arms and the column, and the
  notch is `Outside`), `a_ring_that_bulges_into_its_own_shape_is_still_a_refusal`,
  `a_point_in_a_bowed_frames_bulge_is_inside_the_void` (a point above the chord is inside the void and
  the section keeps the bulge's curve); the fill's `a_notched_frame_builds_a_part_inside_the_arm_the_pointer_names`
  (both modes, and the created panel's stored ring is inside the frame).
- Headless, UI: the atom carries the footprint refusal and its sentence for a ring that bulges inwards.
- On the owner's file, a scratch probe (deleted): four points — the left arm, the bulge, the upper
  column and the notch — answer a cell, `Fit` and `Shape` build in the three inside points, nothing
  outside the frame, and the notch is `Outside`.
