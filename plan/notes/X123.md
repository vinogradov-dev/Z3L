# X123 — What was measured

## What was measured

- With the assistant panel open, `Delete` did nothing even with one tree row selected. The shell
  built its `modals` flag from Settings, Home, the palette, the product card, the destructive
  question **and `self.chat.open`**, and every shell shortcut is skipped while `modals` is true.
- The panel is an `egui::Window`, not a modal: it may stay open while the person works, so it has no
  right to own Save, Undo or Delete. The focused input already owns its keys through
  `text_edit_focused`.

## What was decided

- The panel keeps Escape and the tool keys for `Document::handle_keys`, and stops counting as a
  modal for `take_shortcuts`. A frame test selects one row, opens the panel and presses Delete.
- The chat input still owns Delete while it has the focus; clicking back into the scene releases it.

## What was ruled out

- Dispatching Delete from the panel or from a focused field: a text field must edit text.
- Making the panel modal and closing it on a scene click: the owner wants it open while working.
