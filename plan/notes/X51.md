# X51 — the arc's second click draws the chord its length plate measures

## What was measured

- `crates/ui/src/draw_fields.rs` (`construction`): a three-point arc drew its chord only once
  two clicks were placed (`DrawTool::Arc => draft.points().len() == 2`). The stage that places the
  through point — one click placed — had no chord at all, and the length plate of that stage sits
  on the chord's midpoint (`PointState::at_stage`, `Shape::Segment`): a number on an invisible
  line. The only marks near it were the reference's dotted +X axis and its sweep (ADR 0127, 0133).
- `DrawDraft::preview` cannot fill that gap: `path_with` builds `Path::arc_through` and needs
  three points, so the preview is `TooFewPoints` and `DrawGizmo::refresh_preview` keeps nothing.
  The arc really is absent from the screen until the third click.
- The third stage already draws its chord in the guide amber: `guides::construction` is a 6/4 dash
  in `theme::helper` at `theme::HELPER_STROKE`, painted from `state.construction` in
  `apps/furnicraft/src/draw_gizmo.rs` (`paint_guides`).
- `DRAFT_COLOR` (120, 200, 255) is the placed chain and `PREVIEW_COLOR` (255, 220, 96) the shape the
  next click would add; neither is the ink of a distance.
- "L1" names one object under both readings: whether it labels the arc's first chord or the first
  plate of the pair, it is the chord from the start to the point being placed — the number and the
  line that carries it. No code depends on the distinction.

## Decisions

- The chord is the **construction line** (guide amber, dashed), not a solid draft segment. The same
  tool draws the same chord in that ink one click later, and draft ink promises an edge the
  committed arc does not contain.
- One condition changed: `DrawTool::Arc => !draft.points().is_empty()`, so the chord exists on both
  arc stages and the plate that already sat on its midpoint now sits on a drawn line.
- The chord ends at the point the click would place (`state.at`, typed numbers included), not at the
  raw pointer, so a locked length or angle moves the guide with the number.
- Tests: `draw_fields::tests::the_through_point_of_an_arc_is_drawn_as_the_chord_its_plate_measures`
  checks the chord's ends, the plate's value and the plate's anchor at four pointer positions;
  `draw_gizmo::tests::the_through_point_of_an_arc_paints_the_chord_its_length_plate_measures` runs an
  `egui::Context` and asserts every painted dash lies on the chord and reaches its end at three
  pointer positions.

## Ruled out

- A solid `DRAFT_COLOR` segment from the start to the pointer, the shape the entry proposed: the
  next click replaces it with the arc, so a solid edge would vanish while the same number stayed on
  a different mark.
- A preview path (`Path::polyline([start, through])`) for the second click: `PREVIEW_COLOR` promises
  the shape the next click adds, and that shape is an arc.
- Dropping the length plate at that stage (X and Y only): the through point would then be placed by
  eye, and every drawing stage reads the numbers its figure takes from its own anchor (ADR 0131).
- A new guide ink for the chord: the construction line is the one ink for a measured distance
  (ADR 0127).
