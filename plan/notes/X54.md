# X54 — What was measured

## What was measured

- `app/src/document.rs:1994` — `drawing = interactive && !pointer_on_chrome && …`: the draft is
  updated only on that branch, and its action is applied at `app/src/document.rs:2122`.
- `app/src/draw_gizmo.rs:286` — `update` places a point only on `response.clicked_by(Primary)`;
  `pointer_over_widget` (the orientation cube's rectangle) is the only other pointer gate.
- `app/src/document.rs:1889` — `pointer_on_chrome` is `dismiss_press` + `chrome.covers` + the tool
  card of the last frame + `draw_gizmo.field_contains` + `contour_gizmo.field_contains` +
  `draft_menu.rect()`.
- `app/src/side_panels.rs:224` — `Chrome` names the ribbon band, the method list, the node context
  menu, the tool card and the two panels. The cluster and its popovers are not in it.
- `app/src/view_cluster.rs:201` — the cluster is an `Area` in `Order::Middle`, centered on the
  bottom edge of the content rect; its two popovers are Areas above it.
- egui 0.37 `src/hit_test.rs` — a press goes to the nearest clickable widget within
  `interact_radius` (5 pt, `src/style.rs:1593`), and `src/containers/area.rs:561` gives an area's
  root `Sense::CLICK` unless the area is movable or `interactable(false)`. Every floating surface
  on this tree therefore swallows a press inside its rectangle.

## Ruled out

- A press on a widget of the ribbon, the header, the panels, the cluster, either popover, the
  draft menu, the file menu or the Settings window: measured, each swallows the press.
- The plates: `field_contains` gates them, and a press on a plate locks its number instead of
  placing a point.
- The right button: the drawing branch owns the secondary click and opens the draft menu for a
  polyline or commits a fixed tool, and that path is inside `if drawing`.

## The owner's report, second round

Named surfaces: the axes widget in the view's top-right corner, and either panel ("press on any
panel and the size is fixed at that place"). Settings on the machine: both panels visible and
expanded, ribbon open, ui_scale 100, the demo work of `~/.config/z3l/settings.json`.

What a headless run added:

- the press boundary is the rectangle, not the painted shape: one point outside a floating
  surface's rectangle is a scene press, and `interact_radius` (5 pt) does not help, because the
  viewport is a direct hit at distance 0;
- the panels leave `INSET` (8 pt) of window around them and their corners are rounded, so a click
  in that margin or just past a corner is the scene's;
- `orientation_rect` on the 1280×840 harness is [910, 133]-[1034, 281], the inspector starts at
  x = 1046, so the 12 pt strip between the axes widget and the panel is scene;
- a press on the scene, then a release on a panel (and the reverse) places nothing, and a press on
  a plate then a click elsewhere does not lock the number: the plate's text round-trips because a
  point is quantised to the micrometre and the row's decimals cover it.

`Z3L_TRACE=1` prints, on every press and release, the pointer, `pointer_on_chrome`, what
`Chrome::covers` says, the band, the two panels, the list, the menu and the tool card, and every
`apply_click` with the point and the tool — the next session reads one real reproduction from it.
