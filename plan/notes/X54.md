## Ruled out

- A press on a widget of the ribbon, the header, the panels, the cluster, either popover, the
  draft menu, the file menu or the Settings window: measured, each swallows the press.
- The plates: `field_contains` gates them, and a press on a plate locks its number instead of
  placing a point.
- The right button: the drawing branch owns the secondary click and opens the draft menu for a
  polyline or commits a fixed tool, and that path is inside `if drawing`.

What a headless run added:

  surface's rectangle is a scene press, and `interact_radius` (5 pt) does not help, because the
  viewport is a direct hit at distance 0;
- the panels leave `INSET` (8 pt) of window around them and their corners are rounded, so a click

`Z3L_TRACE=1` prints, on every press and release, the pointer, `pointer_on_chrome`, what
`Chrome::covers` says, the band, the two panels, the list, the menu and the tool card, and every
`apply_click` with the point and the tool — the next session reads one real reproduction from it.
## The cause, measured

A headless frame test reproduces it without a mouse:

1. arm `DrawTool::Line`, place its start at `s`;
2. hover at `s + (200, 37)` — the live length is 772.3341245859851;
3. click the left panel (any surface that takes the keyboard away works, the axes widget too);
4. hover at `s + (600, 37)`.

Before the fix, step 3 prints `field hands over lost=true enter=false due=false
old=772.3341245859851 shown="772.3"` and `plate locks Length = 772.3`, and the length after step
4 is still 772.334…: the plate keeps the promise the person never made.

- `crates/ui/src/scrub.rs` — the `else if enter || lost || due` branch commits the *text*, with
  `commit = parsed != old`. The text is `number_text(old, decimals)`, and `FieldParam::decimals`
  gives every parameter but `Sides` one decimal, so a live value almost never round-trips: losing
  the focus is always a commit.
- `crates/ui/src/point_fields.rs` — `FieldSet::show` turns a non-zero `result.commit` into
  `locked[index] = true`, and a zero into a release. `FieldSet::new` sets `request: FOCUS_REQUESTS`,
  so every new stage takes the keyboard without a click.
- `crates/ui/src/draw_fields.rs` — `PointState::at_stage` reads a locked parameter instead of the
  live one, which is why the length then stays put while the angle still follows the pointer.

## Ruled out, again

- The draft's pointer gate: `on_chrome` is true over the panels and false only over the axes
  widget, where egui gives the press to the widget's own `Area` anyway.
- A point placed by the axes click: none; `apply_click` ran only on the two clicks inside the
  scene.
