# J12 — what the task's two halves rest on, measured before the first edit

## What was measured

- **The profile has no way in.** `model::WorkshopProfile` holds the numbers the rules read
  (`shelf_gap`, `shelf_loose`, `door_gap`, `door_tolerance`, `rail_drop`, `rail_tolerance`,
  `rail_clearance`, `Sheet` 2800 × 2070 mm) and its own doc comment says "a later session fills the
  same type from the workspace settings (J12)". Nothing does: `ui::Settings` carries appearance,
  viewport, gizmo, snaps, pointer, recent, ribbon, panels and assistant and no profile section,
  `app/src/settings_store.rs` never names the type, and `Session::set_profile`/`with_profile` have
  one caller in the whole tree — a test in `crates/engine/src/inspect.rs`. `Session::profile()` is
  read by `inspect`'s `shelf_fit`, `door_gap`, `rail_place` and `sheet_fit` (J9, ADR 0191).
- **The task names a recipe, and the recipe was retired.** `Shape:` ends with "a recorded prototype
  a recipe instantiates". ADR 0192 defined `carcass.box`/`wardrobe.hinged` and `product.build`;
  ADR 0215 supersedes it — "a construction is composed, never written. There is no `carcass.box`,
  no `product.build` and no wardrobe function" — and the tree carries no recipe, no `product.build`
  and no prototype instance (`rg` over `crates/` finds none).
- **The prototype mechanism is unaccepted.** `docs/domain/prototypes.md` opens as "discussion
  draft — not accepted, nothing implemented, no task filed", Q2 is the task that records it, and
  §11 still holds four owner decisions (formula surface, the split's threshold, the naming of the
  split's children, whether a fit-out's minimum width may steer it). §18.2 says the prototype
  format "waits for the formula surface" and that the workshop standards file is "blocked by a
  decision" whose home it gives as `plan/questions.md` — no such entry is there today.
- **A card that names materials has none to name yet.** `crates/catalog` holds the item envelope,
  the registries and the starter rows; a material is still a catalogue row with a thickness, not a
  library record. Y8 (one portable materials library) and O16 (a record refers to a library
  material) are the first two entries of `## Now`, in the render and catalog streams, and neither
  has started.
- `crates/ui/src/tool_card.rs` is the parameter card of one tool, and `crates/ui/src/panel_card.rs`
  the inspector card of one panel: neither is a skill card, and nothing in the tree uses the word.

## What this means

The task's `Done:` clause joins two halves that do not have the same status. Filling the profile
from the workspace settings is ready and grounded: a settings section, a persisted value, one call
to `Session::set_profile`, and the inspector reading the workshop's own numbers. "A build through a
card" is the other half, and it needs three things that do not exist: the prototype record (Q2 and
its four decisions), the materials library a card would name (Y8, O16) and a build path (there is
none by ADR 0215).

## The question for the owner

Which of these is J12?

- **A — the profile is a settings section, and nothing else.** The workshop's numbers become
  workspace state: a *Workshop* section in `ui::settings` over `WorkshopProfile`, persisted in
  `settings.json`, handed to the session once at start and on every change (`Session::set_profile`,
  no revision, no undo). Seeded from the standards the build reads — the starter catalogue's sheet
  and the record's defaults — and every field clamped to a range a person can reach. The `Done:`
  clause becomes: a test sets an 18 mm board in the settings and `shelf_fit` answers with it.
  Cost: `crates/ui/src/settings.rs`, `app/src/settings_store.rs`, `app/src/main.rs`,
  `app/src/scene_build.rs`, `crates/text/src/messages.rs`, `SETTINGS_VERSION` bumped. Ready today.
- **B — A plus a skill card as a settings record.** One record — the materials a shop builds with,
  the methods and the gaps — stored beside the profile, versioned, validated on read, never in the
  document. The card is a section of the same file, drawn from one value. Cost: B adds a record
  type in `model` (or a new module) and its validation; the material it names is a catalogue row
  today and a library id after Y8, so the field is a stable id whose reader must survive Y8.
- **C — the whole path the `Shape:` describes** (profile → card → recorded prototype → a build that
  instantiates it). This is Q2's ADR and its four open decisions first, then Y8 and O16, then J12:
  it is not one session and it is not ready. Deferring J12 into `## Next` until those land is the
  honest reading of §18.2.

The default I would take: **B**, with the prototype reference left out of the card until Q2 lands —
a card is workshop settings, and a card that claims to build is a promise the tree cannot keep.

## The owner's answer (2026-11-23)

The workshop profile and the prototype mechanism are not designed and are not needed by the agent
tasks now: do not look at them and do not tie anything to them. So this entry leaves the order until
the prototype record exists (Q2 and its four decisions), and its `Shape:` must be rewritten then —
"a card becomes a recorded prototype" is the half that has nothing to stand on today. Nothing in
this note is a decision about that mechanism; it is what a session measured when it asked.
