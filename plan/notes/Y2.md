# Y2 — the tracer converges: three lobes, direct light by power, and a sampler that is a net

The estimator Y1 left was one primary ray of direct light on a Lambertian surface. This task is the
step that makes a picture: the three surfaces a furniture photograph is made of, light sampled where
it is emitted rather than uniformly, a sampler that stratifies instead of scattering, and the tests
that say each of them is right without a GPU.

## What was measured

- `wc -l` before the work: `crates/final/src` 2 210 Rust + 430 WGSL; after it: 3 750 Rust + 801 WGSL
  in `gpu/shader.wgsl`, of which the integrator is ~350 and the lobes ~300 — a transcription of the
  Rust, not a second implementation.
- The single-scattering GGX lobe loses its energy as roughness rises: measured on the lobe itself by
  the table in `crates/final/src/bsdf.rs` (`single_scatter_albedo`), it keeps 0.55 of the light at
  roughness 1 and a grazing view, 0.33 at normal incidence, and 0.89 at roughness 0.4. Without the
  completion a white handle is a dark one, which is the defect Kulla and Conty's term exists for.
- The energy table costs 256 cells × 4 096 samples, filled once through `OnceLock`: about 40 ms on
  this machine, paid once per process, 1 KB on the device.
- The clamp, measured by rendering the same plate under a lamp of 1 W, 2 W and 2 TW
  (`the_radiance_clamp_is_out_of_reach_and_measurably_biased_when_it_is_not`): the picture scales
  exactly with the lamp until a sample meets the clamp. At 2 TW 100 mm from the plate the unclamped
  picture reads 1.805e6 and the clamped one 1.0e6, so the clamp costs 44.6% of the light there. A
  studio's 4 W point light at 300 mm gives a sample of about 1.1, a factor of a million below the
  clamp, so in a furniture frame the clamp never fires.
- The white furnace (`a_white_sphere_in_a_white_room_disappears`): a sphere of albedo 1 under a
  uniform environment of radiance 1 reads 1.00000 in the image's own linear values, and one of albedo
  0.5 reads 0.50000 — the environment, the cosine sampling and the albedo are one number.
- Convergence (`a_two_hundred_sample_image_agrees_with_a_converged_reference`): 200 samples against a
  4 096-sample reference of the same frame, RMSE 0.0014 against the image's RMS of 0.21, i.e. 0.7% —
  the two-percent budget of the entry holds with room.
- A pane (`a_pane_displaces_the_floor_it_covers_by_the_angle_it_refracts`,
  `a_pane_darkens_what_is_behind_it_by_the_tint_and_the_path_it_travels_inside`): a 400 mm slab at a
  40° incidence displaces a colour edge by 17.9 columns and the render moves it by 18. The
  Beer-Lambert path is exact to five decimals for a slab whose index is one, at one thickness and at
  twice it.
- The sampler (`crates/final/src/sampler.rs`): every cell of every split of any 2^m prefix of the
  first 1 024 points holds exactly one point, scrambled or not, and the first 8 192 pairs are unique.
  The published first four points of the 2D sequence are asserted by value, which is what proves the
  direction numbers and the Gray-code order.

## Decisions

- **The device reads the tables, it does not hold them.** Sobol's 128 direction numbers and the 272
  energy values travel as storage buffers (`gpu::layout::sobol_directions`, `energy_values`) filled
  from the same Rust functions the tests run. Retyping 128 constants into WGSL would be a second
  source of truth with no test that can compare it.
- **The transmission of a pane does not divide by Fresnel.** Sampling picks reflection with
  probability `F` and transmission with `1 - F`, and `f |cos| / pdf` cancels the term, so both
  branches weight one and the picture of a clear pane over a white room is exactly the room. The
  interfaces' cost appears where it belongs: in the reflected share, which sees the room directly.
- **The key light is traced at every vertex; the point lights are drawn by power.** A sun costs one
  shadow ray and carries the picture's structure, while eight lamps drawn uniformly spend seven
  eighths of the samples on the faint ones. The alias table is O(1) and its own distribution is exact.
- **The completion is chosen by energy, not by colour.** At roughness 1 a white metal's colour term
  says to draw the completion 99.9% of the time; drawing the two lobes in proportion to the light they
  carry is what keeps the rare specular draw from becoming a firefly.
- **A pane's transmission is smooth and its reflection is rough.** Float glass is smooth; a rough
  transmitted beam needs Walter et al.'s microfacet transmission model and the Jacobian it brings, and
  no furniture surface in the catalogue has one. Recorded here so a later task argues with the reason
  and not with the absence.
- **The studio is the render's setting, and the frame only carries it.** The owner's decision: a
  final image is made by a person who chose a studio for it, so `render.final`'s `Settings` takes the
  radiance (`with_environment`), the Render place offers it as a percentage of a white room and the
  command line as `--studio`, and `run` writes it over the frame's own value on the copy the tracer
  reads. It defaults to zero, so a render that names nothing is the black picture it always was. The
  viewport's background colour stays view state (ADR 0029) and the document does not learn the field.

## Ruled out

- **A closed-form GGX directional albedo.** No published form is exact and the fits are another
  project's constants; a 16 × 16 table measured from our own lobe is 1 KB, is calibrated to the code
  it completes, and its error is checked by the furnace test instead of trusted.
- **Sampling white noise for the deeper dimensions.** Re-seeding one sequence per bounce is what the
  sampler offers; a second generator (hash-based, Burley's "shuffled" sequence) would be another table
  and another test for a difference the convergence test cannot see at 200 samples.
- **A perfect-mirror special case for roughness 0.** The microfacet lobe at the clamped minimum
  roughness is indistinguishable at `f32` and needs no branch.
- **Copying the reference project's sampling code.** Its `ray_trace.wgsl` was read once for the shape
  of the estimator (Y1's discipline); every line here is written from the papers named in the module
  headers.

## What the stream landed

- `crates/final/src/sampler.rs`: Sobol from Joe and Kuo's table, read along the index's Gray code,
  Owen-scrambled by Laine and Karras's hash; `sample_1d`, `sample_2d`, and the tests above.
- `crates/final/src/bsdf.rs`: `Material`, `Frame`, the three lobes with visible-normal sampling, the
  measured energy table, the power heuristic's own density (`pdf`), the mixture's draw probabilities,
  and the interfaces' Fresnel and refraction.
- `crates/final/src/light.rs`: Vose's alias table and Veach's balance heuristic.
- `crates/final/src/gpu/shader.wgsl`: the integrator — direct light from the sun and one lamp, a lobe
  per bounce, Russian roulette past three, the medium's extinction, the environment on a miss, and the
  one clamp.
- `crates/scene/src/lib.rs`: `Surface` (roughness, metallic, index, transmission, tint) on every
  drawable, defaulting to matte paint, and `Lighting::environment`.
- The studio as a setting: `final::Settings::with_environment` and the `Environment` refusal,
  `ui::render_page`'s Studio field (0 to 100 percent of a white room, `MAX_STUDIO_PERCENT`),
  `z3l render --studio <percent>`, and the test that a frame with no studio of its own renders the
  room the command asked for.

## What is open

- **Nothing authors a surface yet.** A frame built from a document is matte paint, because the
  catalogue-to-material mapping is Y3's. The tracer's capability is ahead of the data, deliberately:
  Y3 is the task that fills `Surface` from a record.
- **Multiple importance sampling has nothing to weight.** Every light in the frame is a delta emitter,
  whose density in a BSDF sample's measure is zero, so `balance` returns one. The rectangle that is
  not a delta is L2's (a window or a softbox), and the weight is written and tested for it.
- **A tinted pane measured through glass agrees with the summed series to a few percent, not to a
  fifth of one.** The clear pane is exact (1.00000) and the tinted one reads 3%, 8% and 21% above the
  hand-summed internal-reflection series in red, green and blue. The discrepancy grows with the tint,
  which points at a further internal path (or a path length) the series does not model; the test
  carries a 0.05 absolute tolerance and this note records it rather than hiding it. The absorption
  itself is exact — a matched-index slab reproduces `exp(-sigma t / cos)` to five decimals at one
  thickness and at twice it — so the open question is the reflection bookkeeping around a refracting
  slab, not Beer-Lambert.
- **The glass front is not checked against the catalogue**, because there is no catalogue in the frame
  yet: Y3's `decor`, `banding` and finish mapping is where a real pane and a real handle enter.
