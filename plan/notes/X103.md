# X103 — a stored ring is closed for the document and open for the geometry layer

## What was measured

- `crates/geom/src/boolean.rs:41` — `VERTEX_TOLERANCE = 1e-6` mm: the arrangement welds the start
  and end of every edge at a nanometre, and `Contour::new_with_tolerance` of a result ring uses
  `RESULT_JOINT_TOLERANCE = 1e-4` mm. Both are three orders below the document's rule.
- `crates/model/src/contour.rs` — `joint_tolerance_mm` is `2 µm + arc_slack(first) + arc_slack(next)`,
  `arc_slack = 2 µm + radius × 1.746e-5` (ADR 0072). On the owner's `cross.z3l` (schema 31) the two
  rings of "Пересечение 1" miss their arc joints by 4.63, 6.68, 10.07 and 12.44 µm — measured from
  the stored wire form, inside the 25 µm and 17 µm the two radii allow.
- `Contour::to_geometry` built the ring with `new_with_joint_tolerance` and handed it on with its
  gaps intact, so the arrangement saw an open chain: `contour.boolean.union` answered "nothing to
  create: the rings do not meet, so nothing is left" and `object.area.extrude` found no area at all.
  Without the fix the new engine test reproduces exactly that refusal.
- The arrangement repairs a *single* gap by itself: a chord's supporting line crosses the arc's
  circle, both are split there and the walk closes. Two gapped rings built from one arc and a chord
  therefore union correctly with or without the fix, which is why the first test written for this
  task passed both ways and was replaced by the invariant test below. The owner's rings each carry
  two gapped joints and their side lines cross the circle outside the arc's span, so nothing
  bridges them.

## Decisions

- The weld lives in the conversion, not in the boolean: `geom` keeps its nanometre and its exact
  boundary, and `Contour::to_geometry` closes every joint the document's rule accepted, with
  `geom::Contour::welded` (a line endpoint takes the arc's derived end; two arcs shorten the later
  one's start). One place closes every stored ring for the boolean, the area partition, a
  half-plane cut and the mesh, and a ring the rule cannot close is handed on as it came, so nothing
  that loaded before stops loading. ADR 0072 records it; ADR 0203 already does the same for a
  frame's ring at the reader.
- The fixture is `model::testing::cross_rings`, re-exported by `testkit`: the two rings as the file
  stored them, so the regression test is the owner's own geometry rather than a ring invented to
  fit the bug.

## Ruled out

- Welding at the operand's own tolerance inside `collect_edges`/`VertexSet`: the tolerance would have
  to travel through `union_regions`, `intersect_regions`, `subtract_regions` and `partition_regions`
  for every operand, the arrangement would then fuse vertices that are genuinely distinct within
  that tolerance, and the result rings would inherit a looser weld than the layer's own contract.
- Closing the joint in `ContourSegment::to_geom`: a segment does not know its neighbour, and the
  conversion of one segment must stay a pure value-to-value map.
