# V5 — a sphere is drawn around its centre

## What was measured

- `crates/model/src/primitive.rs` stored one shape: `Primitive::Sphere { radius, segments, bands }`,
  and `Primitive::from_kind(kind, size)` turned one typed extent into its diameter. The box and the
  cylinder left with schema 25 and ADR 0138; the sphere was the last cell placed by a bare click.
- The cell was `ToolKind::Primitive(PrimitiveKind::Sphere)` with `state.primitive_size()` as its
  number, `Editor::arm_primitive`/`place_primitive` as its path and
  `app/src/document.rs`'s `placing` branch as its click; `DrawTool` had no sphere arm and the stage
  table (`draw_fields::stage`) knew nothing about one.
- The sketch already had every round the sphere needs: `DrawTool::Circle` (centre and rim, `R`
  plate), `CircleDiameter` (`Ø` plate) and `CircleThreePoints` (circumcircle), and
  `Path::circle_*` validate before storing. `Path::circle_center_radius` did not exist, so a caller
  could not read the pair back out of the chain.
- `Path::creation_origin` of a closed circle is the centre of its bounds, which for four quarter
  arcs is exactly the centre it was built from — the primitive's own local zero.
- The draw gizmo paints its preview on the work plane only (`paint_path`), so a ball needed its own
  paint: there is no node in the document while the round is being drawn, and `Session`'s preview
  overlay needs a node id.
- The drawing tool snaps to draft ends, the 10 mm grid and the axes (`SnapRequest`); it never
  ray-casts a furniture surface, so no face-plane tangency snap exists in the gesture today. The
  ruler's `SnapTarget::Surface` is the shell's one surface probe and belongs to another tool.

## Decisions

- **The sphere cell carries three rounds, as the cylinder cell carries three circles.** The methods
  are `DrawTool::Sphere`, `SphereDiameter`, `SphereThreePoints`; the stage table arms them at the
  circle shapes (`Radius`, `Diameter`, `Segment`), so the plates, the guides, the previews and the
  polyline machinery come for free. A `Sphere` draft builds a `Path::circle_through`, so the figure
  the tool previews *is* the equator the ball is built from.
- **`DrawIntent::Primitive(PrimitiveKind)` is the commit.** The drawn circle is never stored:
  `commit_primitive` reads `(centre, radius)` back with `Path::circle_center_radius`, composes the
  centre onto the plane's frame (`Placement::compose` with a local translation), builds
  `Primitive::sphere(radius, DEFAULT_SEGMENTS, DEFAULT_BANDS)` and dispatches one
  `object.primitive.create`. No edit scope: nothing enters the document before the second or third
  click, so one command is one undo entry by construction.
- **The phantom is a wireframe ball of three great circles.** The draft's preview is the equator;
  `phantom_rings` adds two meridians through the plane's normal, each sample exactly `radius` from
  the centre, painted in a translucent stroke. A filled sphere would need a translucent solid the
  egui overlay cannot draw, and a wireframe is what the box and the fill ghost already use.
- **`Path::circle_center_radius` is model API, not ui arithmetic.** The radius is the number the
  circle was built from, not a measurement of its bounds; reading it back also keeps the sphere's
  radius and the drawn circle identical to the micrometre.
- **`ToolKind::Primitive { kind, tool }` names the shape and the method.** The ribbon never derives
  one from the other, `is_armed` compares the shape, and `ToolAction::DrawPrimitive` hands the
  editor a tool. `Toolbars::primitive_size` and `DEFAULT_PRIMITIVE_SIZE_MM` retire: the plates carry
  the number now, so the ribbon keeps two typed defaults, not three.

## Ruled out

- **Keeping the one-click arm and adding the plates only.** It is smaller, but the owner asked for a
  step-by-step build and the sphere would stay the one shape whose size is typed before it exists.
- **A fourth `DrawIntent` variant per primitive kind.** The kind is the payload; a cone will need a
  profile *and* a pulled height, which is the body's shape, not this one.
- **Storing the drawn circle beside the sphere.** Two representations of one figure, a second
  history entry and a card that must choose which one it edits: the trap ADR 0101 removed.
- **Committing at `object.extrude`-like scope.** A scope is for a gesture whose parts must be one
  entry; a single command already is.
- **A surface/tangency snap inside the sphere method.** The method takes points and never asks where
  they came from, which is the requirement; the snap itself is the sketch's grammar and does not
  exist yet (`plan/debt.md`, `[gizmo]`).

## Looked at

- `crates/ui/src/draw_tests.rs` asserts the radius each method produces from a known set: the centre
  and rim give 40 mm, the same pair as a diameter gives 40 mm, and the triangle (0,0), (100,0),
  (50,50) gives the circumradius of 50 mm about (50,0).
- `app/src/frame_tests.rs` drives all three methods headlessly: one command, the ball's radius from
  the bounds, the centre as the node's origin on the work plane, and one undo taking the whole ball.
- `app/src/draw_gizmo.rs` asserts every phantom sample stands the drawn radius from the centre and
  that the meridians leave the plane.
- `app/src/frame_tests.rs` reads every stage of the three rounds through the plate-layout test, so
  the sphere's plates are held to the same clearance rule as the sketch's.
