# X55 — the sphere's bands are spaced by latitude, not by height

## What was measured

- `sphere_mesh` in `crates/mesh/src/primitive.rs` stepped each latitude band by a constant
  `z = -r + 2 r band / bands`. At the default 32 x 16 the first ring therefore had radius
  `sqrt(r^2 - (r - 2r/16)^2) = 0.484 r`, so the pole was joined to a ring half the radius wide by
  one long triangle per side, and the edge pass drew that fan as a 32-point star.
- Two offscreen shots of one default sphere, before and after the change, showed the star on the
  top pole and then a cap band no wider than its neighbour. Both were taken at the default 32 x 16
  on a 50 mm ball through `app/src/ui_shots.rs`'s paint path — the live window was not used,
  `AGENTS.md` §1.3's silent check instead of a live run. The probe test and its pictures were
  scratch and are not kept.
- The counts were never wrong: `face_count` is `segments x bands`, `vertex_count` is
  `2 + segments x (bands - 1)`, and both are untouched by the change.

## Decisions

- **Equal steps of latitude.** `z = -r cos(pi band / bands)` and `ring = r sin(pi band / bands)`, so
  every band covers one meridian step and the pole cap is one band: 0.195 r at 16 bands. The stored
  `segments` and `bands` keep their meaning, so the card, the schema and the existing count test are
  unchanged.
- **Band 1 stays the bottom ring.** The sign of `z` is negative, not positive, so the ring order
  still runs from the bottom pole up and the two cap faces keep their winding; with `z = +r cos(...)`
  the first cap came out inverted and `assert_outward` failed on face 16.
- **The test measures the meridian section, not `z`.** The chord between neighbouring rings is
  `2 r sin(pi / (2 bands))` for every band; the `z` difference is not equal under this spacing
  (the first band is 0.019 r high and the middle one 0.195 r), so a `z`-span assertion would encode
  the old defect.

## Ruled out

- **Adding rings near the poles only.** It would change `vertex_count` and `face_count`, which the
  analytic balance, the scene tree and the card all promise.
- **Keeping the `z` spacing and smoothing the edge pass.** The soft-looking caps would still be a
  fan of 32 long triangles; the tessellation, not the pen, was wrong.
- **Re-deriving the ring from the `z` step with `.max(0.0)`.** The guard existed because the old
  formula could land outside the sphere at the rounding edge; the trigonometric ring cannot.
