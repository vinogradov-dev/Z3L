# N23 — the empty-space ghost passes the board it meets and holds at the frame envelope

## What was measured

- `app/src/document.rs::update_fill` read the free place off the point under the pointer, and
  `crates/model/src/project/fill.rs::board` clamped it into the cell (`follow`). A headless sweep of
  the default camera (600 × 560 × 720 frame, a shelf at 350, the pointer up a screen line through the
  piece, 24 steps of ~34 points) read the ghost's place as 271, 296, 319, 332, 350, **368 × 6**, 509,
  527, 548: the part held the shelf's top face for 206 points of travel and then jumped 141 mm.
- The cause is the face, not the clamp: `ui::pointer_point` rests on the face the ray meets, and a
  face square to the free axis names one coordinate for the whole face. A projection onto any fixed
  line has the same problem from the other side — its anchor's depth moves the reading by the frame's
  depth times the camera's tangent (about 250 mm between the middle and the far wall here).
- The clamp alone is the second half: a place the hand reaches beyond a board came back to that
  board's face, and a pointer outside the product dropped the ghost (`FillGizmo::refuse`) instead of
  holding it.

## Decisions

- `crates/ui/src/void.rs::free_place` answers both readings with `FreePlace`: `point` (what the face
  says), `hand` (the ray's closest place on the cell's own free axis, `Ray::
  closest_parameter_on_line`, ADR 0153's rule), `face` (its normal and offset) and `square`.
  `pointer_face` is the cursor rule with the face, and `pointer_point` is that without it.
- A face within 2.5° of square to the axis (`SQUARE_FACE`) starts a **carriage** in the shell
  (`Document::fill_carriage`, `Carriage`): the place that face named, moved by the hand's travel since.
  A new face, plane or cell starts a new carriage; a face along the axis, a ray across the whole void,
  or no point at all ends it.
- The plan keeps its clamp, so a part is never placed through a board and the frame's envelope is
  what stops it. `fill_place` answers the place, `FillRequest::free` carries it as one number, and the
  model rounds it to whole millimetres (`follow`, one path for a typed number and a hand).
- A pointer that names no cell passes the carriage's own place on, so the ghost and its card stay the
  part the click writes (ADR 0104).
- ADR 0157 records the rule; `docs/domain/products.md` §7 states it in prose.

## Ruled out

- The hand's place alone, everywhere: measured at 20, 74, 125 where the pointer was on the frame's near
  wall at 271, 296, 319 — the 250 mm projection offset would move the ghost to the floor under a
  pointer aimed at the middle of the wall.
- Re-planning into the cell the hand's place falls in (the text of the entry's first shape): with the
  same offset the ghost dives under a shelf while the pointer is on its top face, and the frame is
  what a maker reads the part against.
- A screen-space scale for the hand (the parallax rate): the rate is per face and per camera, and a
  typed distance is already the exact way to place a part.
- Reading the free place from the point plus its own hand reading (`point + (hand(now) -
  hand(point))`): the point is under the pointer, so the difference is zero and the ghost freezes.

## Verification

`void::tests::a_face_square_to_the_axis_cannot_read_the_hand_but_a_face_along_it_can` (a shelf's top
is square, a wall behind it is not, a ray across the void rests on no face, a miss answers `None`, no
fourth axis); `frame_tests::the_fill_ghost_passes_the_board_it_meets_and_holds_at_the_frame_envelope`
(the ghost under the shelf, over its top face it jumps into the cell above and then moves 50+ mm with
the hand, a pointer in an empty corner of the scene keeps the ghost, the click writes exactly it and
one undo removes it). The same sweep after the change reads 271, 296, 319, 332, 350, 368, 403, 436,
468, 498, 528, 556, 583, 520, 534, 548: no freeze. The rate and the step at 583 → 520 were not driven
by hand in a live window; the numbers are the headless frame's.
