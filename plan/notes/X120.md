# X120 — the switch kept its height, the wheel could not bring the camera back

## What was measured

- `Camera::toggle_projection` (`crates/scene/src/camera.rs`) computed
  `height / (2 · tan(fov/2))` and then clamped it into `MIN_DISTANCE_MM..=MAX_DISTANCE_MM`
  (10 mm .. 1 000 000 mm) without a word, so a height no perspective distance shows came back as
  another frame. Above the cap the answer is `2 · MAX · tan(22.5°)` = 828 427.12 mm of visible
  height.
- The wheel's step was `exp(-scroll · 0.004)` (`app/src/document.rs`), and `egui`'s line scroll is
  40 points (`InputOptions::line_scroll_speed`, egui 0.37), so one notch took 15 % of the distance.
  From the cap, `ln(828427 / 3125) / 0.16` = 35 notches brought the model back: that is the
  "движется по микрометрам" the report names.
- A navigable view cannot *reach* a height no distance shows: `set_orthographic` and `zoom` both
  derive the height from a clamped distance, so a viewport-held height stays inside
  `[8.28, 828 427]` mm and its switch is exact. The clamp is therefore a guard on the type's range
  (a projection written by `Camera::with_projection`), not a state the wheel walks into — but the
  camera at the cap was reachable, and a kilometre away is what the owner saw.
- Both `Camera::new` and the far plane use `MAX_DISTANCE_MM`, so the cap cannot simply grow: the
  model at the target would sit on the far plane.

## Decisions

- `ProjectionToggle { Kept, Pinned, NoDistance { height } }` is the switch's answer, marked
  `#[must_use]`: a caller that drops it is the bug the report describes. A refusal moves nothing.
- A distance within a billionth of a limit is the limit. The cap's height is a multiplication away
  from the cap's distance, and the round trip is off by a rounding step: without the slack a view
  wound out to the cap refused to switch (found by `a_view_at_the_distance_cap_switches_and_keeps_its_frame`).
- The document repairs a refusal with `frame_all` and a second switch, and says so in the bar
  (`Message::StatusRefitted`). The fit is the frame every projection can show, so the second switch
  is always `Kept`; `frame_all` reports its own failure, so the path is never silent.
- `Camera::visible_height` is the one number both projections agree on, and the wheel's unit.
- The wheel's step is `max(near, far)` — the fine share, or half of the gap per notch — capped by
  the gap when coming in from outside the frame. `Document::fit_height` reads `home_camera` over
  the cached bounds, which is arithmetic and no evaluation, so a wheel frame asks it per step.
- The step is measured on `visible_height`, not on `distance`: an orthographic view's distance is
  its pivot depth, and only its height says how much of the scene is on screen.

## What the tests had to relearn

- `zoom_active_view` (the test/shot helper) writes the leaf camera and not `scene.camera`, the
  frame's snapshot, which refreshes on the next frame or on `frame_all`: a document test reads
  `layout().active_camera()` after it.
- A frame test turns the wheel by whole notches (`WHEEL_NOTCH_POINTS`) and runs twelve frames per
  notch, because `egui` spreads one event over the frames after it; without that the first notch
  counts as spent and the count reads one too high.
- `Leaf::set_camera` is a `#[cfg(test)]` setter, because no gesture can put a navigable view in the
  refused state. The document test uses it; nothing shipped calls it.

## Ruled out

- Widening the perspective view's field of view to keep a wide height: a 2.2 m height at the cap
  needs 95°, and the default 45° is part of what a perspective switch means (ADR 0049).
- Letting the distance grow past `MAX_DISTANCE_MM`: the far plane is the same constant, so the
  model would be clipped away at exactly the distance that was supposed to show it.
- Keeping the clamp and only fixing the wheel: the switch would still show another frame than the
  one it promised, and `Done` forbids a switch that leaves the camera clamped.
- A fixed larger wheel step (say 40 % per notch instead of 15 %): it would make the close work
  coarse to speed up the far case, which is the one the maker is not doing.
- A step proportional to `distance / radius` in the exponent without the gap cap: a fling could
  throw the view through the model, and the far share stalls at a fixed point instead of landing.
