# X80 — what the 13 MB log said, and the three fixes

what the 13 MB log said, and the three fixes

## What the log showed

- 3852 carry lines, 135 with `hand=none`, 1157 with `jump=true`, and two drops with
  `delta=(0,0,0)` after seconds of carrying.
- `asked=(591.215,118.633,0)` vs `delta=(0,0,0)` with a distance field showing 0 mm: `Distances::place`
  pinned the face at the wall on a typed `0`, and a typed number outranks the hand (0103). The field's
  own convention is that zero releases (0092, 0126); the carry did not follow it.
- `hand=none`: the hand-to-pivot offset was cleared by a camera gesture or by the pointer leaving the
  work area, and the plane branch only read an offset it already had, so it never recovered.
- `jump=true` with a typed number in force: the jump hold was skipped, so the pending request stayed
  and would fire on release.
- 13.6 MB in 70 s, almost all of it `tracing::span: winit::Window::…` at debug.

## Fixed

- `Distances::place(side, 0.0)` unpins the side and drops its lock; the pointer takes the face back.
- The plane branch computes the offset from the piece's pivot and the hand when it is missing.
- A typed number clears `drag.jump`.
- The sink passes a non-`z3l` target only at `warn` or worse.
- The carry line gained `typed`, `held`, `offset` and the hold's age, so the next report reads without
  a rebuild: `typed=Some(0.0)` is a pin, `offset=false` is a lost bond, `jump=Some(0.08)` is a hold in
  progress.

## Numbers to remember

- One frame of a carry is ~9–10 lines per 0.1 s at debug; a session is now kilobytes.
- The jump hold fires on a crossing only; a `jump=Some(age)` that keeps resetting to 0 is a changed
  request, not a stuck hold.
