# N20 — What was measured

## What was measured

- `crates/model/src/class.rs:472` — `Mount` is `Floor | Wall` and lives in the packed trait set; the
  already-shipped consumers are the furniture move tool (`move_query.rs:282`) and the export row
  (`export/src/rows.rs:810`), and both read it through `TraitSet::mount()`.
- `ClassCode::GENERAL_PRODUCT` (`70.01`) is a `level = Product` row with `Mount::Floor`, so the card's
  placeholder already answers the question; `10.01.02` is `Mount::Wall`; a fitting such as `50.02`
  carries `None`.
- The card drew only `Class <name>`, the search field and the class tree — nothing said which class
  stands and which hangs, which is why a floor cabinet refuses to rise and a wall cabinet does not.

## Decisions

- The words live on `Mount::label()`, beside `ClassRow::label()`, rather than in the card: the trait
  is the single source of truth and the mapping is testable without a window.
- The row sits under the `Class` line, dim, and is absent when the trait is absent — a guessed
  position would be the second source of truth ADR 0097 refused.
- `docs/domain/classes.md` gained the card as a second reader of `mount` in both consumer tables.

## Ruled out

- A `Position` field on the product card: a stored position contradicts the class the maker picks.
- Filtering the class list by position: the class is the only stored fact, and a filter would hide
  the very rows a maker is looking for.
- A height-above-floor number in the same row: that is the maker's placement (ADR 0100), not a trait.
