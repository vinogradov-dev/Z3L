# X39 — What was measured

## What was measured

- `apps/furnicraft/src/side_panels.rs:287` `scroll_body` was one line, `ScrollArea::both().show(ui, body)`,
  and `apps/furnicraft/src/main.rs:588` gave the tree its own `ScrollArea::vertical()` inside the same
  panel frame — two routes and two sets of margins for one surface.
- `egui` 0.37's default `ScrollStyle` is `floating()` (`egui/src/style.rs:626`): `bar_width = 10`,
  `floating_width = 2`, `bar_outer_margin = 0`, `floating_allocated_width = 0`. With `floating: true`
  the scroll area's inner rect equals its outer one (`egui/src/containers/scroll_area.rs:389`), so the
  bar is painted *over* the content instead of allocating a strip: the bar's cross band is the last
  `max_cross - width ..= max_cross` of the outer rect (`scroll_area.rs:1477`). A body that ends flush
  with the viewport therefore ends under the bar — the inspector's last row, which is why the bottom
  read as cut off.
- `furnicraft_ui::theme::apply` never touches `style.spacing.scroll`, so the panel inherits those
  numbers; the theme's `STEP` is 4.0 pt (`crates/ui/src/theme.rs:75`).
- `ScrollArea::content_margin` (`scroll_area.rs:701`) wraps the content in a margin frame inside the
  viewport, so a reserved strip is empty content the bar may cover, not content the user loses.
- The panel frame already keeps `theme::STEP` on all four sides (`side_panels.rs:333`), so the body
  needed no extra side inset; only the bottom was short by the bar.

## Decisions

- `scroll_body` sets `auto_shrink([false, false])` and a content margin of zero on three sides and
  `STEP + bar_width` at the bottom. Shrinking off keeps the body at the panel's inner width (U73's
  rule), and the reserved strip is `STEP` of air plus the bar at its widest, so a hovered bar cannot
  clip the row it just revealed.
- The bar height is read from the live style (`scroll_bar_height(ui) = spacing.scroll.bar_width`)
  instead of a constant, so a theme that ever changes its scroll style cannot leave a stale number;
  the resting bar is 2 pt but the reserved strip holds the expanded 10 pt.
- The tree now scrolls through the same helper: one route, one inset, and the tree gains the same
  horizontal route as the card. Its long names are already truncated by the tree itself, so no row
  starts overflowing sideways.
- `test_support::drawn_text_rects` was added beside `drawn_text`, because the regression is a
  rectangle: a headless test reads where a row landed, not only what it said.
- The shot is `#[ignore]`d and needs an adapter, like the other interface shots; the headless tests
  are the gate, the pictures are the eyes-only check the checklist asks for.

## Ruled out

- A non-floating scroll style for the panels: it would allocate the bar for the tree too, take 10 pt
  from every body even when nothing scrolls, and change the chrome of both panels.
- Reserving the space with `ui.add_space` after the body: the body is arbitrary code (the card sets
  its own height), so the helper cannot know where "after" is; the margin belongs to the scroller.
- Raising the panel's bottom edge or the frame's bottom margin: the panel rectangle is a contract the
  pointer and the orientation widget read (`Chrome::covers`, `panel_rect`), and the air has to be
  inside the body, not outside the panel.
- A fixed constant for the bar height: it is style data; a second copy in `theme.rs` would drift
  silently.
