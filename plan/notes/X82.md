# X82 — the free slot was refused by the staircase

the free slot was refused by the staircase

## What the third session measured

- Board 620x90x100 at (-210,-110,0); standing body 60x400x100 at (-364,-309,0). They overlap on 26 mm
  of Y and all of Z, so a straight X move is honestly stopped at -94.
- The hand asked `(-171, 236, 0)`: forward and cleared in Y. The log's `blocked=none` on the same frame
  says the destination was free; `resolved=(-94, 236)` says the rule refused it anyway.
- Why: `resolve` walked X, then Y, then Z, and asked `free_at` for each single-axis step. X alone was
  inside the standing body's Y band; Y alone was applied; the pair was never asked as one placement.
  `limits`'s own doc said "the whole destination is answered once" and the code did not do it.

## Fixed here

- `resolve` asks `free_at(whole)` first and returns the whole ask with no contacts when it is free.
- Only a refused destination walks the axes, now up to three passes, so an axis cut on the first pass
  is asked again once the axes after it moved the piece (`changed` stops the loop early).
- Test `a_board_beside_a_standing_one_passes_it_where_the_hand_names_free_space` uses the log's own
  boxes: the slot in front (taken), the slot behind (taken), and the in-line move (cut at -94, contact
  = the standing body).

## Ruled out

- The contact predicate was not the problem: `meets` is a face test (swept SAT) and the boxes really do
  overlap on 26 mm. Nothing was needed on the surface side.
- The jump hold and the typed number were already right (X80); `jump=None`, `typed=None`, `offset=true`
  across the whole drag.
