# N28 — a wall fills the niche's width and height

what a session measured about the third fill pair, and why the tool needed no geometry of its own

## What was measured

- The fill trait carried exactly two pairs (`crates/model/src/class.rs`, `Fill::bits`): `1`
  height-and-depth for `10.10` and `2` width-and-depth for `10.11`, packed into two bits of
  `TraitSet` at `FILL_SHIFT = 12`. The third value `3` was free, so no field widened and no schema
  moved.
- `10.12` back panel carried `fill: None`, and `docs/domain/classes.md` §10 said «no void fill» —
  the class table, not the toolbar, was what made a frontal board impossible. A ribbon cell bound to
  a row without the trait is disabled by `crates/ui/src/toolbars/ready.rs` (`fills`).
- The wall stance was already decided: ADR 0105's table has `+X` up, `+Y` across the front, `+Z`
  (the material) towards `+Y`, face `A` at the maker, vertex 1 at minimum `x` and `z`. Nothing had
  created it, so the fill plan's `Stance` was the only thing the model lacked.
- `board()` reads only `stance.{u,v,t}` and the `against_y`/`against_z` flags: the section is
  `niche.cut(stance.t, slab, (stance.u, stance.v))`, the anchor lands on `u` and `v`, and the free
  position on `t`. With the wall's stance the whole Fit/Shape path — the classifier, the reference
  face, the end bevels, the trim against hidden bodies — runs unchanged.
- `FillGhost` (`app/src/fill_gizmo.rs`) is stance-agnostic too: the wireframe comes from the plan's
  rings, and the callouts from `size_mm` and the placement's own axes. The free-axis marker reads
  `model::free_axis(fill)` and compares the two ends, which is why the wall needed only
  `free_axis = 1`.
- `fill_place` and `Carriage` in `app/src/document.rs` take the axis as a parameter, so a fill whose
  free axis is the depth is carried like any other.

## Decisions

- `Fill::WidthAndHeight` is the third value; `10.12` carries it and answers to «стенка», «задняя
  стенка», `wall` and `back` (ADR 0200). Mounting (overlay/inset) is a property of a mounted front
  and is not the tool's question.
- The stance is ADR 0105's own: `u = 2`, `v = 0`, `t = 1`, no `against` flag, `turn =
  R_z(−90°) · R_y(−90°)`. The wall's length is therefore the niche's **height** and its width the
  niche's width — the stance table's reading, not a fresh one.
- The free axis is the product's depth, and the pointer picks it in whole millimetres exactly as a
  shelf's height is picked: the tool asks nothing new and stores nothing.
- The ribbon cell `furniture.panels.wall` is a third fill cell with the same two builds; the gesture,
  the ghost, the tool card, the distances and `panel.create` are shared.
- The icon mirrors `scene/post.svg` on the vertical axis, because in the one isometry
  (`assets/icons/STYLE_GUIDE.md` §9.1) the wall's frontal face is spanned by `v` and `w` where the
  post's is `u` and `w`; `assets/README.md` gained the row.

## Ruled out

- A fourth stance or a mirrored board: ADR 0105 already fixes the wall as the post turned a quarter
  turn about `+Z`, and a mirror would flip the vertex ids a band order reads.
- A `Wall` tool that creates a panel at the frame's back face without a niche: the owner asked for a
  fill beside the post and the shelf, and a second measurement of the gap is what the niche atom
  exists to prevent (ADR 0118).
- Overlay/inset as a tool parameter: the owner answered it belongs to the mounting rule, and no
  document field for it exists yet.
- A second `Fill` byte or an enum tag in the document: the trait set is derived from the const table,
  so the pair costs two bits that were already reserved.
- Reusing the post glyph for the wall: two tools in one group with one drawing reads as one tool.

## Verification

- Headless: the model's `a_wall_takes_the_voids_width_and_height_in_the_frontal_plane` (both modes,
  the stance axes, the free span) and `a_wall_in_a_splayed_case_takes_the_shaped_board` (Fit keeps
  the rectangle through the whole slab, Shape bevels both ends into the posts' planes); the class
  test proves `10.12`'s pair and the «стенка» alias; the ribbon test proves the cell's two builds,
  the hint «Wall: Largest rectangle» and that no hint asks overlay or inset; a frame test clicks one
  in an empty frame and checks the frontal placement, the class and the single undo entry.
- The icon was rasterised at 96 px beside `scene/post.svg` to read the two boards as one plate turned
  rather than two columns; the coverage and footprint test passes.
