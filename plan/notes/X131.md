# X131 — The rows under the conversation take their height; the transcript takes the rest

The rows under the conversation take their height; the transcript takes the rest

## What was measured

- `app/src/chat.rs` drew the transcript as a `ScrollArea` with `.max_height(TRANSCRIPT_HEIGHT)`
  and `TRANSCRIPT_HEIGHT = 300.0`; `egui::Window` follows its content (`containers/resize.rs`:
  `desired_size = desired_size.max(last_content_size)` in `begin` and in `end`), so the window's
  height was pinned to the rows it drew — 504 points headless on an 840-point screen.
- The headless probe opened the panel at `[16 320]-[436 824]`: dragging the bottom edge up by 120
  changed nothing (the content clamps the size) and dragging it down by 120 pushed the pointer off
  the screen, where `constrain_window_rect_to_area` pulled the window back to the same height. The
  drag reached egui's bottom edge band; the number it produced could not change in either
  direction, which is the owner's report.
- The transcript's 300-point cap was the whole reason: every row of the window was content, so the
  content and the window were the same height.

## Decisions

- The rows under the conversation — findings, checklist, the busy line, the backend sentence, the
  consent card and the field — moved into one `egui::Panel::bottom("assistant_footer")` with
  `Frame::NONE`, shown before the transcript. The panel takes the height its content needs from the
  bottom, so the transcript's `ScrollArea` (`auto_shrink([false, false])`, no `max_height`) gets
  exactly what is left; no reserve is guessed.
- `HEIGHT` is the opening height only. The content is the minimum a shrink stops at, so "a short
  window keeps its minimum" needs no number of ours.
- The order a person reads is unchanged: transcript, findings, checklist, busy line, warnings,
  consent, field.

## Ruled out

- `max_height(ui.available_height() - reserve)` with a guessed reserve: X108 was a guessed width
  (`available - 72`) that asked the window for nine points more every frame in Russian; the sizing
  pass measures the reserve instead of guessing it.
- `.auto_sized(false)` with a forced height: the height would be a constant rather than the drag's.
- A cap that grows with the window (`window height - rows`) needs the rows' height before drawing
  them, which is the same guess in another place.

## Tests

`frame_tests::the_assistant_window_takes_the_height_a_drag_gives_it`: opens the panel with a
fourteen-line answer, drags the bottom edge up 100 points and asserts the window and the last
transcript line moved with it, drags it back down and asserts both returned while the width stayed
as it was.
