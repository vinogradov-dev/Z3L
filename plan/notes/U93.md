## What was measured

`crates/ui/src/object_card.rs` was 1779 lines and `show` drew eight `CollapsibleHeader`s, every one
`default_open = true`, stacked between the placement card (two headers), the pivot card (one) and the
properties card (one). What each geometry kind actually met:

| kind | headers | rows drawn | of which explain a refusal |
|---|---|---|---|
| mesh | 3 | 7 | 0 |
| primitive | 5 | 15 | 2 |
| constructive with a figure | 6 | 17 | 2 |
| constructive with a free ring | 6 | 22 | 2 |
| path | 5 | 16 | 2 |

1. **One condition, three sentences.** With two nodes selected, `mesh_section`, `contour_edit_section`
   and `collapse_section` each printed their own `SelectSingleObject…` sentence — three messages for
   one fact (`SelectSingleObjectMesh`, `SelectSingleObjectContour`, `SelectSingleObjectCollapse`).
2. **One state, two sentences.** While a path's contour was edited in the viewport,
   `FigureEditingInViewport` and `ObjectContourEditingInViewport` both printed, and the entry button
   printed a third refusal beside them.
3. **Two kinds spent a header on refusing.** A primitive and a constructive drew a *Mesh editing*
   header whose whole body was "collapse first"; a path drew one whose whole body was "a path has no
   mesh".
4. **The primary content was fourth.** A primitive's parameters are its source of truth — the card
   said so in prose — yet they sat under *Name*, *Geometry* and *Mesh editing*.
5. **The measurement was written instead of shown.** Four prose sentences explained the card because
   its order did not explain itself.

## Brief, in five lines

- The surface: the inspector card of the selected object, owned by `ObjectCard`, inside the
  floating inspector panel.
- The person's goal: change the thing they selected — a box's sides, a figure's radius, the ring
  behind a body — and know what they are looking at.
- The one primary action: the field of the selected kind; the mode (`Edit mesh` / `Edit contour`) is
  the one button, and `Collapse to mesh…` is the destructive secondary.
- The density: inspector, 76 px fields, one row per block.
- What is removed: six headers, two of the three selection refusals, both viewport-mode duplicates,
  the *Rename* button and the mesh refusal for the two kinds that cannot edit a mesh.

## Options

- **A — trim in place.** Keep the eight headers, delete the refusals and the mesh header for the two
  kinds that cannot use it. Cost: about 40 lines; the parameters still sit below the counts, and a
  person still opens four headers to reach three numbers. No new convention learned.
- **B — one reading order, no headers (chosen).** Head (kind badge + name field + one measure line +
  the source sentence), then only the fields this kind has, then one row of actions, then the live
  mode's detail. Cost: the eight fold keys and eleven messages go, and every kind's row order
  changes once. Learned convention: the properties panel of a single object is a flat list in every
  editor that has a short one; folding is for long lists, and this card stops being long.
- **C — move the numbers to the ribbon's tool card (ADR 0119).** Cost: the card is the one place a
  selection is described, and the pointer would travel to another surface to read it. 0119 puts
  *the armed tool's* parameters at the pointer, not the selected node's, so C is a different
  surface, not a cheaper B.

## Decisions

- The order is identify → measure → edit → act → detail, and no block draws a header (ADR 0116).
- One message for a multi-selection (`ObjectSelectSingle`) and a disabled action row beside it, so
  the refusal has a second cue and one home.
- One status line for the viewport mode (`ObjectContourEditingInViewport`); the figure fields are
  drawn disabled beside it rather than with a second sentence.
- `Collapse to mesh…` keeps its two-step question, but the question takes the action row's place
  instead of growing a header, and it is offered only when no contour edit is running — a collapse
  would make the viewport's working copy stale.
- The name commits on losing focus, like every scrubbed field (ADR 0092); the *Rename* button was a
  second commit path for one value.
- The measure line carries only derived numbers (counts, arcs, the ring's height), never a number the
  card can edit, so no field appears twice on one screen.
- The fold state per node kind that the entry expected is dropped with the headers: nothing in the
  card is long enough to fold, and a fold that hides the only three fields makes the card empty.

## Ruled out

- Keeping the headers for the long tail (a free ring's six-row text editor, a twelve-vertex list):
  both are modes, and a mode's detail already disappears when the mode ends.
- A summary band that repeats the fields (`Width 600 · Height 400` over a `Width` and a `Height`
  box) — the same number twice on one screen, which is the defect this task removes.
- Showing a material in the band: an object has no material in the document; material belongs to a
  panel and to the product, and inventing a field here would break AGENTS §4.9.
- Fixing the panel card's thirteen headers in the same change: it is a bigger surface with its own
  kinds, and it is filed as debt so this decision does not guess at it.
