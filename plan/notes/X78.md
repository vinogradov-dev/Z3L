# X78 — the pause, the zero-gap stop, and the object move's icon

the pause, the zero-gap stop, and the object move's icon

## What was measured

- The flip the owner reported is the destination rule doing exactly what ADR 0166 says: a free place
  beyond an obstacle is taken whole, so as soon as the post fits above the slab it crosses in one
  frame. The pause is therefore a gesture rule, not a document one — the rule stays stateless and the
  carry keeps the request.
- A second defect hid under the first: a post whose top already touched the slab reported **no
  contact at all**. `blocking_faces` skipped every axis whose gap was at or under `CONTACT_MM`, which
  is right for the edge-edge noise of turned boxes and wrong for a face that is already touching and
  being pushed into. A thin gap now counts as a stop at zero when the direction closes it, and as
  nothing when the direction runs along or away from it.
- The icon asset is the approved draft `a-solid-arrow.svg`, adapted to the format: 24 grid, inks
  `#6E9EF7` (model) and `#A98BF0` (modify), 1.5 silhouette and 1.2 inner edges, 0.18/0.12 face tone.
  Its raster passes the ink-footprint and coverage tests of the icon module unchanged.

## Decisions

- `settled_carry(previous, asked, pending, pointer_moved, dt)`: a step over `JUMP_MM` (15 mm) with the
  pointer moved less than `JUMP_POINTS` (6 pt) is a jump; the previous delta holds until the same
  request has been asked for `JUMP_HOLD` (0.12 s). A fast hand, a different request or an abandoned one
  clears the hold. A typed number is never delayed (ADR 0103).
- `assets/icons/transform/object.svg` is the first icon of a `transform` theme; `Theme::Transform`,
  `transform::OBJECT`, `assets/README.md` row, and `icon_image` (a widget image for a control that
  pairs a glyph with a word) are the plumbing. The band draws it at 14 pt beside the object move's
  word; the other three cells stay words, and the narrowest-window test still passes.
- The icon count in `every_icon_uri_names_its_theme_and_file` moved from 116 to 117.

## Not resolved

- The owner's first symptom — a lower vertical block that cannot cross the carcass's right boundary
  while standing behind it — was not reproduced from one front view: a body behind a case and a body
  inside its depth both answer a free destination in the model's own tests. It needs the scene file or
  an axonometric screenshot with the block selected; the likely candidates are the block being a child
  of that product whose box pokes out of the envelope, or the band's 0 mm field pinning a gap.
