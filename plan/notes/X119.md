# X119 — what was measured, and what the fit follows

## Measured before the first edit

The camera was fitted once, in `Document::new` (`home_camera(scene_bounds)`), and `home_camera`
answers `Camera::default()` for an empty scene: target at the document's corner, distance 1.2 m,
direction from the opening preset. Nothing refitted it after a build, and `frame_all` runs only on
`F`, the View tab's cell and the cluster's cell. `Leaf::frame` already fits a leaf on a whole-scene
camera while keeping its direction, its preset and its projection kind (ADR 0049), so the fit
itself needed no new geometry — only a rule for when it runs.

Two things were already right and stayed untouched: `view.projection.frame-all` has its `KEYS` row
(`F`), its `ATOMS`-free ribbon method (`crates/ui/src/toolbars/spec.rs`) and its cluster cell
(`ClusterView`/`ClusterAction::FrameAll`), so the third `Done` clause was measured, not built.

## The decision that had to be made first

*When* a view follows the scene. The naive "refit on every rebuild that leaves the model outside
the frame" moves the camera under a hand that is working: a fill click, a drawn point, a pulled
height or a carried piece would land somewhere other than the screen showed, because the next
click reads the picture the camera just moved. So the rule is two-part:

- a leaf nobody has framed follows the scene;
- the fit waits while a gesture or an armed tool is reading the scene (`Document::scene_in_hand`).

The wait is not a loss: the check runs on every frame in `sync_scene`, not only after a rebuild, so
the first frame after the hand lets go fits the view on the same bounds. That also covers the case
a rebuild alone cannot see — an arm that ends without writing the document.

## Shape

- `camera::Camera::holds(corners, aspect)` — the geometric question: every world corner inside
  `±1` NDC. It answers in the projection the view really draws with, so an orthographic view is
  held only up to its own height.
- `Leaf.framed` — "a person has framed or navigated this view". `frame` (Frame all), `orbit`, `pan`,
  `pan_pixels`, `zoom_about`, `look_from`, `roll_to`, `roll_by` set it; the automatic fit uses the
  private `Leaf::place`, which changes the camera but leaves the leaf following. A preset does not
  claim a view: it writes a direction, not a framing, and the following fit keeps the preset.
- `Layout::fit_left_behind(fit, corners, area)` — every unclaimed leaf of the shown layout whose
  aspect-specific frame does not hold the corners takes the fit. `Layout::follows_the_scene` is the
  cheap question the shell asks each frame, so a workspace whose views are all claimed costs a scan
  of four flags and no projection at all.
- `Document::fit_left_behind` — called from `sync_scene` (every frame) and `set_layout` (a layout
  that was never shown held the cameras the document opened with). It says so in the bar:
  `Message::StatusFitted`, six seconds (`FIT_NOTICE`), quiet ink, in the slot the workspace notice
  uses.

## What the tests had to relearn

`splayed_shelf_harness` built a frame with two leaning posts on an empty scene and aimed the pointer
at the envelope's centre. With the old 1.2 m camera the pointer entered the frame through the back
wall and landed in the trapezoid; the fitted camera stands further back and higher, so the same
screen point enters over the top and names another cell (the fit mode reads
`largest_rectangle_through(footprint, point)`, so the pointer's *place* is the answer). The harness
now applies the `Front` standard view before aiming: orthographic rays are parallel, so the pointer
names the same cell and place whatever the fit did to the distance. The expectation (200 × 560) is
unchanged.

## Ruled out

- Refitting from `refresh` only: the arm-ending case above is invisible to a rebuild.
- Letting the auto-fit claim the leaf: the first fit would end the following, which is what the
  owner asked for and not what he wants.
- A timed refit or a nudge ("the model is 80 % out"): the containment test is exact and costs one
  projection per corner; a heuristic would be a second rule with no owner.
- Touching `crates/ui/src/inventory.rs`: the frame-all binding and its ribbon method were already
  there; the frame test presses the cluster's cell and `F` instead of adding a row nothing draws.

## Left behind

A new tool that owns the pointer must add itself to `Document::scene_in_hand`, or the view may
refit under its gesture. The list is one place and the doc comment says so; the mirrored debt
entry is not filed because the fit is deferred, not lost.
