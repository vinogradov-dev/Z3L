# U113 — four gestures, four keys, and a band that prints them

## What was measured

- `app/src/main.rs` held `TransformMode` and the two maps the shell made from it (`transform()`,
  `move_kind()`); the value was read by `document.rs` in seven places, by `status_bar.rs` and by
  `ui_shots.rs`. Nothing in `crates/ui` could name it, so the four gestures could not reach the node
  menu or the inventory's key table.
- `KEYS` already carries every binding as one row; `words(id)` resolves a row through `ROWS`,
  `ACTIONS` or the ribbon map, so an id added to `ACTIONS` gets a Settings row and a language check
  with no second table. `crates/ui/src/settings.rs` walks `KEYS`, so the shortcut page needed no edit.
- The free keys on the sketch/view/niche side are `Q`, `W`, `E` and `Shift+W`: `R`, `M`, `A`, `C`,
  `N`, `L`, `P`, `V`, `G`, `S`, `O`, `F` and `Alt+W` are taken.
- `InputState::consume_key` matches a press that carries *more* modifiers than the pattern, so a
  plain `W` read before `Shift+W` swallows it (the rule U78 already recorded for `Ctrl+0`).
- The band's four cells plus `Message::Gizmo` measure 393 pt in Russian at the narrowest window
  (640 pt, where the readouts begin at 448). Before the keys the same band was ~205 pt, so the four
  keys and the two spaces in front of each cost ~190 pt and about a half of the row's left zone.

## Decisions

- `TransformMode` moved to `crates/ui/src/transform.rs`, because three surfaces read it — the band,
  the node menu and the Settings page — and the menu is in `ui`. `app/src/main.rs` keeps what is the
  shell's own: `Transform` (which gizmo a mode arms) as `Transform::of`, and `MoveKind::of` beside
  the gizmo maths.
- The four are `ACTIONS` rows, not `ROWS`: they write no document, so no command and no undo entry
  answers for them, and `ACTIONS` is exactly "a shell action the shell runs from a key". Their words
  are the band's own messages, so the cell, the menu entry and the Settings row print one spelling.
- Keys: `Q` select, `W` free move, `Shift+W` furniture move, `E` rotate. The two moves are one
  decision made two ways (ADR 0147), so the furniture move is the shifted pair of the free one rather
  than a fourth unrelated letter.
- `NodeAction::Mode(TransformMode)` is a session entry with no id, like the eye and the padlock. The
  tab belongs to one document and the mode belongs to the window, so the editor cannot write it: it
  records the wish (`Editor::take_mode_request`) and the shell drains it once per frame after both
  menus have drawn. One slot, not a queue: only one menu is open at a time.
- The cell is a `LayoutJob`: the name in the body face, the key in the small face in the dim ink. The
  key is a reminder, not a second label, and the small face is what keeps the band inside the left
  zone; the band's button padding drops to `theme::STEP` for the same reason.
- The menu offers the four for a node and not for the root or empty space: a mode is armed on what is
  selected, and the band is always one press away.

## Ruled out

- `NodeAction::Mode(&'static str)` keyed by the action id, resolved by a match in the shell: a
  stringly round trip and a second table for a value the crate can simply own.
- Four booleans in `Shortcuts` instead of one `Option<TransformMode>`: the four are one value, and a
  frame that somehow carried two keys must leave the viewport in one mode.
- Keeping the enum in `main.rs` and adding an `impl`-shaped trait in the shell: an inherent impl
  cannot move with the type, and a one-type trait for two mappings is more machinery than `of`.
- Printing the key in the hover only, or dropping `Message::Gizmo` to save room: the band had the
  room once the key was small, and the narrow-window fit is now measured instead of assumed.

## The bug the taller menu found

`ViewportMenu` dismissed itself on `!response.contains_pointer()`. That flag is the previous pass's
hit test, and on a popup the window had to pull back inside the scene (a ten-row menu near the bottom
of the viewport) it stayed `false` while the pointer sat on one of the menu's own rows: a press on
"Hide" closed the menu and ran nothing. The dismissal now asks the rectangle the frame drew — the
same one `Chrome::covers` names — and `crates/ui/src/node_actions.rs` has the regression test.
