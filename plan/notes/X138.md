# X138 — the render place keeps the system arrow over its own surfaces

the render place keeps the system arrow over its own surfaces

## What was measured

- `App::draw_work_cursor` (`app/src/main.rs`) read `Document::scene_rect()`, and that rectangle is written only where the viewport draws (`crates/ui/src/document.rs`, the `self.scene_rect = Some(content)` line in the viewport update). While the render place was open the value still named the workspace area the place had replaced, and `work_cursor::show` hides the system arrow for any pointer inside the rectangle it is handed: the rail of numbers, the status row and the air beside the picture all drew the square and crosshair.
- The place replaces the whole design area (`App::design_area`: `if self.render_open { self.render_page(ui); self.status_bar(ui); return Chrome::empty() }`) and nothing in it is drawn, dragged or picked: `ui::render_page::show` paints the picture, the rail and the status row.
- Reproduced headless: with the place open, the frame reported `CursorIcon::None` over the rail's air (20 pt below its last row) and over a point 20 pt inside the place's left edge, both of them inside the old scene rectangle.

## Decisions

- The condition is one `if` at the call site: `draw_work_cursor` hands `work_cursor::show` no rectangle while the place is open, the same answer the shell gives where the work area is absent, so the module keeps its one rule (the square inside the scene rectangle, nowhere else).
- The viewport's own cursor is untouched: closing the place gives the square back on the next frame, and the test reads both ends of that.

## Ruled out

- Clearing `Document::scene_rect` when the place opens: the rectangle is the viewport's own geometry and the document reads it around that (`fit_left_behind`, the pointer fallback), so a surface that is not drawn must not erase it.
- A `render_open` check inside `work_cursor::show`: it would hand the shell's places to the module whose whole contract is the scene rectangle, and the same answer is one condition where the rectangle is chosen.
