# L2 — a window is a face the document carries and the tracer samples by solid angle

A rectangular emitter is the one light a picture of furniture cannot be made without: a point light
hardens every shadow the same way and has no body to see, so an interior lit through a window stayed
flat. The task adds the third light kind, the pane the viewport draws, and next-event estimation of
the face by solid angle with multiple importance sampling.

## What was measured

- `crates/model/src/light.rs` carried `Sun` and `Point` only, one wire layout of six fields, and a
  `LightNode::new` that took six arguments: the face entered a `LightSpec` struct, not a seventh
  parameter (`AGENTS.md` §1.5).
- `crates/final/src/light.rs` already had Vose's alias table and Veach's `balance`; both were
  written for this task and untested, because every light was a delta emitter and the weight was
  always one (`docs/decisions/0213`, `plan/debt.md` `[render]`).
- The frame knew one key light and eight point lights; the tracer's uniform grew by four faces of
  four `vec4`s, a count and three alias words.
- The sampler carried four dimensions; a rectangle spends four more (a pick pair and a point pair),
  so `DIMENSIONS` is 8 and four Joe–Kuo rows were added, asserted by value.
- The alias arithmetic: a one-metre window of 100 W is `L = 100 / (PI * 1)` = 31.83; a 0.2 m window
  of 100 W is 795.77, and the plate under it reads the cosine-law integral to 2% over a patch of
  pixels (`device_tests::a_window_lights_the_plate_under_it_by_the_irradiance_a_rectangle_gives`).

## Decisions

- **One face per light, stored for all three kinds.** `LightSize` is two `Length`s with a 10 mm …
  10 m range and a metre square default; a sun and a point light carry it and never read it, because
  one wire layout keeps a kind switch from losing an edit. Schema 34, one regenerated golden file.
- **The frame carries the watts and the geometry, never a radiance.** `AreaLight::radiance` is the
  one conversion (`P / (PI * A)` in `W/(m^2 sr)`), and it is the function the test measures.
- **The normal is derived, not stored.** `AreaLight::normal` is `half_height x half_width`, the ring
  order `corners` writes. A stored normal disagreed with the edges the shader sampled: the first
  picture was black and the "turned" case still emitted, which is how the field was removed.
- **The soup skips a source.** A light's pane is a `Drawable` with `FaceShading::Source`; `faces_of`
  drops it, so a shadow ray passes through the face that gives the light and the BVH holds no
  emissive triangle. The frame's `Lighting.areas` is the tracer's only view of it.
- **A source is a third viewport pass.** `crates/render` gained a blended, unlit, depth-tested pass
  with no stencil stamp and no depth write, ordered far to near, and every shaded pass, the shadow
  map and the contact-shadow mask skip it (`partition_visible`).
- **The viewport is lit by the middle of the face at the same power.** The owner's decision: an
  approximation of the softness and never of the watts, so a room the render fills with daylight does
  not draw as a black box.

## Ruled out

- **An egui overlay pane.** Cheaper (forty lines in `scene_gizmo`), but it draws a window over a
  cabinet standing in front of it and adds a fourth overlay ink to the `[gizmo]` debt. The owner
  chose the real pass.
- **Sampling the rectangle with the pair the point lights already use.** The two draws would be
  correlated with the surface's own sample, and the four new dimensions cost one row each.
- **A second alias table over points and areas together.** A delta light and a face have different
  densities; one table would need kinds inside one index space for no gain.
- **Pixels compared one at a time.** A pixel retires when its *estimated* error falls under 0.2%
  (`denoise::TARGET_RELATIVE_ERROR`), so one pixel reads a stopping rule's answer — the first
  comparison was 4–8% off for that reason alone. The test compares a patch against the same patch of
  the analytic profile.
