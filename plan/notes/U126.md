# U126 — A board named by points in space, and the third point fixing its plane.

A board named by points in space, and the third point fixing its plane.

## What was measured

- `crates/ui/src/draw.rs` (`DrawDraft`, 1152 p): a draft holds **work-plane** `[Length; 2]` clicks and
  `app/src/draw_gizmo.rs` maps the pointer onto that plane before the first click, so a board lying on
  a leaned face, or taken between points on different faces, cannot be a `DrawTool` — it needs a draft
  of world points and a gizmo that never asks for a plane.
- `crates/ui/src/toolbars/spec.rs:954` carried `planned("furniture.panels.points", …)`; the cell was in
  the map, live cells are one `ToolSpec` with `run` and `variants`, and `ready.rs` decides whether a
  cell runs here (`product_open && has_panel_material` for every panel cell).
- `app/src/measure_gizmo.rs` (`resolve`, `face_under`, `measure_point`) already resolved a pointer to a
  **world** point through the probe (ADR 0103) and the snap resolver (ADR 0051) on `SnapTarget::Free`.
  Two consumers of that rule is the trigger the `place-capability` skill names: it moved to
  `app/src/scene_snap.rs` (`SceneSnap`, `ScenePoint`, `resolve`) and the ruler now calls it.
- `crates/engine/src/panel_cmds/panel.rs` (`create_panel`, 639–700): `panel.create` takes
  `outline: Option<PanelOutline>` and `class: Option<ClassCode>`; `outline: None` is the standard
  rectangle, a `Contour` outline is re-anchored at its base corner by
  `add_panel_contour_with_bevels`, and a contour's `nominal_size` must equal its measured bounds. So
  three points need no command: the rectangle is `outline: None` + the plane frame, the ring is the
  ring + the frame.
- `crates/model/src/panel/classify.rs:69` (`role_of_plane`) is the class rule (ADR 0238), read in the
  parent's frame; `commit_panel` already reads it off the world placement.
- `crates/ui/src/tool_card.rs` (`body`): a card whose `rows` are empty returned after its choices and
  drew no pair, which the ring's finish needs; the fill card is choices-only with `ToolCommit::Gesture`,
  so drawing the pair only for `Pair` changes nothing there.
- `xtask/src/ui_rules.rs:48` (`SHELL_INK_DEBT`) lists the shell modules that may still hand-pick an
  ink and "only ever shrinks", so `app/src/points_gizmo.rs` takes `theme::accent`/`text_dim` instead.

## Decisions

- **`model::panel::points` owns the plane.** `PointsPlane::from_three` answers the origin and the two
  axes, `board_from_three` the rectangle's sides and `ring_from_points` the projected ring; the
  arithmetic is float geometry, every answer is whole micrometres. Collinear or coincident points are
  `PointsError`, so no tool guesses a plane from two points.
- **The three-point board is a standard panel.** The frame's origin is the first point, X along the
  edge, Y towards the third point, so the board is `outline: None` at its base corner and the fourth
  corner `(long, wide)` is computed — the ghost. The third point need not be a corner; only its
  perpendicular distance matters.
- **The ring commits on the finish alone, at every count.** A click on the first point would close a
  ring of three or four and silently not a longer one; the card's ✓/✕ pair (Enter/Escape) is the one
  finish. The pair appears only once the draft holds three points, because a check that answers with a
  refusal is worse than none (ADR 0077).
- **The class row is a four-option menu**: «По плоскости» (the plane's role), post, shelf, wall. The
  draft holds `Option<ClassCode>`; the commit reads `role_of_plane` from the parent-frame normal when
  the maker chose nothing, so a leaned side gives a post without asking (ADR 0238, 0239).
- **The draft refuses on the click that would fix a bad plane** (the third point), so a maker never
  places a fourth point on a plane that does not exist; a self-crossing ring is judged at the finish,
  because a chain still being drawn may pass itself on the way.
- **The refusal is a `Message` kept on the draft** (`PointsDraft::refusal`), drawn as the card's note,
  and the shell also reports it on the status bar — the two read one value.
- **A new icon, `assets/icons/scene/points.svg`**: the isometric face of a board with three control
  points at three of its corners and the 1.15 gaps, so the fourth corner is the computed one (guide
  §10.1, §9.1). The plate's 2-unit thickness band cannot host a 3.15 gap, which is why the glyph is the
  face and not the slab.
- **`tool_view` takes the language**, because the by-points card's note is text: the card and the
  inspector print one sentence in the person's language.

## Ruled out

- **A `DrawTool` variant** for the points tool: `DrawDraft` stores 2D work-plane points and its gizmo
  freezes a plane; a 3D point next to a 2D one in the same enum would make every method of the draft
  branch on the tool.
- **A new command** (`panel.points.create`): `panel.create` already carries outline, class and
  placement, and a second command would be a second revision for one gesture.
- **Auto-committing a ring at three or four points** and finishing only longer ones: two commit rules
  behind one gesture, and the maker would have to know which one the next click is.
- **A modifier** for the two methods: the same clicks would mean two things (the ADR records it).
- **A `ToolKind` per method**: `ToolKind::PanelPoints(PanelPoints)` keeps one cell and two rows, the
  same shape `ToolKind::Primitive` and `ProductBox` already use.
- **Repainting the refusal as a red `ToolOutcome::error`**: the card's fields carry no numbers here, so
  the note under the row is the surface that already exists.
