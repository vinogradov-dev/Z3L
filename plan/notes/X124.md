# X124 — What was measured

## What was measured

- The go gateway answers `400 MissingSessionID` without `x-opencode-session`, and `401 Invalid
  credential` with it and a fake key; the owner's curl worked because it sent the header.
- The previous build exposed a raw `Name: value` field, so the person had to know a provider quirk.
  A provider quirk belongs to the program; SolidCraft recognises the Go address and adds the header
  itself with one stable id per chat.

## What was decided

- Remove the extra-headers field and its schema. `is_opencode_go` recognises `opencode.ai` with
  `/go`; `OpenAi::with_session` sends one stable id per chat as `x-opencode-session`; no other
  endpoint is sent the header.
- Provider presets and a model picker are the next task: this one removes the field and teaches the
  Go quirk, which is what the owner's endpoint needed.

## What was ruled out

- A per-provider table in this change: the presets and the model list are a design of their own and
  need the ADR 0189 reversal recorded.
- A header in the key file: the key and the session are different things.
