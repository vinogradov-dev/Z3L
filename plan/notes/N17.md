# N17 — the frame reserves a footprint: a cut corner or a drawn ring, and its height

## What was measured

- `Envelope` (`crates/model/src/container.rs`) was three `Length`s behind a `TryFrom<EnvelopeWire>`, and
  `Copy`. `ProductEnvelope` (`crates/scene/src/lib.rs`) carried `size: DVec3`; the renderer dashed the
  twelve edges of that box (`ENVELOPE_EDGES`) and `ray_hit_product_envelope` was a slab against it.
- The panel already had the whole corner rule: `CornerFeatureKind` (`crates/model/src/panel_features.rs`)
  stores a chamfer as `incoming`/`outgoing` setbacks and a fillet as a radius, and
  `PanelOutline::ring_for` (`crates/model/src/panel/contour.rs`) turns a rectangle plus corners — or a
  stored `Contour` — into a `geom::Contour` in millimetres. Its refusals are exactly the ones this task
  needed: `FeatureOverlap` for a cut that consumes an edge, `RingSizeMismatch` for a ring whose bounds
  are not the sides.
- `Contour::sample(tolerance)` flattens arcs, and `Contour::joint_is_smooth` says where a real corner is,
  which is what a vertical edge of the wireframe needs.
- `Envelope` was `Copy`, so `Container::envelope()` returned it by value; 35 call sites, of which ~20
  read it. Returning `&Envelope` was the smaller change than a boxed/`Arc` shape.
- The golden file and four engine diagnostics fixtures carried the schema number as a literal (`26`).

## Decisions

- `Footprint` (`crates/model/src/footprint.rs`) is `Rectangle | Cut { corners: [Option<CornerFeatureKind>; 4] } | Ring { contour }`,
  keyed in `RectCorner::ALL` order. `x`/`y` stay the footprint bounds, so the export row, the niche probe
  and the containment test never learned a new shape (ADR 0159).
- Validation *is* the outline: `Footprint::outline(width, depth)` builds a `PanelOutline` and calls
  `ring_for`, so a value that exists has already been drawn once, and a cut that eats an edge is the
  panel's own readable sentence. `Envelope::with_footprint` and `Envelope::with_ring` are the two authors;
  `Envelope::new` stays the rectangle every existing caller already used.
- `Envelope` is `Clone` (not `Copy`) and `Container::envelope()` returns `Option<&Envelope>`. The
  accessors take `&self`, so call sites read unchanged.
- A ring is anchored at its own minimum corner in `Envelope::with_ring` (`Panel::anchored_contour`), so the
  product's zero stays the base min corner and the stored bounds are derived from the ring once.
- The scene carries the *sampled* outline plus the corner points, not the contour: scene stays free of
  the model's curve types, and the renderer needs points anyway. Sampling is `app/src/scene_build.rs`
  (`FOOTPRINT_SAMPLE_MM = 0.25`), because the shell is the only layer that already joins `model` and
  `geom`.
- Picking is the outline's prism: clip the ray to the box, split it at every crossing of the outline,
  test the middle of each piece with an even-odd point test (with a micrometre of slack so a grazing ray
  on a face still hits). The innermost-product rule moved from box volume to outline area × height.
- The renderer dashes the base ring, the top ring and one vertical per corner, so a rectangle still draws
  twelve edges and a fillet stands none at its tangency.
- Schema `26 → 27`; the golden scene now carries a product with a chamfer of two unequal setbacks, and the
  four diagnostics fixtures were bumped. No migration, no alias (ADR 0096).

## Ruled out

- A fixed-capacity ring array to keep `Envelope: Copy`: an invented bound on a drawn shape, and a second
  spelling of the rectangle.
- Storing the cut as an angle: the maker measures setbacks, and an angle would have to be inverted into
  offsets by every reader that draws or cuts it.
- Deriving the ring's bounds on every read: the read path would then own a `Result`, and the golden file
  could disagree with itself; validating once at the door is enough.
- Sampling the arcs in `render`: the renderer would need `geom::Contour` and a tolerance constant, and the
  scene would no longer be plain points.
- A second picking rule for rings and for cuts: both are the same prism test.
- Keeping a corner grip on a cut corner: it would stand in empty space, outside the piece it edits.
