# T8 — the P2 and P4 accents separate from the model and scene inks

The v2 icon check found two hue collisions that the existing contrast test cannot see. The role inks
are preset-independent (U70); a preset changes the neutrals and the accent, never the meaning. So an
accent that sits on top of a role ink is the defect to remove, either by moving the accent or by
moving the role ink. This note holds the measurement; the owner chooses the road.

## Measured on 2026-11-15

Contrast is `theme::contrast_ratio`; ΔE is CIE76 between the role ink and the preset accent, same
variant. The weakest role on each preset is also listed.

| Preset · variant | Weakest role on panel/ribbon/surface | Closest role to the accent |
|---|---|---|
| P1 copper · dark | 4.61:1 (`danger`) | `reference` ΔE 29.3 |
| P1 copper · light | 4.30:1 (`draw`) | `reference` ΔE 25.8 |
| P2 azure · dark | 4.84:1 (`danger`) | `model` ΔE 7.2 |
| P2 azure · light | 4.37:1 (`draw`) | `model` ΔE 8.6 |
| P4 mint · dark | 4.46:1 (`danger`) | `scene` ΔE 7.0 |
| P4 mint · light | 4.42:1 (`draw`) | `scene` ΔE **1.9** |

- P4 light: the scene green and the mint accent are effectively one colour. P2: the model blue and
  the azure accent are close, but still distinguishable.
- The new reference yellow is safe on P1 (ΔE 29.3 / 25.8); do not move it.
- The closest pair of role inks is `model`/`modify` (ΔE 23.5 dark / 20.1 light); any move must keep
  that above ~20.
- The chosen glyph is the theme foreground, not white; `e07c7db` fixed the light-theme invisibility.
  The accent and a role ink never appear as two glyphs, but the accent's job is to be chrome beside
  the glyphs, and on P4 it is not.

## The options

**A. Move the P2 and P4 accents.** Keep the seven role inks; choose a P2 accent far from `model` and
from `draw`/`modify`, and a P4 accent far from `scene` and from `draw`/`reference`. This preserves
the icon meaning and changes only the preset's chrome. It changes the preset identity (`ink·azure`,
`slate·mint`), so it needs the owner's word and an update of the preset labels, `plan/notes/U71.md`,
the contrast tests and the palette docs. A candidate is a deeper cobalt/indigo for P2 and a warm
copper or violet for P4, measured against all seven inks; no candidate is chosen here.

**B. Move the role inks.** Keep the presets; shift `model` away from azure (an indigo/ultramarine)
and `scene` away from mint (a forest/jade green). This keeps the preset identities and the accent
chrome, but the role inks are shared by every preset, so P1 changes too, and the new hues must stay
distinct from `modify` violet and `draw` cyan. This is the smaller visual change to the presets and
the larger change to the icon set (T6/T7 redraw the glyphs anyway).

**C. Accept the overlap.** The active glyph is the foreground, so no two glyphs collide; the accent
is a background and a border. This contradicts U70's "the accent is never a role ink" in spirit, so
accepting it needs an ADR or a `plan/debt.md` entry, not a comment.

## Recommendation

Measure candidates for A and B against all seven role inks (contrast ≥ 3:1 on panel/ribbon/surface,
ΔE ≥ 20 between the accent and every role and between role pairs), then choose with the owner:
B keeps the presets and fixes the set; A keeps the set and fixes the presets. Do not move `reference`
and do not introduce a per-preset icon ramp (U70 ruled it out).

## The test this task adds

`crates/ui/src/icons.rs` has the contrast test; add a **hue-distance** test beside it: for every
preset and variant, the accent's ΔE to every role ink is at least 20, and the role pairs keep their
existing separation. The current suite would pass the P4 collision, which is why it went unnoticed.
## Decision (2026-11-15): option A

The owner chose **A**: keep the seven role inks and move the **P2** and **P4** accents. The role
inks, the icon families and the v2 artwork stay as they are; only the preset chrome changes.

### What the task must hold to

- Each new accent is at least **20 ΔE** (CIE76) from every role ink — `draw`, `model`, `scene`,
  `reference`, `modify`, `view`, `danger` — and the role-pair separation is not reduced (the closest
  pair, `model`/`modify`, is ΔE 23.5 dark / 20.1 light).
- Each new accent keeps **≥ 3:1** contrast on panel, ribbon and surface in both variants of its
  preset, and the chosen glyph still uses the theme foreground (the `e07c7db` rule).
- The preset's **stored name and label** are part of the settings file: `ink_azure` and `slate_mint`
  are written by users, and a P4 accent that is no longer mint makes the name lie. The task decides
  and records whether the label/name changes, with the settings migration if it does — the same
  question the rename task R1 will meet.
- The **hue wheel is crowded**: cyan draw, blue model, green scene, yellow reference, violet modify,
  grey view, red danger. An accent must be far from all seven, so the candidates are likely a
  lightness/chroma variant between two roles (for example a deep orange for P1/P4 or a magenta for
  P2) rather than a new hue. If no candidate clears the floor, stop and ask the owner again; do not
  silently lower the 20 ΔE floor or recolor a role ink.
- Add the **hue-distance test** beside the contrast test: for every preset and variant, the accent's
  minimum ΔE to the seven inks is ≥ 20. The current suite passes the P4 collision (ΔE 1.9), so the
  test is the proof that this task worked.

### Tendering the accent

Measure candidates by rasterising the real palette (do not eyeball hex codes): compute ΔE against all
seven inks, contrast on `panel`, `surface` and the ribbon mix, and how the accent reads beside an
armed cell. P1's copper is the reference case: it is 25.8–29.3 ΔE from the nearest role and can stay.
