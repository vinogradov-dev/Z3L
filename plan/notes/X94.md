# X94 — the row is three rectangles, and a zone paints inside its own

## What was measured

- `status_row_ui` split the row with `right = (row * 0.30).clamp(120, 260)`: 192 pt at the narrowest
  window the shell opens (640 pt, `main.rs`'s `min_inner_size`), 260 pt from 867 pt up.
- The right zone's readouts measure **293.4 pt in English and 343.4 pt in Russian** (four items and
  three dividers). The reserve was short at every width, so the readouts were painted past it.
- The row was laid out by egui's cursor, and `allocate_ui_with_layout` advances that cursor by the
  child's *content*, not by its rect: at 1024 pt the command's overflow pushed the right zone to
  [781, 1041]. `Ready` landed at [1041, 1074] — outside the window — and `100 %` at [1008, 1041] was
  cut by the screen edge, while the snap toggle began at 768, inside the zone before it.
- `egui` does not clip a child ui to its rectangle: the frame's clip rect is the panel's, so any
  overflow paints over the neighbour. No shape in the row carried a clip rect of its own.
- Only `hint()` truncated its label, and `Label::truncate` truncates to the layout's *available*
  width, not to what the row still needs: at 1024 pt in English the viewport hint took 414 pt and left
  the selection 12 pt — the centre zone was effectively invisible.
- Armed content at 640 pt: contour 445 pt (En) / 578 pt (Ru), draft 440 pt (En) / 521 pt (Ru); the
  mode band alone is 433.4 pt in Russian, against the 448 pt X88 measured.

## Decisions

- Three explicit rectangles, one per zone, each drawn in a `scope_builder` whose `max_rect` is the
  zone and whose `set_clip_rect` is the zone: a widget that does not fit is cut at the boundary
  instead of painting under its neighbour, and the cursor can no longer carry a zone off the window.
- The right zone's width is **measured from its own widgets**, not taken as a share of the row: a
  hidden probe (`new_child` + `invisible` + `set_clip_rect(Rect::NOTHING)`) lays the readouts out and
  `min_rect().width()` is the answer. `COMMAND_ZONE_MIN = 448` keeps X88's floor at the narrowest
  window, so `zone_widths(row, readouts)` returns the command's width and the readouts' width.
- The command zone's own lines take a `reserve`: the centre's measured width (`center_width`, the same
  probe idiom). A hint elides first, a node's name second — the row reports before it explains.
- The readouts are drawn **left to right** and `status_right_ui` walks in visual order (snap, divider,
  projection, divider, scale, divider, state). The rect is known from the measurement, so the group is
  simply placed at the row's right edge.
- `test_support::drawn_text_rects` now skips a shape whose clip rect has no area. `epaint` skips such
  a shape, so a test must too: without it a measuring pass's galleys counted as drawn text and a
  test asserting "the readouts drew" would pass on the probe alone.

## Ruled out

- **A right-to-left layout for the readouts**, with or without a zero-size spacer, and through
  `allocate_ui_with_layout` and `scope_builder` alike: in egui 0.37 the first widget of an RTL flow is
  placed with its galley at the rect's right edge and every later item marches from there, so the group
  shifts right by one item's width. Measured: `CCC` at [1024, 1045] inside a rect ending at 1024.
- **A fixed reserve for the readouts** (a fraction, or a constant): the measured 293/343 pt say the
  reserve was never a matter of taste, and a constant a reader cannot check is the defect again.
- **Eliding the armed branches' labels** so all three buttons stay inside 448 pt in Russian:
  `Свободное перемещение · Применить · Отмена` alone is 274 pt, and the label needs a second
  measurement per frame. U115 moves those buttons to the tool's card, so the gap is a debt entry, not
  a layout.
- **Reserving a slice of the row for the centre** (a ratio, or its natural width unconditionally):
  the measured width is what the centre draws, and a ratio would elide the hint for a two-word name.

## What the row looks like now

- 1024 pt, English: band 328 · hint (elided) 141 · divider · selection 213 · readouts 293, where the
  readouts end exactly at the window's edge.
- 1024 pt, Russian: band 433 · hint 12 · selection 193 · readouts 343.
- 1280 pt on the dark preset (`target/ui-shots/…-scale100.png`): `Select (Q) Move (W) … Turn (Shift+E)`
  · `LMB select · double-clic…` · `Back panel · Panel · 900 mm × 600 mm × 18 mm · Oak veneered
  chipboard · edge 2 mm` · `Snap 10 mm (G) | Perspective | 100 % | Ready`, one run per zone and no
  shape under another.
