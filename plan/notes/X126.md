# X126 — What was measured

## What was measured

- The field already turned Send into Stop while a turn ran, but `OpenAi` inherited the trait's
  no-op `cancel`, so the worker kept the turn's receiver: Stop ended the loop only at the next
  `take`, and the answer was still there.
- The transcript had no copy affordance; the working line said only "The agent is working…".

## What was decided

- `OpenAi::cancel` drops `answer`, the turn's receiver; the worker's send fails and the turn ends
  on the frame of the press. A blocking TLS read cannot be aborted without a socket handle, so the
  gateway may still finish the request; its tokens are the provider's, not the window's.
- A Copy button beside Send copies `Chat::transcript_text(lang)`: every line with its speaker
  (`You`, `Agent`, `Tool`, `Note`), so a person can hand a turn to a reader without a screenshot.
- The working line names the request number from `Turn::calls`, so a long turn says which call is
  in flight.
- The copy sits in the field row, not on a row of its own: the panel keeps the size it opens at
  (`the_assistant_panel_keeps_the_size_it_opens_at`).

## What was ruled out

- A sidecar file with the conversation: the copy solves the sharing need without persisting chat
  state; the audit sidecar is a separate decision (ADR 0028).
- Aborting the TCP/TLS request: ureq's blocking call exposes no socket handle here.
