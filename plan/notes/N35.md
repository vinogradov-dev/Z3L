# N35 — a niche keeps the whole shape the frame draws

what a session measured about a cabinet built out of a drawn ring, and why the niche now has two readings

## What the owner reported, and what was measured

The scene is `/home/alx/skaf.z3l`, whose product reserves an L: 2244 x 4423 x 3885 mm, six segments, one
1746 mm arc along the long side, a 545 x 719 step in the top edge. Four sentences, and the measurement
behind each:

- «Простая панель не все места определяет». A fit was searched in the **piece** N34 defined (the convex
  cut around the pointer), so in the left arm it was 1654 x 2688 mm where the frame holds 2087 x 2703,
  and a hand in the arc's bulge got a rectangle measured elsewhere. Measured by walking a 100 mm grid:
  97 of 884 points planned a part that did not stand under the pointer.
- «Со стороны радиуса вообще не определяется ниша». No refusal: every point of the L answered a niche. The
  defect was in `ui::pointer_face`, which entered the product's **box** with `box_entry`; over the corner
  the arc cut away, the ray's entry is outside the ring, so the point named no void while the space
  behind that corner was under the cursor.
- «Фигурная панель не заливает всю форму каркаса». The cell's ring was seeded as the pointer's piece, so
  the Shape mode filled one arm: 1654 x 2688. The frame's free area at that height is 9.9 m2; the part was
  4.4 m2.
- «При частичной заливки вместо радиуса скос». `exact_section` refused the ring whenever a hidden body
  was present (`untrimmed`) and whenever a post stood across the cell, so the outline came from the chord
  polygon: a radius arrived as a chamfer.

## Decisions

- **One frame, two readings** (ADR 0204). The **ring** is the space the frame reserves — whole, any shape,
  the pointer only has to stand inside it — and it is what a part takes; the **piece** stays the convex
  cell the planes, the corners, the probes and the non-shelf stances are written in. The seed therefore
  computes both: `frame_ring` (counter-clockwise, no convexity test) and `convex_piece`.
- **The box is the ring's**: `local_min`, `size` and `corners` come from the ring's bounds and its two
  heights (`Cell::region_bounds`, `Cell::region_corners`), so an L reports the L and the ghost matches the
  part instead of understating it.
- **`Cell::contains` asks the ring and the two heights**, not the planes, when a ring is kept: the planes
  are the piece's chords and a piece is smaller than its frame.
- **The pointer enters the reserved prism** (`reserved_entry`): `Region::line_intervals` along the ray's
  own plan line, intersected with one `slab_interval` from floor to ceiling; `box_entry` stays only where
  a box is the right reading.
- **A shelf's section is the ring clipped by the walls**: probe planes clip it around the pointer
  (`clip_half_plane_around`), and a hidden body clips it by the same half-plane `trim_footprint` chose for
  the polygon, so one decision feeds both readers and a trim keeps the arc.
- **A fit goes where the hand is**: `search_rectangle` with a point, `band_span` over the outline's
  breakpoints (a convex `vertical_span` at the two ends is wrong for an L), `rect_inside` by a Liang–Barsky
  crossing test. The old ternary refinement is kept for a slanted wall but scores through `band_span` and
  the point, so it can never steer towards a rectangle the answer may not be.

## Ruled out

- Making the **whole** cell a ring: `cut` for a vertical stance, the corner enumeration and the bevel
  classification are plane algorithms, and a prism's vertical section of an L is not one ring. The shelf
  is the stance the ring is written for (ADR 0106 keeps the plane cell).
- Moving the box to the **pickable curvature** of the arc in `pointer_face`: the correct entry is the
  prism's own, and a box with a cut corner would have to be re-authored for every footprint shape.
- Cutting a **hole** where a body stands inside the region: a ring with a hole is a `Region` already, but
  the corners, the probes and `holds_body` all read planes, and nothing about the owner's case needed it.
- Searching the fit in the **sampled ring**: the arc sampled at 0.5 mm is 3700 points, and a search over
  every breakpoint pair is quadratic in that.

## Verification

- Headless, UI: `the_pointer_enters_the_space_the_frame_reserves_and_not_the_box_around_it` (a ray over a
  notch's corner finds the arm behind it; the box's entry is `Outside`), and the atom still prints the
  footprint refusal for a ring that bulges inwards.
- Headless, model: `a_notched_frame_reserves_the_whole_shape_it_draws` (three points of an L answer the
  same 600 x 560 x 720 box and the same eight-segment section; the notch is `Outside`),
  `a_point_in_a_bowed_frames_bulge_is_inside_the_void` (the box is the ring's 660 mm, the section keeps
  the bulge), `a_trimmed_shape_still_keeps_the_frames_own_radius` (a hidden sheet trims to 482 mm and the
  ring still carries its arc). The whole workspace passes, `cargo xtask ci` is green.
- On the owner's file, a scratch probe (deleted): 884 sampled points of the L, every one answering a
  `Shape` plan whose ring is the frame's six segments with its arc, no plan refused, no outline point of
  either mode outside the frame, the niche box 2244 x 4423 at every point.
