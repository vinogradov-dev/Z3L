# X106 — the panel that holds the plan/build switch had no entry in the interface

the panel that holds the plan/build switch had no entry in the interface

## What was measured

- The switch existed and was drawn: `app/src/chat.rs` `mode_switch` (two segments and the sentence
  under them), reading `Session::agent_mode` (`crates/engine/src/session.rs`).
- The panel itself was reachable by one thing: `shortcuts.assistant` in `app/src/shortcuts.rs`,
  bound to `Ctrl+J` in `crates/ui/src/inventory.rs` (`KEYS`). `assistant.open` had an `ACTIONS`
  row and a key and no surface that drew it: no ribbon cell, no palette entry, no menu.
- `crates/mcp/src/report.rs` `batch` answered `mode: plan` and said nothing about who holds the
  mode, so a client could read the dry run as a tool it had not learned yet.
- ADR 0209 had already decided the fix: "When an icon is drawn for it ... the header gains one cell
  and this record's second bullet is what it must keep".

## Decisions

- The header's right cluster gains one cell, drawn in `app/src/header.rs` `cluster`: gear, the
  assistant's cell, a divider, the ribbon fold, the two panel switches, a divider, the band. The gear
  and the assistant are the two surfaces that open *over* the scene, so they stand together at the
  row's edge and the layout switches keep their group.
- The glyph is a speech bubble with two lines in the `view` ink, 1.7 units on the 24 grid: a service
  icon (STYLE_GUIDE §19), not a geometry one. `assets/icons/actions/assistant.svg`, and the README
  table, the guide's §19.3 metaphor row and §19.6 name set move with it.
- The cell is a toggle whose selected state is the panel being up, and its hint is
  `tool_hint(Message::ShortcutAssistant, inventory::key_label(ASSISTANT_OPEN))`, so the key is
  printed from the table and never spelled in the header.
- `Chat::toggle` is the one act: the header's cell and `Ctrl+J` both call it, so a panel that opens
  from two doors cannot close from one.
- The wire's answer carries `mode_note` (`mcp::PLAN_NOTE`) only while the session is in `plan`: it
  says nothing was written, that the switch is a person's, where it lives and the key. A build answer
  carries no note, and a test asserts both halves.
- ADR 0222 records the place and the glyph; ADR 0209's status line points at it rather than the body
  being rewritten.

## Ruled out

- The palette. It lists `CommandRegistry::specs()`, and `assistant.open` is a shell action, so a
  palette row would have meant a second dispatch path for actions -- a larger change than the bug
  asks for and one that touches the palette's contract.
- The ribbon. A cell is visible only while its tab is active, and the complaint was that the panel is
  not findable at all.
- A new command id. The switch writes no document and belongs to the session (ADR 0190); a command
  would have given it an undo entry it must never have.
- Spelling `Ctrl+J` in `crates/text` as a message. The binding is `KEYS`' data and the hint reads
  it; a message would be a second spelling of a key. `mcp` keeps its own spelling because it may not
  read `ui` (ADR 0186), and `app` -- the one crate that sees both -- asserts the two agree
  (`the_plan_answer_names_the_key_the_header_cell_prints`).
- Hiding the cell while a modal is up. The header is always visible and the cell's selected state says
  the panel is open; a second rule about modals would have added a state nobody asked for.

## Gates run

- `cargo test -p ui --lib icons`, `-p app --bins`, `-p mcp`: green; `cargo test --workspace` green.
- `cargo xtask ui-rules`, `docs`, `skills`: green. The inventory needed no new row: `ACTIONS` and
  `KEYS` already held `assistant.open`, and the gate reads the surface it now has.
- The icon was rasterised at 256 px and looked at (a speech bubble with two lines, inside the
  footprint); the header cell was driven by a headless frame test, not by eye. No colour or contrast
  is new: the glyph takes the action theme's `view` ink like every other chrome icon.
