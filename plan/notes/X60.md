# X60 — a dense frame keeps every drawable's wireframe

## What was measured

- Before X57: `geometry::edge_geometry` flattened every drawable's edges into one list and stopped at
  `MAX_VERTICES` = 4 000 000, after which each later drawable's slice had `vertex_count` 0. A
  32 x 16 sphere is 1 440 edges = 2 880 line vertices, so the 5 000-sphere stress scene drew the
  wireframe on 1 388 balls (27.8 %) and the rest read as solids.
- After X57: one line buffer per drawable, keyed by the edge list, the world transform and the frame's
  ink, drawn per drawable in both edge styles. The budget is per buffer, so the cut depends on a
  drawable's own list and never on its place in the frame.

## Decisions

- A list past the budget is refused by name (`RenderError::EdgeListTooLarge`) before anything is
  allocated, which is what the entry asked for instead of a cut.
- No device-limit check was added: 4 000 000 vertices of 40 bytes is 160 MB, under the 256 MiB that
  every wgpu limit preset (`default`, `downlevel_defaults`, `downlevel_webgl2_defaults`) reports as
  `max_buffer_size`, so the length guard refuses first on every backend the product supports.

## What replaces the test the entry asked for

- `tests::every_drawable_of_a_dense_frame_keeps_all_of_its_edges`: 1 500 drawables of 2 880 line
  vertices are 4.32 M, past the old frame budget, and each one keeps its full list. Cheap because the
  lists are built and dropped one at a time.
- `tests::an_edge_list_past_the_budget_is_refused_by_name`: one list past the budget is the readable
  error.
- `tests::the_edge_pass_draws_every_drawable_own_lines` (ignored, needs an adapter): both drawables'
  own buffers reach the picture.
- The live window with `target/stress-5000.z3l` was not re-run here. The observation was a cut keyed
  to a drawable's position in the frame, and that code path no longer exists; the upload cache test
  also asserts that each drawable owns its edge buffer after an edit.

## Ruled out

- Chunking one drawable's list into several buffers. It only shows on a mesh past 4 M line vertices
  in one style (about 1.3 M triangles with *all surface edges*), and the refusal names the drawable
  until then; the residual is recorded in the `[perf]` entry X57 added.
- Keeping the frame-wide list and splitting it into as many buffers as the budget needs — the shape
  the entry proposed before X57 landed. It would still cut a frame at a fixed count and still copy
  every line on every edit.
