# T5 — the drawing rules, and the colour check that came with them

## What was measured

- **The chosen ribbon glyph was white on the accent fill.** On paper the accent fill is pale (P1
  #f0d3a8, P2 #cfe0f7, P4 #cdebdd), so the armed icon and its caret read at 1.27–1.44:1 and nearly
  disappear. The theme foreground reads 4.31–11.69:1; `ribbon_face`, the split cell and its caret now
  take that ink, and a contrast test beside the role table holds it. Commit e07c7db.
- **The resting role inks on the three schemes**: the weakest is draw on the light ribbon, 4.30:1
  (P1) / 4.37 (P2) / 4.42 (P4); every role clears 3:1 on panel, ribbon and surface. The new reference
  yellow clears 9.67:1 on the dark panels and 4.8:1 on the light ones.
- **Hue spacing**: the reference yellow is ΔE 29.3 (dark) / 25.8 (light) from the P1 copper accent, so
  it does not read as the accent. Two existing pairs are much closer: `model` blue vs the P2 azure
  accent (ΔE 7.2 / 8.6) and `scene` green vs the P4 mint accent (ΔE 7.0 / **1.9** on light). The
  active glyph is the theme foreground, so the accent and the role ink never appear as two glyphs;
  still, P4 makes the accent and the scene ink the same hue, and a later theme pass should separate
  them.
- **Icon geometry**: the demo's nodes are circles r 2.0 with a 1.15 gap; the baseline stack puts the
  long dimension outermost and stops the near witness 2.5 above its own line; radius and diameter
  leaders are at 45°; the angular icon uses 45° ticks because arrowheads lose their shape below
  ~40 px.

## Decisions

- A control point is neutral `view`, not a darker tone of the class: on dark a darker cyan sinks into
  the panel, and a tone keeps the same hue. Neutral reads on both variants and matches the canvas
  handles.
- The measured object and base are `view`; the dimension annotation is `reference`; a result is its
  class ink. This is the two-ink rule the icon set follows.
- A dimension icon is not a drawing: the drawing arrow touches the extension line, the icon keeps
  1.0–1.1 units of air. The rule is written in `.pi/skills/drafting/references/dimensions.md`.

## Ruled out

- A preset-specific icon ramp to dodge the P2/P4 collisions: U70 ruled it out, and the active glyph is
  already the foreground.
- White as the chosen-glyph ink: it is invisible on all three light accent fills.
- Four arrows on the chain icon at 26 px: they merge; the middle witness carries the split, and the
  drawing rule stays in the note.
