# X43 — What was measured

## What was measured

- `crates/ui/src/point_fields.rs`, `plate_corner`: every plate was placed independently at its own
  anchor plus `PLATE_GAP`. `crates/ui/src/draw_fields.rs`, `Shape::Position`: X and Y push rows with
  the same anchor (`at`), so the Y plate was drawn exactly over the X plate; the screenshot
  `09-52-11` shows only `Y 240 mm`.
- `Shape::Segment` anchors its length plate on the chord's midpoint and its angle plate on the
  reference arc (`arc_anchor`). At some pointer positions those two rectangles intersect; the
  screenshots `09-52-25` and `09-55-03` show the angle plate half under the length plate.
- `crates/ui/src/guides.rs`, `reference_between`: took `from`/`to` in radians and a screen radius and
  sampled a circle in screen space. In a perspective or axonometric view the work-plane circle
  projects to an ellipse, so the sweep and the X axis were not the plane's own figure.
- `crates/ui/src/theme.rs`: `REFERENCE_DOT` 0.4 and `REFERENCE_DOT_LIGHT` 0.22 put the whole mark
  under one antialiased pixel across; on the dark workspace the dark rim disappeared and the light
  centre was too small to carry the line.
- `crates/ui/src/draw_gizmo.rs`, `sync_fields`: the `FieldSet` is rebuilt when the draft's point count
  changes. Switching tools through the ribbon resets the gizmo (`show_ribbon` on
  `outcome.started_drawing`), so a tool change is clean there; the scenario test drives the same
  reset explicitly.
- `crates/ui/src/editor/drawing.rs`, `start_drawing_with`: a new draft always starts empty, resets
  the arc mode to false and the intent to `Path`; `start_panel_drawing` sets the intent after it.
  `Editor::arm_box`, `arm_measure`, `arm_void` and `start_contour_edit` clear `drawing` through
  `leave_pointer_modes` or by assignment, so a non-drawing mode cancels the draft.

## Decisions

- The plates of one point are one layout (ADR 0132). `FieldSet::plate_layout` resolves every row
  before drawing: preferred corner from the anchor, then a collision moves the row away from the
  point past the rectangle it hit. The size is the last measured rectangle, or a conservative
  estimate on the first frame of a stage. No declared row can be hidden.
- The reference is work-plane geometry (ADR 0133). `Reference::samples` returns the axis' two ends and
  a sweep sampled once per degree; `draw_gizmo::reference_screen` and the same helper in
  `contour_gizmo` project every sample. `guides::reference` now takes the two projected polylines and
  no longer knows an angle or a screen radius.
- The dot grew to 0.7 with a 0.38 light centre and a 4-point step, still under `HELPER_STROKE` in
  diameter and still the fixed two-tone pair of ADR 0121. ADR 0127 keeps its decision; its sizes are
  marked superseded by 0133.

## Ruled out

- Keeping independent placement and nudging only X/Y: the same collision appears for every
  length-and-angle stage, and a special case per shape would put the layout in `draw_fields`.
- Measuring a plate before drawing it: an egui `Area` sizes itself on its first pass, so a second
  measurement pass would double the widget or duplicate ids. The previous frame's rectangle plus an
  estimate is enough and keeps one draw per plate.
- Shifting by a fixed plate pitch: anchors can be offset by any amount, so one pitch can leave a
  sliver of overlap. The shift is the distance to the hit rectangle's far edge plus the gap.
- Drawing the sweep as a dashed screen-space arc: the whole defect is that the screen is not the
  plane. Only projected samples can be right in perspective and axonometric views alike.
- Raising only the light dot's radius: the mark's diameter is what survives antialiasing; a large
  light centre inside a tiny dark rim still reads as a speck.
- A separate guide layout for the contour latch: the latch shows the same reference, so it calls the
  same sampler and the same painter.
