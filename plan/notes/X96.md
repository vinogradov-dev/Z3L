# X96 — both directions of the along-contour move

## What was measured

- The drag is clamped on the motion segment in two places, and both freeze the outward direction.
  `crates/model/src/contour_edit.rs:187` (`Path::move_corner_at`, `CornerMove::Along`) projects onto
  the support and calls `clamp_to_segment` -> `clamp_to_extent` (line 731); `Contour::move_corner_with`
  (line 504) does the same for a ring.
- `crates/geom/src/segment.rs:156` (`Line::clamp_to_extent`) pins the projection parameter to
  `[margin/length, 1 - margin/length]` with `margin = CLAMP_MARGIN_MM = 1e-3` mm
  (`crates/model/src/contour_edit.rs:46`); the arc variant (line 629) clamps the travel inside the
  sweep it already has. The moving corner sits at parameter 0 or 1, so the first along frame pulls it
  one margin inside and every outward frame returns the same point: the gesture shortens and can
  never lengthen. `an_along_contour_drag_cannot_pass_the_opposite_node`
  (`crates/model/src/contour_edit.rs:1763`) pins the inward wall.
- The typed length is clamped a second time, in the document: `ContourSegment::point_at_distance_from`
  (`crates/model/src/contour.rs:165`) clamps the number to `[0, length]` for a line and `[0, arc_len]`
  for an arc, so a number larger than the remaining distance lands on the far end, which is the
  collapse, and nothing can grow.
- The collapse is recorded, not applied: `crates/ui/src/contour_edit.rs:213` and `:255` record the
  collapsed segment, `app/src/contour_gizmo.rs:352` sets it when the measured length is `<= 2 µm` while
  dragging, and Apply turns it into `Contour::remove_segment`.
- The plate measures the distance from the fixed far end (`ContourDraft::along_contour_length`,
  `crates/ui/src/contour_grips.rs:219`, with `motion_far_end` at `:243`), so it falls to zero at the
  far end and is continuous past it. Its range is `0.0..=1_000_000.0` mm
  (`app/src/contour_gizmo.rs`, `grip_field`), and no numeric atom in the tree takes a negative length
  (`crates/ui/src/distance.rs:95` refuses one).
- The arc extension rule already exists for a neighbour: `Arc::with_start_point` and `with_end_point`
  (`crates/geom/src/segment.rs:504` and `:532`) allow `MAX_ARC_EXTENSION = pi/2` (line 29) past the
  near end and refuse more; `trim_segment` already leans on it.

## Decisions

- The corner slides on the motion segment support with `join_corner` reconciling the neighbour it
  shares the corner with: the neighbour pays for the growth, so a line neighbour takes the new corner
  and an arc neighbour extends by up to `MAX_ARC_EXTENSION`, and a corner that leaves the neighbour
  circle is the existing `PointOffCircle` refusal.
- The two ends of the number: `0` is the collapse, because a drag to the far end and a typed zero are
  one act today; the top is the arc own sweep plus `pi/2`, never a full turn, and for a line the
  field own 1 000 000 mm cap. Above the arc bound the edit is refused with a sentence naming the 90
  degree rule, and a refused drag leaves the draft on its last accepted frame, so the wall is visible.
- The clamp becomes one-sided: the corner is held short of the opposite node only, so the near end is
  no longer a wall. The geom helper needs to know which end the corner is on, because the wall moves
  with it.

## Ruled out

- Letting the corner cross the far end and flip the motion segment: a segment stores two points and no
  sign, so a negative length is a different shape rather than a longer one, and
  `ContourSegment::line` already refuses a zero-length pair (`crates/model/src/contour.rs:178`). The
  far end stays a wall and the collapse stays a collapse.
- Turning an arc neighbour into a line when the grown corner leaves its circle: the task rule is the
  existing refusal, and a silent curve change loses the shape the person drew.
- A second numeric field for the along move: the shared scrub and the shared `FieldSet` already carry
  this value.

## Open

- The typed number direction is the owner to decide: the plate measures the distance from the far
  end, so one positive number reaches both directions once the document clamp goes (`48` shortens,
  `300` grows to 300, `0` collapses), while the alternative is a signed delta from the current
  position (`-40` shortens, `40` grows) and would be the first negative length in the interface.
## Decided with the owner

- The typed number keeps one meaning, the distance from the fixed far end, and the document clamp
  goes: a smaller number shortens and a larger one lengthens the same corner. A signed delta was
  ruled out by the owner, so no length field in the interface takes a negative number, and the
  field's range is the segment's own two ends (zero and the sweep an arc may add).
- The corner does not cross the far end: a segment stores two points and no sign, so the collapse
  stays what reaching it means.
