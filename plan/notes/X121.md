# X121 — A standard view writes the target, so the model lands in the middle

A standard view writes the target, so the model lands in the middle

## What was measured

- `Leaf::apply_preset` (`app/src/view_layout.rs`) wrote the direction and, for a standard preset,
  the projection — nothing else — because ADR 0049 says a preset keeps the target and the distance.
  `Leaf::look_from` did the same, and the corner widget's six axis balls go through it, which is the
  door the owner pressed.
- The target was therefore whatever the view already had: the empty scene's origin on a tab whose
  model was built elsewhere, or the union centre of parts that no longer exist. Measured headless:
  a near board at (600, 200, 0) and a far one at (6000, 4000, 0), the automatic fit centred on the
  union, the far board undone — the view still held the near one, so nothing refitted — and pressing
  Top left the target at (3660, 2109, 300) while the model's centre was (900, 560, 300).
- `home_camera` (`app/src/scene_build.rs`) already answers the scene's frame: the bounds centre and
  `1.25 · radius / tan(fov/2)`. *Frame all* and the automatic fit use it; the preset did not.

## Decisions

- `Leaf::apply_preset(preset, fit)` and `Leaf::look_from(direction, fit)` take the scene's fit
  camera. A standard view writes the direction, the projection, the target (`fit.target`) and, when
  the leaf is not framed, the distance (`fit.distance`); a leaf the person framed keeps its distance,
  so the size they chose survives and only the direction, the projection and the target move.
- The axonometric preset keeps ADR 0049's rule untouched — direction only — because `is_standard()`
  excludes it and Home must not throw away a pan or a zoom.
- A free direction through the corner widget still keeps the frame; only world-axis directions are
  standard views. A distance `Camera::new` refuses turns the view and keeps its frame rather than
  doing nothing.
- `Document::scene_fit` is the one place a caller asks for the frame: `home_camera` of the latest
  bounds, falling back to the active view's own camera when no camera can hold the scene, so a preset
  never moves a view to a point nobody can see.
- ADR 0049 carries the amendment the task asked for. ADR 0019's sentence about `Camera::look_from`
  still describes the camera primitive, which the leaf calls after it has written the target.

## Ruled out

- Threading the bounds into `Document` and doing the frame arithmetic there: the direction, the
  projection and the frame are one motion, and splitting them across two layers is how they
  disagreed in the first place.
- Centring the axonometric preset too: Home would then lose a pan or a zoom, which ADR 0049
  deliberately protects.
- Keeping the whole frame (target and distance) for a hand-framed view: the task carves out the
  size, and a Top view that stays panned to a corner is the defect being fixed.
