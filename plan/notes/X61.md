# X61 — a fine sphere loses its meridians, so its poles read bald

## What was measured

- `crates/mesh/src/lib.rs:152`, the crease margin: `COPLANAR_COS` was `0.999`, an angle of 2.56
  degrees. `mesh_edges` keeps a shared edge only when the two face normals differ by more than that.
- `crates/mesh/src/primitive.rs:95`, `sphere_mesh`: a banded surface, one polar fan of triangles at
  each end and quads in between. The fan triangles are almost horizontal slivers, so their normals
  are near the axis and adjacent fans differ by a very small angle.
- A 50 mm ball at 256 x 16 with the old margin: 15 x 256 = 3840 display edges, every one a latitude
  ring, and no meridian anywhere. At 32 x 16: 480 rings, 448 meridian strips, and zero pole-fan
  edges — the cap already drew as bare concentric rings at the default tessellation.
- With `0.999_999` (0.08 degrees) the same measurements are 256 x 16: 3840 ring edges, 14 x 256 =
  3584 strip edges, 2 x 256 = 512 pole-fan edges, 7936 in all; the whole graticule is present.
- The default 32 x 16 ball goes from 928 to 992 edges. The 480 rings and 448 strips it had are
  untouched; the 64 edges it gains are its two pole fans, which is the defect this task fixes, so
  the entry's "the default wireframe is unchanged" reads as "no form edge it showed is lost" — a
  literal size equality is impossible while the same margin draws every sphere's poles.
- `0.999_999_9` (0.008 degrees) starts drawing triangulation diagonals as hatching on a flat face,
  because the micrometre-rounded `f32` positions of a flat quad's triangles disagree by more than
  that when scaled up; `0.999_999` keeps them dropped.

## Decisions

- One margin, lowered rather than selecting it from the size of the mesh or from the normal's tilt:
  the tilt is a property of a legitimate smooth surface near a pole, which is exactly the case that
  must keep its edges.
- 0.08 degrees sits between the two measured bounds with two decades of room on the rounding side
  at a 50 mm ball and a factor of six on the hatching side.

## Ruled out

- Measuring the fold at the ideal geometry instead of the face normals: the triangle list is all
  `mesh_edges` receives, and the qualifier "coplanar enough" has to survive a mesher that emits
  near-equal normals rather than exactly equal ones.
- Excluding triangles that touch a pole from the test, or giving the pole fan its own rule: the same
  loss happens on any finely tessellated smooth body, so the fix belongs in the margin, not in a
  sphere-shaped special case.
- The line-vertex budget re-measurement: it lives in `plan/debt.md` under `[perf]` and X60 owns it.
