# X104 — a ring is one closed chain, and a union that leaves a circle stores it

## What was measured

- `crates/geom/src/boolean.rs` — `simplify_loop` merges consecutive pieces on one supporting line or
  circle, so a union that meets a circle of four quarter arcs hands back **one 360° arc**. The
  geometry layer has no minimum (`Contour::new_with_joint_tolerance` refuses only an empty list), so
  the ring travelled until the document refused it.
- `crates/model/src/contour.rs` — `Contour::new`, `Contour::assemble` and `Contour::from_polygon` all
  refused `count < 3`. The engine answered "ring 0 of a contour set is not a valid ring: a contour
  needs at least three segments, got 1", and the union of a circle with a disjoint shape could not be
  stored at all.
- Lowering the minimum to one segment (the temporary probe) broke **no test in the workspace**, so
  nothing in the tree depended on the count: the ring's real invariants are the joint rule, the
  non-zero signed area and the crossing test, and `Path` already accepted a one-segment closed chain.
- The same rule also refused a shape the drawing tools make: a half circle closed by its diameter is
  two segments, and `Contour::new` refused it.
- A full arc round-trips the file (`ContourWire` -> `Contour::assemble`), encloses `π r²` through
  `arc_area2`, samples as its circle in `mesh::path_points`, and `object.area.extrude` pulls it into a
  body (measured, not assumed — `param::profile` and truck take the one-edge wire).
- `Footprint::outline` welds a ring with `Contour::welded`, which returns `None` below three
  segments, so a one-arc frame ring is handed on unwelded, exactly as it came (ADR 0203).

## Decisions

- The document accepts a ring of one segment: closed, with an area, not self-intersecting is what a
  ring is, and the count is not part of it (ADR 0221). The refusals keep their own sentences —
  `Empty` for a ring of nothing, `TooFewCorners` for `from_polygon`, `TooFewSamples` for a profile
  that samples to two points — so no message tells a user about a count that is no longer the rule.
- `ContourEdit::remove_segment` keeps the three-segment minimum, as an edit rule: what is left of a
  smaller ring has no corner to join. It now carries `ContourEditError::TooFewSegments` rather than
  borrowing the ring's error, so the sentence names the editor.
- `Contour::from_polygon` keeps three corners: a polygon of two points is not a polygon.

## Ruled out

- `simplify_loop` stopping its merge at three segments: it would store a circle cut into arbitrary
  arcs, and the split point would be whatever the traversal happened to produce. The document would
  then hold a figure no tool draws and no edit can name.
- Raising the geometry minimum instead: `geom` has no minimum and its callers build rings of any
  count; the rule belongs to the document, which is where the count was.
