# Y3 — the render reads the catalogue: a decor with a size, a band's colour, a handle's metal

The tracer has the three lobes and the sampler (Y2) and every face is matte paint, because nothing maps
a catalogue record onto `scene::Surface`. This task is that mapping and everything it needs on the way
to an image: the decor's pixels at a real size, the grain's direction, the banding's own colour, a
handle's metal, and nothing of it in the viewport's colours.

## What was measured

- `crates/catalog/src/item.rs:75` — "Appearance, finish and the part document join it with their
  readers, so no field exists here that nothing reads". No `Appearance` type exists anywhere in the
  tree; `docs/domain/nomenclature.md` §7 specifies it as colour, an optional texture reference inside
  the package, roughness and metallic, and says the shell translates it into scene material data.
- `crates/catalog/src/view.rs` — the narrow views (`BoardView`, `BandingView`, `StockView`, `PriceView`)
  read typed properties by slug. `color` is a *text* naming the decor (`oak`), never a colour; a colour
  and a texture have no reader yet.
- `crates/scene/src/lib.rs:345` — `Surface` is roughness, metallic, ior, transmission and a
  Beer-Lambert absorption; its own doc names Y3 as the task that fills it. `Drawable::color` is the
  linear albedo, `[f32; 4]`.
- `crates/final/src/soup.rs:41` — `Face` is a world-space triangle, its albedo and its `Surface`;
  `gpu/layout.rs:44` packs `finish` and `tint` as two `vec4`s per face. No UV, no texture index, no
  texture table anywhere in `final`.
- `app/src/scene_build.rs:37` — a panel's colour is one of three placeholder tones by tree order and a
  band is one grey constant: `v1 has no per-panel material colour`. `Session::directory()` is in reach
  at build time (`crates/engine/src/session.rs:253`), and `Panel::material()` is an `Option<ItemId>`.
- `crates/model` has no hardware node: the drill objects a panel sees arrive with O13's instances, and
  "a handle" cannot be a document test today.
- `PROJECT.md:343` — "Grain. How is grain direction stored and how it constrains rotation, cutting and
  edge banding?" is an **open model question**, so Y3 must not answer the storage.
- Exposure and the filmic curve are already the command's: `final::Settings::exposure`,
  `image::tone_map`, the Render page's stops and `--exposure`, with `image.rs:226` proving stops are
  stops and `image.rs:209` proving the curve is monotonic. That clause of the entry is already green.
- `png` is already a dependency of `final` (write side only) and decodes as well as encodes, so a
  package texture needs no new crate.
- `final/src/device_tests.rs:27` — a render test takes an adapter when the machine has one and says so
  when it has not, so a decor test is a device test beside the pane and the furnace.

## Decisions

- **The record carries the size of its decor, and the render reads it.** "At its real texel scale" is
  not a number a renderer may invent: the image is a scan of a board sample and only the shop's record
  knows how many millimetres it covers. A texture without a size is not renderable and falls back to
  the record's flat colour.
- **Grain is the render's vocabulary, not the document's.** `PROJECT.md` has not answered the storage
  question, so `Surface` (or its decor) carries the turn as a quarter-turn count supplied by whoever
  builds the frame; the shell passes the record's answer, and the day the document stores a grain the
  shell changes and the tracer does not.
- **The viewport keeps its placeholders.** Y3 renders the final image; the viewport's three panel tones
  stay until the viewport reads the directory, which is a different task (its own texture upload,
  sampler and cache).
- **The tracer's decor is a table beside the frame, not a path in the shader.** `Face` gains the three
  corner UVs and an index into a decor table; the mapping is decided once, in `soup.rs`, from the
  drawable's own local frame — the panel's frame is where the grain is defined.

## What landed (the render half)

- `crates/scene/src/decor.rs`: `Decor` (texels, `covers`, `Turns`), `DecorImage` (eight-bit sRGB RGBA,
  square texels, a 4096-texel side cap), `DecorId`, and `Decor::uv` — the one projection both consumers
  call. On `Drawable` it multiplies `color`.
- `crates/render`: a face vertex is `position, normal, color, uv` and only the face pipelines read the
  extra attribute, so an edge list keeps its smaller vertex; one texture per `DecorId` in a swept
  table; a one-texel white picture for a drawable with no decor, so the fragment never branches.
- `crates/final`: `soup::Soup` (faces + the pictures they name, one per id), the coordinate at the three
  corners of every `Face`, the pictures as two storage buffers, and `albedo_at` performing the multiply
  with its own bilinear, repeat and sRGB→linear — the arithmetic the viewport's sampler performs for it.
- Measured on this machine's adapter: a board's left half is the picture's red half and its right the
  blue one, and a quarter turn crosses the stripes, in the viewport and in the tracer's image.
- **A defect the change exposed**: the ground's mask pass draws the same face buffers with a pipeline
  built for the old 36-byte stride, so a decor-bearing frame lost the contact shadow entirely until the
  cover pass moved to the face layout. Any later vertex-layout change must sweep every pass that draws
  `drawn.faces`, and the shadow test `a_see_through_floor_tints_the_part_below_it…` is what catches it.

## What is still open

- **Nothing authors a decor.** The material library the owner described — a portable file in the user's
  settings, a `textures/` folder beside it whose files a person can replace, and a catalogue record that
  references a material — is a subsystem of its own, filed as the next task. Until it lands the shell
  feeds frames no decor and every picture is the colour it always was.
- **A banding's own colour and a handle's metal** are the shell's mapping from a record onto
  `Drawable::color` and `Surface`, and they wait for the same library: `app/src/scene_build.rs` still
  draws a panel from one of three placeholder tones and a band from one grey constant.
- **The exposure clause was already green** before this task (`final::Settings::exposure`, the Render
  page, `--exposure`, `image.rs:209` and `:226`), so it cost nothing here.

## Ruled out

- **A shader-graph material.** A foreign vocabulary in the document and a bigger model than furniture
  needs (`AGENTS.md` §4.6, §4.9).
- **UVs in the mesh.** `MeshData` has positions, normals and indices; a decor is planar per face, so the
  UV is a projection of the local point, computed at flatten time, and no mesh changes.
- **A fixed tile size in the tracer** (a decor covering 600 mm by default): a made-up number that
  silently rescales every real decor a shop scans.
- **Sampling the decor in the viewport as well.** It needs a texture cache, a sampler and a new vertex
  attribute in `render`, and the entry names the final image.
