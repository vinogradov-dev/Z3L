# X84 — the cut was measured from the start of the drag

the cut was measured from the start of the drag

## The frame that mattered

Fourth session, one take, one drag. The piece is 60x950x100 at (-630,-490,0); the bar 1270x70x100 at
(-370,-510,0). Two homes only: the bar's near face (delta 200) and its far face (delta 1530).

```
t 27127  asked.x 1636  resolved.x 1636  delta.x 1636   (the jump past the bar, legitimate)
t 28035  asked.x 1529  resolved.x  200  delta.x 1533   <- 1 mm back into the bar, the answer is the
                                                          NEAR face: the piece is held 1333 mm away
t 28736  asked.x 1555  resolved.x 1555  delta.x 1555
```

`resolved.x 200` while the piece stands at 1533 is the "прыгает влево": the cut was resolved against
the piece's start box, so the near face won every time.

## Fixed here

- `limits_from(from, proposed)`: the axis walk starts at the place the piece was drawn last frame, and
  every cut — including the travel still to make — is measured from there. `limits(p)` is
  `limits_from(ZERO, p)`, so the axis handle and every earlier answer keep their meaning; the app's
  plane carry passes `drag.delta`.
- `blocking` treats a sliver thinner than `CONTACT_MM` at a face as a touch: measuring from the face a
  drag just stopped at must not read as a pair already sunk into one another (the retry pass was
  losing the contact that way, two older tests caught it).
- A retry pass accepts an axis only when it is strictly closer to the hand's request: a pass frees an
  axis after the axes beside it moved, never loses the face already reached.

## Test

`a_piece_resting_past_a_body_stays_there_and_is_not_pulled_back_across_it` — the log's own boxes: from
the start the near face (200), from 1530 a 1 mm drift stays at 1530, onward follows, and the near side
is reachable when the destination is there.
