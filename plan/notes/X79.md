# X79 — the log sink, and why the piece jerked

the log sink, and why the piece jerked

## What was measured

- `log` was a workspace dependency of `app` with no sink and no call, so every macro was a no-op.
- The jerk is ADR 0167's hold measuring the wrong thing: `(asked - previous) > 15 mm` plus
  `pointer_moved < 6 pt`. The second term is in **points**, the first in **millimetres**, and the
  camera-plane mapping between them is whatever the zoom is: at a zoomed-out view six points is a
  hundred millimetres, so every ordinary frame counted as a jump. The answer held, the hand kept
  asking, and after 0.12 s the piece moved — a stutter, not a carry.
- A carried frame's numbers are otherwise invisible: the preview is one placement, and whether the
  rule, a snap or the hold decided it is not in the document afterwards.

## Decisions

- One sink (`app/src/logging.rs`), installed first in `main`, `Z3L_LOG` for the level, the file beside
  the workspace settings, bounded at 4 MB, appended, and the same lines on stderr. 0168.
- The carry logs `node`, `piece` (pivot plus settled delta, world mm), `cursor` (points), `hand` (the
  world point the plane meets), `asked` (the hand's delta), `resolved` (the rule's), `delta` (after the
  hold), `jump`, and `contact`/`capture` per axis. The held drag logs its one axis; take, drop and
  cancel are `info`.
- The jump is `(asked - previous) > hand_step + 15 mm`, where `hand_step` is the hand's own world
  travel since the last frame: an answer that moves no further than the hand did is travel at any
  zoom. `Drag::asked` carries it.

## How to read a report

A line with `jump=true` is the hold acting; a line whose `resolved` differs from `asked` is the rule
cutting, and the `contact` slot names the part; a line whose `resolved` equals `asked` but `delta`
differs is the hold. A cursor that stays still while `asked` jumps is the carry plane being re-read,
not the hand.
