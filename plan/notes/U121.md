# U121 — the work plane is the face under the first click

## What was measured

- `DrawGizmo::update` froze `WorkPlane::from_camera(camera)` on the first pointer frame
  (`app/src/draw_gizmo.rs`), so the 3D snap mode of U119 had no plane a 3D sketch could be stored on:
  `from_camera` is world XY (z = 0) for every view but an axis-aligned orthographic one, and
  `work_plane_of_a_face` did not exist.
- The ruler already measured the surface under the pointer and stepped past a product's reserved box
  (`measure_gizmo::surface_under`, `SHELL_INSET_MM`, `MAX_SHELLS`). That function returned only the
  point of the face, which is enough to order a Face candidate and not enough to build a plane.
- `frame_tests::a_body_and_a_ball_answer_a_snap_on_every_corner_edge_face_and_centre` encoded the old
  rule — "in 3D a candidate above that plane is not a place the sketch can store at all" — and went
  red as soon as the sketch took the face plane. That red was the decision arriving, not a defect.
- A world span built from the plane's centre in the 3D view can run one end behind the eye: the first
  cut of `plane_axes` returned `None` for the app's fitted perspective camera (the +Y end stood
  1090 mm behind the eye), so no guide was painted at all. The span is now extended in screen space,
  where a straight world line is a straight screen line.

## Decisions

- **The plane is the pointer's until the first point is placed.** The draft with no point asks the
  probe for the face under the pointer in the 3D mode; the first placed point freezes that plane
  (`DrawGizmo::plane_for`). "The first click freezes it" in the entry is read as *the first placed
  point*, because freezing on the first frame would take whatever face the pointer happened to cross
  when it entered the viewport, which is not the face the person clicked.
- **The face is the ruler's reading, not a second probe.** `surface_under` moved from
  `measure_gizmo` to `gizmo_math` and now answers the whole `model::Probe`, so the sketch takes the
  face's normal and the ruler its point from one function (ADR 0118).
- **A face plane's `u` is the world axis least aligned with its normal**, `v = normal × u`. A face
  square to an axis therefore keeps round world coordinates, and the frame stays right-handed
  (`WorkPlane::from_face`). `u`/`v` do not follow the screen here: a face is a place, not a view.
- **The 2.5D mode keeps today's reading**: the plane is the camera's, a candidate above it answers
  with its foot, and no face plane is read. The mode is the switch (U119), so the two behaviours do
  not need a second setting.
- **Alt does not change the plane.** Alt drops the snaps; the plane is where the click is stored, not
  a candidate the hand may refuse.
- **The guide is the plane's own two axes**, crossed at the plane's point in the middle of the view,
  in `theme::helper` at 34 % and the construction line's dash. A filled patch would cover the model
  the sketch is drawn on; a mark at one point would not say the span. The spans are extended in
  screen space so one end cannot fall behind the eye.
- **A ball's tangent plane is a plane.** The probe answers the surface normal, so pointing at a ball
  in the 3D mode takes the plane that touches it there. Nothing of the ball lies in it, so the grid
  answers — which is the honest reading, and it is what the frame test now asserts.

## Ruled out

- Taking the measured face only when it is parallel to the camera plane: a sketch on a board's side
  is a sketch on a vertical plane, and refusing it would leave the 3D mode useful only from above.
- Freezing the plane on the first frame (today's code path): it names the face the pointer crossed
  last, which is not the face under the click.
- Clamping the plane guide to a fixed world size: a number that means nothing at another zoom.
- Handing the measured face to the resolver as `SnapRequest::face`: a sketch keeps every candidate
  it can contain (the resolver's own note says so), and the Face kind would capture the surface the
  plane already is.
