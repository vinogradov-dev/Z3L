# N25 — a piece in hand blocks nothing, and the view may move under it

## What was measured

- `viewport` in `app/src/document.rs` asked one question for two very different states:
  `translate_gizmo.is_dragging()` fed both `capturing` (does a gesture keep the pointer over the
  chrome) and the local `dragging` (does the camera move). A handle drag needs both; a carried piece
  needs neither, because it owns no button.
- `target.camera` is built *before* the gizmo's `update`, and the orbit/pan/zoom land *after* it, so
  the camera the hold measures against lags the drawn one by a frame. That is fine for the
  distance — the ray and the parameter are cast through the same camera — but it means a camera move
  is noticed on the frame after the gesture frame.
- The distance fields are placed beside the hand and are declared chrome (`Chrome::tool`), so with
  `capturing` off they cover the pointer and made the viewport non-interactive: the first version of
  this fix broke orbit and zoom through the fields alone.
- `Session::project().revision()` is the one value every command moves. Storing it with the hold
  answers "did the document change under the hand" for every command, without a list of entry points.
- `Camera` derives `PartialEq` and `Copy`, so a hold can remember the camera and the viewport
  rectangle it was measured in and compare them for free.

## Decisions

- `is_held()` beside `is_dragging()`: the camera gate reads `is_held` (a button under the hand) and
  `capturing` keeps reading `is_dragging`. One predicate per question, and the two no longer hide
  behind one name.
- The hold re-anchors: it keeps `delta`, and rewrites `start_parameter` as
  `parameter(pointer now) - delta·axis`, so the piece cannot move when the view does. Doing it every
  frame would be a no-op for the piece and therefore useless; it is done on a camera/rect change and
  on every frame of a camera gesture.
- A camera gesture also freezes the piece, not only the camera: the pointer travels to turn the view
  and none of that travel is the piece's. `middle_down || secondary_down || smooth_scroll_delta` is
  read in the gizmo — the same three inputs the viewport turns into a camera move.
- The revision stored with the hold cancels it on the next frame after any command; the targets, the
  rule and the capture were all read from the document the hold no longer has.
- The tools that take the pointer cancel the hold in one place, beside the guard that already skips
  the gizmo for them (contour, draft, box, pull, fill, ruler, niche, mesh edit, locked node).
- A held handle drag keeps freezing the camera. Its axis, parameter and capture are read through the
  camera, so an orbit mid-drag would move the piece under the hand — the caution ADR 0151 borrowed is
  right for that tool and only for it.

## Ruled out

- Putting the piece down on the *press* so a rubber band could start in the same gesture: a press is
  not a click, the drop would land under a chrome press that the shell treats as a field's, and the
  owner's gesture is a click. The primary button belongs to the hold until it is put down.
- Re-latching the axis after a camera turn: near a 45° view the choice flips and the distance
  restarts from the take point, so the piece would jump. The latched axis stays; a view that turns
  the axis into the eye direction simply stops moving it.
- Cancelling the hold from `undo`, `delete` and every other command: a list to keep in step, where one
  revision comparison covers all of them and the ones not written yet.
