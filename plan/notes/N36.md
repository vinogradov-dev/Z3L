# N36 — a part never runs through a post the pointer never pointed at

what a session measured about a shelf that pierced a wall, and why the ring is clipped by the bodies in it

## What was measured

«В одном случае полка прошила стенку» — the owner, on `/home/alx/skaf.z3l` again. The scene now holds a
post and five shelves:

- «Стойка» (id 12): x 1792..1817, y 1090..3704, z 2457..3885 — a wall of the frame, not of a part.
- «Горизонт 4» (id 13, z 3681): its stored ring runs `L(0,4423)→(39,76)`, the arc, `L(1792,522)→(1792,3704)`
  — clipped at the post's face, correct.
- «Горизонт 5» (id 14, z 3212): the frame's own ring, 2244 mm wide, so it **crosses the post** over the
  post's whole footprint.

The post existed before both shelves were planned (id 13 is clipped, id 14 is not), so the difference is
the pointer, not the document: every probe is cast *from the pointer*. The hand that made id 14 stood in
the top-left arm, past the post's far end at y = 3704, and looked along +x above it — no ray of its own
ever met the post. ADR 0204 made the shape the frame's whole ring, which turned that blind spot into a
part through a wall; the piece-only cell of 0203 could not reach the post either, but it never claimed to.

## Decisions

- **A body standing in the ring bounds the part.** After the probe rounds settle, every surface of the
  surroundings is read in the product's frame (`local_box`): a body that does not reach into the cell's
  own height is no wall of the plan; a body that stands in the ring (`ring_meets_rect`) and spans the cell
  from floor to ceiling is a post, a divider or a side wall, and the ring is clipped at its own face.
  The face follows the rule a hidden body already trims by (X73, ADR 0175): the side the pointer stands
  on wins, and a hand inside the body's own footprint takes the cut that keeps the most of the frame.
- **A body that stands inside without spanning the height is still a refusal**: the void is then no one
  ring, and a hole in a part is the work of a ring with a hole, not of this rule.
- **The rounds repeat until a round clips nothing**, so a post behind a post is bounded too; the loop is
  bounded by `MAX_CELL_ROUNDS`, and a ring that never settles is unrepresentable rather than answered
  with a wall dropped.
- The frame's own envelope is skipped: its faces are the bounds the ring was seeded from, and clipping by
  them answers a sliver of the frame instead of the frame (`SurfaceKind::Envelope`).
- `the_part_a_shaped_case_creates_stands_inside_its_footprint` gives each of its six parts a fresh case:
  a part left standing is material, and the next part is now measured against it — which is the point of
  this task, and made the old loop stack parts until the void had nothing left.

## Verified

- Headless: `a_part_never_runs_through_a_post_the_pointer_never_pointed_at` — a notched frame with a post
  in the arm beside the pointer's own: the niche is 450 mm wide and no point of the Shape plan's outline
  stands inside the post.
- On `/home/alx/skaf.z3l`, a scratch probe (deleted): the niche at three heights around the post answers
  1792 mm wide, the Shape plan runs x 0..1792 and crosses the post nowhere, and no outline point stands
  outside the frame. A sweep of 1771 plans over eight heights found no planned part overlapping any
  existing panel's box.
- One measure of the old blind spot, for the record: at the same heights the plan was 2244 mm wide and
  covered the post's footprint for its whole depth.

## Left open

A body that stands inside the cell without spanning its height is refused rather than cut around. A shape
with a hole — a shelf around a post with material on both sides — is the work of a ring with a hole and
the corners of a non-convex cell (ADR 0106 keeps the plane cell), and nothing in the owner's scene asks
for it.
