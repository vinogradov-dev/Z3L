# X132 — What was measured

## What was measured

- The 38-line recipe of `app/tests/recipes/wardrobe-800x460x2200.jsonl` held 14 `panel.create` lines
  and 24 other steps. Twelve of the fourteen are one of the three stances `PanelType` carries: the
  two shelves pairs and the five shelves `10.11` with an identity rotation, the two sides and the
  divider `10.10` with `rotation_y(-90°)`, the back sheet `10.12` with `[-0.5, -0.5, -0.5, 0.5]`.
  The two doors are `10.20`, which is no stance of the three.
- Running both recipes through `z3l run … --out` and diffing the documents showed the sizes, the
  classes and every world box identical; the only difference was one unit in the last place of the
  quaternion — `-0.7071067811865475` from the model's stance against the hand-written
  `-0.7071067811865476`, and the same for the wall's `±0.5`. The typed command computes sin/cos, the
  recipe wrote a rounded literal.

## Decisions

- `at` is the old `placement.translation`, unchanged: a panel is anchored at its base corner
  (ADR 0083), so the placement the recipe wrote already named the corner the typed command asks for.
- The class is now the type's, never the caller's: the recipe's codes were exactly the three the
  types answer, so dropping `class` from the line changes nothing in the document.
- The doors keep `panel.create` with their literal quaternion. `PanelType` has three words and a door
  is a fourth class, so typing it would mean a fourth stance this task has no decision for.
- The scenario asserts the fixture's form as well as its result — 12 typed creates and 2 raw ones —
  because the recipe is the document a model reads, and a silent revert to the hand-written turn is
  exactly what X132 was filed about. It failed on the old recipe, which is the regression check.

## Ruled out

- Converting the legs and the rail: they are `object.extrude.create` bodies, not panels, and have no
  typed form.
- Leaving `app/tests/recipes/README.md` alone: its paragraph said a carcass part stands up "by being
  placed with a quarter turn", which stops being what the recipe does, and its units note did not
  name `at`, `length` and `width`.
