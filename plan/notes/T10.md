# T10 — the group digits follow the order of the work, so the case is 10 and the seat is

the group digits follow the order of the work, so the case is 10 and the seat is 80

## What was measured

- The order a person met came from the group digits, because every surface walks `registry()` and
  `ClassCode::row` binary-searches that table. The families were numbered in the order they were
  written down: `10` seating, `20` lying, `30` storage, `40` surfaces, `70` boards and trim,
  `80` general.
- T9 had put a `PRESENTATION_ORDER` beside the digits so the picker sorted them. That leaves a stored
  project saying `10.01` for a chair, and every export, report and external reader still meets the
  seat first; the owner rejected it for exactly that reason.
- No build has shipped, so ADR 0094's "a code, once in a released build, is immortal" has not
  started: the group digits are still free to move.

## Decisions

- The groups are assigned in the order of the work, keeping every type and variant digit:
  `30` storage to `10`, `70` boards and trim to `20`, `40` surfaces to `30`, `20` lying to `40`,
  `50`/`60` unchanged, `80` general to `70`, `10` seating to `80`. So `10.01` is the cabinet again,
  as `docs/domain/furntypes.md` wrote it, the worktop is `20.01`, the chair is `80.01` and the
  placeholder `product.create` assigns is `70.01` (ADR 0145).
- The type and variant digits never moved, so a row keeps its place inside its family and the diff
  is a group digit everywhere. A 43-form map derived from the registry was applied by one pass, so
  the chained moves (`20`->`40` and `40`->`30`) could not collide; a generic pattern was rejected
  after it rewrote float literals like `10.0` in `geom` and `theme`.
- The registry table, the document section and the section numbers are all ascending by code again,
  and `presentation_rank`/`PRESENTATION_ORDER` are gone: the digits are the order, so no surface
  sorts. The doc test that compares the family paragraph now compares the sequence rather than the
  count.
- The placeholder stays out of the first slot on purpose: a product card pre-selects it silently, so
  the row a maker has to find is the real class; `70` puts the fallback after every family it could
  stand for.
- Group `10` names storage, not the product role of 0097. The role is still `level = Product`, a
  table is `30.01.01` while a cabinet is `10.01.01`, and 0097 carries a note that its "group 10"
  sentence is about the role, not about this number.
- The exporter's golden package was regenerated with `Z3L_UPDATE_GOLDEN=1`; no schema version moved,
  because the document stores the integer either way and nothing has shipped.
- `plan/questions.md` cited `30.15`, `30.16`, `30.3` and `30.10`; they were rewritten through
  `cargo xtask file`, never by hand.

## Ruled out

- Keeping the presentation sort and the digits: two orderings drift, and the stored number is the
  one every consumer reads.
- Renumbering only the families a person sees first and appending the rest after `80`: a group is two
  digits, so the set has to stay a permutation of `10..80`; `90` is reserved for a workshop.
- Rewriting the numbers inside ADRs 0035 and the context of 0094: those describe the previous
  registry, and a decision is superseded, not rewritten. Only the live example in 0097 was brought
  forward, with a note.
- A migration or a schema bump: no released build exists and no saved file carries a changed code.
